# Research reports

These reports support the quest guide and guide-assistant work. Sources were fetched 2026-10-25; see each report's
source table for URLs and limits.

- [`guide-research.md`](guide-research.md) — what makes player handbooks and guides popular and usable: UESP, OSRS,
  BG3 wiki, PoE wiki, Wowhead, Steam, Game8, Fandom migrations; 12 patterns, 9 anti-patterns, DFO recommendations.
- [`quest-research.md`](quest-research.md) — Daggerfall/DFO quest formats and player pain points: time limits, finding
  quest items, journal gaps, permanent breaks, reputation, gates, DFO online differences; proposed quest-page template
  and 20-question checklist.
- [`quest-data.md`](quest-data.md) — the pinned quest-pack extraction: 304 records, 283 player-facing quests, 14
  questlines/39 steps, field coverage, gaps and the no-dialogue policy.
- [`guide-assistant.md`](guide-assistant.md) — small/cheap guide-assistant options: candidate models, four
  architectures, costs/latency, citation/verification contract, evaluation plan and an MVP sketch.

Decision records: [`../36-guide-design.md`](../36-guide-design.md) and [`../37-guide-assistant.md`](../37-guide-assistant.md).
