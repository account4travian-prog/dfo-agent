# Questions a New Player Asks

*Short answers to the questions a newer DFO Online player usually has, for Tiger Fist (Khajiit, the custom
hand-to-hand class, no magic, level 7, about Renown 5). Each answer links to where the guide explains it in full.
Studied at DFO Online commit `4bba28c6c` (2026-10-20).*

## Getting started

### What should I do in my next session?

1. Stand in a **city with a General Store**, outdoors, by day, so questline letters can arrive and a stalled step
   (like The Court Crafter's) can begin. Gothway Garden, next to Privateer's Hold, has one.
2. **Join the Fighters Guild** (Gothway Garden has a hall) and take its quests: each gives you a private dungeon and a
   choice of gear.
3. Buy your **first 8 stars** in the Firmament (refunds are free until Renown 10).
4. **Bank your gold** before you go out: dying costs a tenth of what you carry.
5. Check the **event clock** and catch a falling star or a raid if one is near.

See [How to catch up](23-playing-online.md#how-to-catch-up-with-players-who-play-ten-hours-a-day) and
[Building Tiger Fist](23-playing-online.md#building-tiger-fist-every-system-at-a-glance).

### Do I need a registered account?

Yes. A guest account can play, but most of the new questlines (The Court Crafter among them) only begin for a
registered account, and the account service that runs professions, Festival Soul Cakes and Starfall claims refuses a
guest. [Other Players and Staying Out of Trouble](35-people-and-trouble.md) lists exactly what a guest misses.

### Why haven't my questline letters arrived?

Three rules hold them back: one new line starts per hour of your character's clock, a courier only hands a letter over
in a town outdoors by day, and a step that needs a town building (a General Store, tavern, armorer or temple) can't
start in the wilds or a hamlet without one. It retries about two real hours of play later. See
[Questlines](23-playing-online.md#quests-are-the-core-loop-now).

### What's the difference between my level and my Renown?

Your **level** is Daggerfall's: it rises as your skills rise and sets your health and your foes' level. **Renown** is
online's own level, 1 to 50: it adds +3 health and +2 magicka a level online and pays **2 Firmament stars a level**.
Events, quests and kills earn it. See [How power works online](23-playing-online.md#how-power-works-online-three-ladders).

## Getting stronger

### What do I spend level-up points on?

Speed first (it shortens your swings), then Strength, then Luck and Agility, Endurance last. You get 5 points a level
online. See [Building Your Character](29-build-systems.md).

### Which stars do I buy?

Start at **The Steed**. Your first 8: Path of the Sign, Path of the Sign, Gate of the Sign, Starway, Starway (the only
bridge to the fist stars), then three Endurance and Health stars. At Renown 10 you reach Iron Knuckles and Pankration
and the +30% damage cap; after that, the plan spends on defence. Skip Steed's Charge. See
[The Star Tree (Firmament)](28-firmament.md).

### How do I use my special moves?

You need a pair of **Gauntlets with a technique line** (Haymaker or Flying Kick). Press the **Weapon technique** key,
the mouse's back side button by default. See
[Your gauntlet techniques](23-playing-online.md#your-gauntlet-techniques).

### Which skills should I master at 100?

Hand-to-Hand first, then Critical Strike (or Dodging for safety), Running, Backstabbing, and later Blunt Weapon for
the Brawler path. Masteries are permanent. See [Building Your Character](29-build-systems.md).

### What gear should I look for?

The best armour in metals you may wear (not Steel, Elven or Dwarven), Gauntlets with a technique line, and lines that
raise Hand-to-Hand, Speed, Strength, Luck or Agility. Weapon-only lines (weapon damage, leech, elemental, slayer) do
nothing for fists. The dream piece is **Gauntlets of the Rose**. See [Building Your Character](29-build-systems.md).

### Are professions worth it?

As support, yes: Alchemy keeps a no-magic fighter alive, and Smithing keeps your armour repaired. You may take only two
crafts past rank 50, for good. See [The professions](23-playing-online.md#the-professions-in-detail).

## Catching up with hardcore players

### How can I keep up with people who play all day?

Take every **capped** reward every day (they reset at 00:00 UTC, so a 10-hour player gets no more than you), play the
scheduled events instead of grinding, and avoid permanent mistakes. See
[How to catch up](23-playing-online.md#how-to-catch-up-with-players-who-play-ten-hours-a-day) and
[Caps, Timers and Catch-Up](27-caps-and-timers.md).

### When are the big events?

Festival stars fall at :45, :52:30 and :00 each real hour until 7 November; a Dagon's Breach opens every two real
hours; raids roll every game day; the sea serpent rises every four real hours. One star or raid is worth 15–30 kills of
Renown. See [The event clock](23-playing-online.md#the-event-clock-plan-a-session-around-it).

### Should I party with strangers?

Yes. Kills aren't split: everyone earns the whole kill plus 10% per extra member, and mentor mode scales stronger
players down to your level automatically. See
[Group up with the strong players](23-playing-online.md#group-up-with-the-strong-players).

## Dungeons, the world and travel

### Why are dungeons full of people, or already empty?

A public dungeon is one shared room: what others kill stays dead for 20 minutes. Take quests for private instances, or
leave the roads to find spawned dungeons nobody has touched. See
[Dungeons without the crowds](23-playing-online.md#dungeons-without-the-crowds).

### Where is it safe to go?

Danger comes from the type of place, not the distance from town: towns, mines and caves are gentlest; the wilds are
harder at night; Crypts and up hit much harder. Stay out of the Wrothgarian Mountains (the Open Zone) for now. See
[Where it's safe](23-playing-online.md#where-its-safe-danger-by-place) and
[The World, Region by Region](25-world-regions.md).

### How do I find a town with a carriage, port, guild or General Store?

Use the map's **Find a service** filter and its **Nearest to** helper. Only cities have carriages: the nearest to
Gothway Garden is Chesterwark, about 8 km south-west.

### How do I travel fast without magic?

The **hired carriage** outside a walled city's gate (about 1 gold a travel day), **Portal Stones** (5 Welkynd Shards
each), ship passage between ports, and party travel to your leader. See [Getting Around](30-travel.md).

## Your companion

### What is my companion, and what do I do with it?

A revenant you beat and chose to **Spare**. Keep it beside you, read its Art, aim for 90 loyalty, and spend its stars on
health, then damage, then its Art. Missions are a simple timed reward for a second companion. See
[Companions](31-companions.md).

## Fighting

### How do I fight well with my fists?

Watch for the enemy's **glint and the shape marked on the ground**, step out of it, and punish the foe while it's
**Open** after a missed attack: an overreached foe takes 30% more damage, and a **perfect dodge** (inside the mark until
the last quarter second, then out) keeps it open longer. There is no block button for you; moving is your defence. See
[Fighting Well](33-fighting.md).

### Does the direction of my punch matter?

Yes. The default "Click or Hold" mode picks a random direction each click. A **down** swing does +4 damage at −10 to
hit; **up** does −4 damage at +10 to hit; left and right are even. Switch to **Gesture** in Controls if you want to
choose by dragging. See [Fighting Well](33-fighting.md).

### What kills new fist fighters?

Archers and casters you can't reach, **poison** (your low Willpower resists it poorly), and running out of fatigue:
exhaustion near enemies costs 10% of your health each time. Close on ranged foes through cover, carry Cure Poison, and
keep a Restore Fatigue potion on the hotbar. See [Fighting Well](33-fighting.md).

### What do I do when a fight turns bad?

Drink from the hotbar early, then sprint away and break line of sight. You can't rest beside enemies. If you die, you
rise at a safe place and lose a tenth of the gold you carry, so bank it first. See [Fighting Well](33-fighting.md).

### Do I fight differently in a group?

Enemies pick their own targets and there's no taunt, so call a target and focus it together. Your swing can hit more
than one foe in reach, so pull enemies away from allies before swinging into a crowd. See
[Fighting Well](33-fighting.md).

## Money and loot

### How do I make money early?

Sell dungeon loot at shops (Mercantile matters most, Personality a little), take bounties (about 30–50 gold plus a gear
piece at your level), finish quests, and turn daily Marks into gold at the Empire's bank (up to 300 Marks a day at 8 gold
each). See [Money and Loot](34-money-and-loot.md).

### What do I do with loot?

Keep what you can use or list; sell common spares; **salvage** unwanted Magic-or-better pieces for **Welkynd Shards**
(1 from Magic, 3 from Rare, 8 from Legendary), which buy Portal Stones (5 shards) and reforges. Don't pay a smith to
repair something before selling it: it adds no resale value. See [Money and Loot](34-money-and-loot.md).

### Where should I keep my money?

In the Empire's bank: online it's one account you can use at any bank counter, and dying never touches it. A letter of
credit carries gold without weight (1% fee, at least 100 gold). Online loans are small: 1,000 gold per level. See
[Money and Loot](34-money-and-loot.md).

### What are Marks, shards and the other currencies for?

**Marks** (the game also says "silver") are the account's currency for the market, guild seats and star refunds;
**Welkynd Shards** come from salvage and pay for Portal Stones and reforging; **Soul Cakes** are the festival's;
**Weir Keys** open Elite Gates. See [Money and Loot](34-money-and-loot.md).

### Should I buy a home?

Later. Online homes cost 5,000 to 250,000 gold depending on size and town, and give you a private cupboard, a bed and a
door you can open to your party or guild. See [Money and Loot](34-money-and-loot.md).

## Other players and trouble

### Can other players attack me?

Not in ordinary play. PvP happens only in duels you accept (refereed on a separate bar, your real health is untouched),
Arena bouts, guild sieges, and the Open Zone in the Wrothgarian Mountains. Loose loot in shared dungeons is first come,
first served. See [Other Players and Staying Out of Trouble](35-people-and-trouble.md).

### How do I find a group or a guild?

Use chat: `/lfg` to look for a group, `/world`, `/region` and `/trade` channels, and invite people from their Inspect
card or the F menu (parties hold eight). Guilds need a registered account. See
[Other Players and Staying Out of Trouble](35-people-and-trouble.md).

### How do I stay out of trouble with the guards?

Don't take from shop shelves or strangers' cupboards, don't trespass or assault anyone. If the watch stops you, pay the
fine or face the court: running or dying only ends the chase, it doesn't clear the crime. Bounty contracts on a region's
board improve your standing there. See [Other Players and Staying Out of Trouble](35-people-and-trouble.md).

### How do I rise in the Fighters Guild?

Promotion checks reputation and skills: one guild skill must reach the next rank's high bar and two its low bar, and
Hand-to-Hand counts. Online there's no waiting period once you qualify. See
[Other Players and Staying Out of Trouble](35-people-and-trouble.md).

### Should I become a werewolf or a vampire?

Your disease immunity doesn't stop either curse. Lycanthropy's beast form gets +30 to skills including Hand-to-Hand,
but only in beast form, with a monthly kill requirement. Vampirism's spells don't suit a no-magic build. Neither is a
free bonus. See [Other Players and Staying Out of Trouble](35-people-and-trouble.md).

### What is city control, and should I care?

Player guilds compete for town seats each week (Sunday to Sunday, 18:00 UTC). A seat gives its guild shop discounts, a
tithe and a hall. You can help a guild by walking its pledged town, fighting gates and delivering your own materials.
See [City Control and Guilds](26-city-control.md).
