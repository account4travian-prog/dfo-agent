# R2: Player guilds and city control
Commit: 0df2d87eb | Researcher: Luna | Date: 2026-10-21

## Player-facing summary
- Player guilds are account-server groups with four ranks; the Guildmaster and Officers have different powers. (`src/net/guild/guildLaw.js:36-74` | port.)
- Guilds pledge towns, earn weekly influence and may claim a seat Charter at the weekly Turning if they clear the influence threshold and can pay the fee. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:230-288,404-408,453-476` | port.)
- A Charter brings weekly upkeep, town rules and services; it is not the same system as an NPC-guild chapter. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:478-536`; `src/net/guild/guildLaw.js:36-74` | port.)
- Members can contribute through town Watch activity, gates, a home, Renown and material writs; individual accounts have weekly contribution caps. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:257-288,311-330` | port.)
- A Right of Siege is a scheduled battle for a held seat; fortifications change defenses and provide services. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:429-546`; `src/net/fortLaw.js:28-48` | port.)

## Rules and numbers

### Guilds, ranks and founding
- Guild ranks are Guildmaster, Officer, Member and Recruit, with rank indices 0-3; guild powers are granted in `GUILD_POWERS` / `guildMay`. (`src/net/guild/guildLaw.js:36-74` | port.)
- Guild founding requires 10,000 gold and Renown 10, and the roster cap is 50 characters. (`src/net/guild/guildLaw.js:1-36` | port.)
- Guild rename costs 25,000 gold and has a 14-day cooldown. (`src/net/guild/guildLaw.js:112-122` | port.)
- Vault capacity and permission behavior are specified in `guildVaultLaw.js`; its capacity is 50, raised to 100 with a hall. (`src/net/guild/guildVaultLaw.js:1-79` | port.)
- Guild rules and permissions belong to the player-guild service; NPC-guild chapters use a separate chapter system and must not be conflated with seat Charters. (`src/net/guild/guildLaw.js:36-74`; `src/net/npcChapterLaw.js:1-80` | port.)

### Pledges, influence and weekly Turning
- A seat week is seven days; it contains the Muster and a final 48-hour Reckoning before the Turning. The UI says the Turning is Sunday 18:00 UTC. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:227-238,355-358`; `src/ui/seatTab.js:74-89` | port.)
- Guild officers and the Guildmaster may pledge; a guild may pledge in up to five regions. Pledges lock at Friday 18:00 UTC. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:248-252,338-356`; `src/ui/seatTab.js:81-89` | port.)
- A guild member must have belonged to the guild for seven days before their influence counts; one account contributes at most 2,000 influence per seat per week. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:284-286` | port.)
- Watch grants 1 influence per qualifying tick up to 60 per day; the gate grants 300 each, with 900 per week and three region-agreed receipts. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:257-267,311-330` | port.)
- Home influence is 25 per day and counts from at most five homes; Renown contributes at 20 XP per influence up to 400 influence / 8,000 XP per week. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:269-276,311-330` | port.)
- Writs grant 1 influence per eligible Mark of delivered material value; Tribute converts at 10 Marks per influence and is capped at a share of 20% of the target. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:279-288,296-330` | port.)
- At Turning, a Palace claim threshold is 6,000 influence and fee is 8,000; a Crown threshold is 30,000 and fee is 80,000. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:404-408,453-476` | port.)
- Guilds within 10% of one another can enter a Tourney for an unheld Charter; `turningPlan` selects eligible claimants and checks the treasury fee. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:408,453-476` | port.)
- Ten percent of influence carries into the next week as Legacy, but does not carry past Season end. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:287-288,416-424` | port.)
- A Seat Season lasts eight weeks; the system resets Legacy and moves Standing toward 50 at season transition. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:230-238,409-424`; `src/net/townSeatLaw.js:536-546` | port.)

### Charters, upkeep, edicts and seats
- The Turning law resolves claims, upkeep, siege rights, Edicts, standings and revolts in a single weekly plan; use that as authority over a UI summary or bible description. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:429-546` | port.)
- Held seats owe upkeep based on tier and overreach; the Turning can record neglect or unpaid state, and a two-week nonpayment can lapse the Charter. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:478-493`; `src/net/townSeatLaw.js:579-582` | port.)
- Edicts are paid after upkeep and can fail when their required cost is not paid. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:523-536` | port.)
- Seat Tithe, powers, claim, standing, defense and edict costs are data-driven in `townSeatLaw.js`; do not copy the former guide's prices without mapping each rule to current constants and account-service enforcement. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:387-424,453-546` | port.)

### Sieges and fortifications
- A siege is scheduled inside the Wednesday-Saturday window, 16:00-02:00 UTC; the holder's battle window, roster cutoffs, team sizes and battle lengths are controlled by siege law and account enforcement. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:429-453`; `src/net/siege/siegeRef.js:1-80`; `server-account/src/seatBattles.js:1-100` | port.)
- A Right of Siege and its award are resolved at the Turning after the challenger meets the defense and claim threshold. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:496-522` | port.)
- Available fort works include Walls, Gatehouse, Watchtowers, Barracks, Market Hall, Shrine, Forge, Workshop, Apothecary and Harbor; tiers and material/Mark costs are in `FORT_WORKS`. (`src/net/fortLaw.js:28-48` | port.)
- A Gatehouse is allowed at Crowns or at a Palace with tier-3 Walls; Harbor is coastal-only. (`src/net/fortLaw.js:28-48` | port.)
- Walls, gate vitality, defender respawn waves, Rams, watchtower warnings and other battle effects are calculated by fort and siege systems, not by a guild member's local preference. (`src/net/fortLaw.js:1-120`; `src/net/siege/siegeRef.js:1-100` | port.)

## Gates
- Founding a guild requires Renown 10 and 10,000 gold; joining an existing guild is invite-based. (`src/net/guild/guildLaw.js:1-36`; `server-account/src/guilds.js:1-100` | port.)
- Influence from a member begins after seven days in the guild. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:284-286` | port.)
- A Charter requires the tier-specific claim threshold and fee at Turning; a Right of Siege requires the eligible challenger and defense calculation. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:404-408,453-522` | port.)
- Chapter membership refers to separate NPC guilds and does not itself grant player-seat power. (`src/net/npcChapterLaw.js:1-80` | port.)

## Where it shows in the interface
- Town Notice Board - Seat tab shows phase, pledges, standings, influence, Charter and seat actions; it is also available from the Guild page. (`src/ui/seatTab.js:62-89`; `src/net/townSeatBook.js:1-80` | port.)
- The phase labels are Muster, Reckoning and Turning; the law supplies the Sunday 18:00 UTC Turning label. (`src/ui/seatTab.js:74-89`; `src/net/townSeatLaw.js:227-238` | port.)
- The seat Chronicle and Hall of Records read history; the law caps the Hall of Records display at 400 rows. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:552-559,663-671`; `src/ui/hallOfRecords.js:1-80` | port.)
- Use the seat tab and map-card view code to confirm exact map labels and current actions; never put live holders or standings into the handbook. (`src/ui/seatTab.js:62-89`; `src/net/townSeatLaw.js:175-207` | port.)

## Online vs offline
- Player guilds, Seats and their account-server authority are online systems; offline DFU guilds are the unrelated NPC-guild system. (`src/net/guild/guildLaw.js:1-74`; `server-account/src/guilds.js:1-100` | port.)
- The town-control schedule and influence are based on UTC/server time and account-service results, rather than character-specific local game-clock advice. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:227-238,311-330,453-546`; `server-account/src/seatInfluence.js:1-100` | port.)
