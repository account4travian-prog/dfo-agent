## Player-facing summary
- There are 18 stock classes; online, their primary, major and minor skills use the revised lists in `stockClasses.js`. (`src/systems/chargen.js:41-46` `CLASS_CAREERS`; `src/systems/stockClasses.js:36-75` `STOCK_CLASS_REVISIONS`)
- The custom class sets 3 primary skills, 3 major skills and 6 minor skills; a skill cannot be assigned to more than one tier. (`src/systems/customClass.js:84-95` `availableSkills`; `src/systems/customClass.js:107-127` `buildCustomCareer`)
- Each tier has its own 6-point chargen skill pool; skill starting values use tier baselines of 28, 18 and 13, plus a 0–3 roll. (`src/systems/chargen.js:78-95` `rollSkills`)
- The custom class has 4–30 hit points per level, with 8 as the default. (`src/systems/customClass.js:12-17` `HP_MIN`, `HP_MAX`, `HP_DEFAULT`)
- Its difficulty score sets the class advancement multiplier; higher difficulty slows skill advancement. (`src/systems/customClass.js:25-36` `difficultyPoints`, `advancementMultiplier`)
- You can add up to 7 total special advantages and disadvantages, subject to compatibility rules. (`src/systems/specialAdvantages.js:68-75` `ONLY_ONE_KEYS`, `MAX_ITEMS`; `src/systems/specialAdvantages.js:157-184` `cannotAdd`)
- A custom class starts at 0.5× Intelligence in spell points; a primary or major magic skill instead gives it the Spellsword starting spell set. (`src/systems/customClass.js:97-127` `buildCustomCareer`; `src/systems/customClass.js:149-155` `customSpellSetIndex`)

### Custom class builder
- The custom builder defaults to 8 HP per level; allowed range is 4–30. (`src/systems/customClass.js:12-17` `HP_MIN`, `HP_MAX`, `HP_DEFAULT`)
- Difficulty points are `hp - 8` for HP ≥ 8, and `-2 × (8 - hp)` below 8, plus advantage and disadvantage adjustments. (`src/systems/customClass.js:25-30` `difficultyPoints`)
- The advancement multiplier is `0.3 + 2.7 × (points + 12) / 52`; endpoints correspond to -12 and 40 difficulty points. (`src/systems/customClass.js:16-17,33-36` `DIFFICULTY_MIN`, `DIFFICULTY_MAX`, `advancementMultiplier`)
- The difficulty dagger visual maps the default score 0 to y=115, the hardest end to y=46, and the easiest end to y=186; it is a nonlinear visual scale. (`src/systems/customClass.js:39-54` `daggerY`, `daggerFraction`)
- Skill groups contain 3 primary, 3 major and 6 minor picks, with duplicates excluded from remaining choices. (`src/systems/customClass.js:84-95,107-118` `availableSkills`, `buildCustomCareer`)
- Each skill starts at 3–6 by default; primary skills start at 28–31, major at 18–21, minor at 13–16, and each group gets 6 distributable points. (`src/systems/chargen.js:78-95` `rollSkills`)
- Each custom-class attribute defaults to 50 in the builder and can be edited within 10–75. (`src/systems/customClass.js:19-23` `FREE_EDIT_MIN`, `FREE_EDIT_MAX`, `STAT_DEFAULT`)
- A new custom class has the 0.5× Intelligence spell-point multiplier by default. (`src/systems/customClass.js:97-127` `buildCustomCareer`; `src/systems/specialAdvantages.js:229-231` `DEFAULT_MAGERY_BITS`)
- The spell-point choices are 0.5×, 1×, 1.5×, 1.75×, 2× and 3× Intelligence; spell points are the floor of Intelligence times the selected multiplier. (`src/systems/chargen.js:180-186` `SPELL_POINT_MULTIPLIERS`)
- A custom class with any magic skill in primary or major starts with the Spellsword spell set; otherwise it gets no standard class spell set. (`src/systems/customClass.js:149-155` `customSpellSetIndex`)
- The custom builder's skills do not alter starting spells unless a magic skill is primary or major. (`src/systems/customClass.js:149-155` `customSpellSetIndex`)
- Difficulty costs for advantages/disadvantages are signed adjustments to class difficulty; positive costs increase advancement multiplier and negative costs lower it. (`src/systems/specialAdvantages.js:112-155` `DIFFICULTY`, `advDisAdjustment`, `totalAdjust`; `src/systems/customClass.js:25-36` `difficultyPoints`, `advancementMultiplier`)
- The builder accepts at most 7 entries across the advantages and disadvantages. (`src/systems/specialAdvantages.js:68-75` `ONLY_ONE_KEYS`, `MAX_ITEMS`)
- Increased Magery, Darkness-Powered Magery and Light-Powered Magery are limited to one pick each within their respective picker list. (`src/systems/specialAdvantages.js:68-72` `ONLY_ONE_KEYS`)
- An identical primary/secondary pick is rejected across both lists; Bonus to hit conflicts with Phobia for the same enemy, Expertise in conflicts with Forbidden Weaponry for the same weapon, and tolerance picks conflict for the same effect. (`src/systems/specialAdvantages.js:157-184` `EXCLUSIVE_PAIRS`, `cannotAdd`)

## Online vs offline
- Online chargen revises stock class primary/major/minor skill lists at load time while leaving career attributes, HP per level, advancement multiplier, magery, advantages/disadvantages and other equipment restrictions intact. (`src/systems/stockClasses.js:1-25,81-94` `reviseStockCareer` comments and function)
- The revised lists are applied where chargen careers are loaded. (`src/systems/chargenSession.js:106-114` `loadCareers`)
- The standard list, skills and builder rules in this file describe the online client implementation. Runtime CFG values supply standard class attribute and special-field values. (`src/systems/chargenSession.js:106-114` `loadCareers`; `src/formats/classFile.js:27-54,69-105` `ClassFile.load`)
- Custom class advancement costs include the selected advantages and disadvantages; this was wired into the live builder, not left as inert difficulty adjustments. (`src/systems/customClass.js:25-36,101-127` `difficultyPoints`, `advancementMultiplier`, `buildCustomCareer`)
