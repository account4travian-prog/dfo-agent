# P1: Levelling, masteries and specialisations
Commit: 0df2d87eb · Researcher: Atlas swarm (P1) · Date: 2026-10-21

## Player-facing summary
- Online, every level hands you exactly **5 attribute points** — never the offline random 4–6 — and your maximum health can never sit below what your class would have at your level with average rolls and your current Endurance, so a run of low rolls or late Endurance no longer gimps a character for good. `src/systems/levelEven.js:28` (`ONLINE_BONUS_POOL`); `src/systems/levelEven.js:35` (`evenHpFloor`)
- **Master Skills is always on online** — there is no switch. Every skill can reach 100, but only the skills you *master* may climb past it, up to a hard cap of 200. `src/systems/masterSkills.js:108` (`masterSkillsActive`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:45` (`SKILL_HARD_CAP`)
- You may master **2 primary, 2 major and 1 minor** skill — five in all. Miscellaneous skills can never pass 100. A mastery is **permanent**: it can never be undone, swapped or refunded. `src/systems/masterSkills.js:113` (`MASTERY_SLOTS`); `src/systems/masterSkills.js:162` (`masterSkill`)
- Past 100 each point costs far more uses (×4, then ×8, then ×16), is worth only a quarter of a point in every formula, and combat/magic skills only learn there from foes tough for that skill — weak foes and repetition teach nothing. `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:51` (`SOFTCAP_TIERS`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:47` (`OVERCAP_POINT_WEIGHT`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:136` (`challengeQuality`)
- Mastering **two of the six magic schools** opens one of **15 arcane specialisations**; mastering **two of the six weapon skills** opens one of **15 martial specialisations**. You may hold one of each family at once. Each gives a passive and a signature (a spell you can only get this way, or a technique on the Weapon Technique key). `src/systems/arcanePaths.js:239` (`PATH_ROWS`); `src/systems/martialPaths.js:309` (`PATH_ROWS`)
- A chosen path is for good — the one way back is a **paid reset: 200,000 gold per family**, paid from coins, letters of credit and (online) the Empire's bank account. Your masteries survive a reset; only the family's path is cleared, and you may then choose again among the paths your masteries open. `src/systems/arcanePaths.js:59` (`SPEC_RESET_GOLD`); `src/systems/specReset.js:67` (`resetSpec`)
- For **100,000 gold** you can **swap one class skill** you have not mastered for a miscellaneous skill, in the same career group — the way a class built around one weapon can still open a martial path. Skill values and your level do not move. `src/systems/skillSwap.js:42` (`SKILL_SWAP_GOLD`); `src/systems/skillSwap.js:100` (`swapSkill`)
- The climb has a price in the world: as your best combat/magic skills pass 100, dungeon and wilderness foes grow stronger in step (more skill, up to ×3.4 health and ×1.6 damage at full share in the deepest dungeon kinds). `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:304` (`ENEMY_SCALING`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:345` (`combatEdge`)

## Rules and numbers

### Layer: what decides these rules online
- Layer: **port** — Master Skills, the softcap past 100, both specialisation families, the paid reset and the skill swap are the port's own systems (`src/systems/masterSkills.js`, `skillSoftcap.js`, `arcanePaths.js`, `martialPaths.js`, `specReset.js`, `skillSwap.js`); no vendored mod names any of them, and the online lane forces none of their switches — online they are live because `masterSkillsActive` answers true for every online character. `src/systems/masterSkills.js:108` (`masterSkillsActive`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:308` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS`)
- Layer: **port, online departure** — the fixed 5-point pool and the health floor are LEVEL-EVEN, an online-only layer that is never saved and never applies offline. `src/systems/levelEven.js:28` (`ONLINE_BONUS_POOL`); `src/systems/levelEven.js:35` (`evenHpFloor`)
- Layer: **mod (Oblivion Remaster Leveling), character's own online** — whether a character uses the mod's "virtue" leveling is the character's own (`Enabled` is the one key left to the player in the whole-mods table), but every dial of it is held at the room's shipped value online; and a character *born* online is never offered it (see Levelling). `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:469` (`'oblivion-remaster-leveling'`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:511` (`'oblivion-remaster-leveling'`)

### Levelling — the law the masteries hang on
- A skill raise needs `floor(skillValue × skillMult × careerAdvMult × 1.04^level × 2/5) + 1` adjusted uses; tallies are checked every 360 classic minutes. Below 100 this is Daggerfall Unity's law untouched. `src/systems/advancement.js:68` (`skillUsesForAdvancement`); `src/systems/advancement.js:64` (`SKILL_RAISE_CHECK_INTERVAL`)
- Your character level stands on the class skills alone: level-up sum = sum of primary skills + sum of major skills − the lowest major + the highest minor, and a level is earned per 15 points of that sum: `floor((currentSum − startingSum + 28) / 15)`. `src/systems/advancement.js:78` (`calculatePlayerLevel`); `src/systems/advancement.js:76` (`LEVELUP_SKILL_SUM_PER_LEVEL`)
- Daggerfall's lock — once any primary skill is mastered (at 100), no other skill may rise past 95 — is **lifted while Master Skills is in force**, so online every skill can reach 100; the gate still applies where Master Skills is off (offline, unswitched). `src/systems/advancement.js:186` (`raiseSkills` cap gate); `src/systems/advancement.js:91` (`alreadyMasteredASkill`)
- The level-up attribute pool online is **5 every level** (LEVEL-EVEN), drawn from no random stream; offline it is a 4–6 draw. `src/systems/advancement.js:349` (`bonusPoolFor`); `src/systems/levelEven.js:28` (`ONLINE_BONUS_POOL`); `src/systems/advancement.js:65` (`LEVELUP_BONUS_POOL_MIN`)
- Online health floor: your stored maximum health is never below `25 + h + (level − 1) × max(1, (trunc(h/2) + h)/2 + HitPointsModifier(Endurance))`, where `h` is your class's hit points per level and Endurance is your permanent Endurance (with Firmament stars in it, not a spell's or an item's). Rolls above the floor are kept. `src/systems/levelEven.js:35` (`evenHpFloor`); `src/systems/levelEven.js:31` (`averageLevelHp`)
- A character **born online always levels the classic Daggerfall way**: Oblivion Remaster Leveling is not offered at online creation or classic-save import, whatever was asked for (`ONLINE_LEVELING_SYSTEM = 'classic'`). An existing character's own system is read from its save and is not rewritten by a load. `src/systems/oblivionLeveling.js:127` (`ONLINE_LEVELING_SYSTEM`); `src/systems/oblivionLeveling.js:128` (`newCharacterLevelingSystem`); `src/systems/oblivionLeveling.js:136` (`usesVirtueLeveling`)
- The virtue system itself (a character created offline with it): skill raises feed a point bar of 100 (`LEVELUP_TOTAL`); filling it levels you, and the level-up hands out a purse of attribute points (`attributePoints`, a room-held dial online) instead of the classic pool. `src/systems/oblivionLeveling.js:72` (`LEVELUP_TOTAL`); `src/systems/oblivionLeveling.js:107` (`levelingSettings`)

### Master Skills and masteries
- Master Skills is **always in force online**; offline it is the player's own switch, off by default, and it cannot be changed inside a dungeon. While off, a skill above 100 reads as 100 and nothing is lost — the stored value returns when it is on again (or when the character plays online). `src/systems/masterSkills.js:108` (`masterSkillsActive`); `src/systems/masterSkills.js:203` (`masterCappedSkill`); `src/systems/masterSkills.js:66` (`MASTER_SKILLS_ABOUT`)
- Mastery slots by career group: **primary 2, major 2, minor 1** — five masteries in all; a miscellaneous skill has no group and can never be mastered. `src/systems/masterSkills.js:113` (`MASTERY_SLOTS`); `src/systems/masterSkills.js:116` (`careerGroupOf`)
- A skill can be mastered only once it has reached 100, only while its group still has a slot, and the choice is **permanent** — there is no un-master, and the paid reset (below) does not free a slot. `src/systems/masterSkills.js:151` (`masteryRefusal`); `src/systems/masterSkills.js:162` (`masterSkill`)
- Only a **mastered** skill may climb past 100, and only while Master Skills is in force; an unmastered skill stops at 100, classic Daggerfall's ceiling. `src/systems/masterSkills.js:148` (`skillCanPassCap`)
- The choice is offered the first quiet pass after a skill reaches 100 while its group has a slot (asked once per skill), and at any time afterwards from the skill screen / Master Skills page; the box says how many that group allows and how many would remain, and that the decision cannot be undone. `src/systems/masterSkills.js:178` (`nextMasteryChoice`); `src/systems/masterSkills.js:184` (`masteryChoiceRows`)
- The first time a **primary** skill reaches 100 online, a one-time box explains Master Skills (OK only — it is already in force). Offline the same moment asks Yes/No instead. `src/systems/masterSkills.js:236` (`masterSkillsBoxDue`)

### Skills past 100 — the softcap
- Hard cap: **200**; the soft cap is 100. Below 100 nothing changes. `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:45` (`SKILL_HARD_CAP`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:44` (`SKILL_SOFT_CAP`)
- Cost ladder, by the value the skill is leaving: 100–125 a point costs **×4** the uses, 125–150 **×8**, 150–200 **×16**. `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:51` (`SOFTCAP_TIERS`)
- A point past 100 is worth **a quarter** of a point below it in every formula (`effectiveSkill`), plus milestone bonuses granted once each: **+2 at 125 ("Expert"), +3 at 150 ("Master"), +4 at 175 ("Grandmaster"), +6 at 200 ("Legend")** — a 200 therefore reads as effective 140, never 200. Screens print the real number; formulas read the effective one. `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:47` (`OVERCAP_POINT_WEIGHT`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:59` (`SKILL_MILESTONES`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:88` (`effectiveSkill`)
- Real-use law past 100: a use is weighted by the foe's tier against the skill — foe tier = 5 × level + 30; at ratio r = tier/skill, r < 0.75 teaches **nothing**, 0.75–1 ramps in quadratically, r ≥ 1 counts in full up to 1.5×. So at skill 100 a level-9 foe starts to teach and a level-14 foe teaches in full; at 150, levels 17/24; at 200, levels 24/34. The foe must have traded a blow within 6 seconds to count. `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:136` (`challengeQuality`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:130` (`challengeTier`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:121` (`CHALLENGE_TTL_MS`)
- A spell cast at no foe (a self-heal, a light) counts at a floor weight of **0.25** past 100; a tough foe raises it, a weak one never lowers it below that. `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:124` (`UNCHALLENGED_MAGIC_WEIGHT`)
- Spam damping past 100: each skill keeps a leaky counter of recent weighted uses (60-second half-life); past a free burst — **40 combat / 10 magic / 6 utility** uses in roughly a half-life — each further use is worth free/recent of itself. `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:127` (`SPAM_FREE_BURST`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:126` (`SPAM_HALF_LIFE_MS`)
- Movement skills (Running, Jumping, Swimming, Climbing) take no spam damping and no foe law past 100: only real ground covered counts, read off the body's own odometer — Running and Jumping need 1 m of ground per use, Swimming 3 m through the water, Climbing 0.5 m up or down; banked credit caps at 2 uses. Holding a key standing still teaches nothing. `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:208` (`MOVEMENT_SKILLS`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:225` (`movementTallyWeight`)
- Climbing past 100: the classic climb chance is already certain by 95, so each effective point over 100 instead adds **1% climb speed** (×1.4 at 200). `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:107` (`CLIMB_OVERCAP_SPEED_PER_POINT`)
- Every magic school pays past 100 online at **+1% per effective point of its climb** (to ×1.4 at 200), each in its own dimension — Destruction's damage, Restoration's heals, Alteration's Shield, Illusion's concealments, Thaumaturgy's wards on yourself, Mysticism's chances at a foe. Online only, and none while mentoring. `src/systems/spell/spellPower.js:43` (`SCHOOL_POTENCY_PER_POINT`); `src/systems/spell/spellPower.js:52` (`schoolPotency`)
- Every weapon skill pays past 100 online at the same **+1% per effective climb point** (the shared `WEAPON_POTENCY_PER_POINT`), each its own way: Long Blade and Archery — every blow/arrow 1% harder (×1.4 at 200, the Thunderlock's shot included, scored on Archery); Axe — blows at a foe under half health 2% harder (×1.8); Blunt Weapon — blows at a foe at half health or more 2% harder (×1.8); Short Blade — every swing 1% quicker, never past the swing law's 0.45 s floor; Hand-to-Hand — nothing more, its damage formula already reads the climb (11–21 at 100, 15–29 at effective 140). None of it applies against a player, in a refereed fight, or while mentoring. `src/systems/weaponMastery.js:45` (`WEAPON_POTENCY_PER_POINT`); `src/systems/weaponMastery.js:75` (`weaponPastPct`); `src/systems/weaponMastery.js:49` (`WEAPON_HALF_LINE`)

### The world's answer — tougher foes
- Your **combat edge** is the mean effective gain over 100 of your three best combat or magic skills (0–40; real permanent values only — no potions, no worn Fortify; 0 while mentoring or with Master Skills off). `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:345` (`combatEdge`)
- Two scaling layers answer it in dungeons, each multiplied by the dungeon kind's share and the foe's own share: **Veteran** (ramps in as your three best skills climb 75→100 even before any pass 100: at full, +10 foe skill, ×2.0 health, ×1.25 damage) and **Overcap** (at edge 40: a further +26 skill, ×1.4 more health, ×1.35 more damage — at best skills of 200 and full share: +36 skill, ×3.4 health, ×1.6 damage). A foe's real level is not raised, so its spells, loot and gear are not scaled twice; what it *teaches* rises with its skills (challengeLevel = level + skill bonus / 2). `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:304` (`ENEMY_SCALING`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:410` (`progressionScaling`)
- Dungeon-kind shares of that scaling: Mine and Natural Cave **11%**; Human Stronghold, Ruined Castle, Spider Nest, Cemetery **22%**; Harpy Nest, Prison, Scorpion Nest **44%**; Crypt, Orc Stronghold, Giant Stronghold **88%**; Laboratory, Barbarian Stronghold, Coven, Vampire Haunt, Desecrated Temple, Dragon's Den, Volcanic Caves **100%**. Foe share: a class enemy (bandit, knight, sorcerer) counts in full; a monster counts `clamp((baseLevel − 4)/8, 0, 1)` — a low-level monster stays itself. `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:310` (`DUNGEON_SHARE`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:360` (`foeShare`)
- The wilderness scales too: **22% by day, 44% at night**. Towns, cities and other locations' grounds never scale from your climb. `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:356` (`WILDERNESS_SHARE`)
- Separately, the **place itself** sets a Veteran floor by its loot-ladder tier (none at a town's tier 4 or under, whole at a Dragon's Den's 18) that stands even without Master Skills, while mentoring, or offline; your own layers sit beside it and the larger applies. `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:399` (`PLACE_THREAT`); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:401` (`placeVeteran`)


### Specialisations — the shared law
- There are **30 paths**: 15 arcane, one for each pair of the six magic schools (Destruction, Restoration, Illusion, Alteration, Thaumaturgy, Mysticism), and 15 martial, one for each pair of the six weapon skills (Long Blade, Short Blade, Axe, Blunt Weapon, Archery, Hand-to-Hand). `src/systems/arcanePaths.js:239` (`PATH_ROWS`); `src/systems/martialPaths.js:309` (`PATH_ROWS`); `src/systems/martialPaths.js:42` (`MARTIAL_SKILLS`)
- To choose a path you must have **mastered both of its two skills** (each at 100 and mastered, spending mastery slots — the two masteries can come from any career groups, but the single minor slot means two minor skills can never both be mastered). Master Skills must be in force and you must hold no path of that family yet. `src/systems/arcanePaths.js:294` (`pathChoices`); `src/systems/martialPaths.js:362` (`martialChoices`); `src/systems/masterSkills.js:131` (`masterableCount`)
- A character may hold **one arcane path and one martial path at the same time**; neither choice reads or blocks the other, and both draw their masteries from the same 2/2/1 slots. `src/systems/martialPaths.js:25` (`MARTIAL` header — one of each); `src/systems/arcanePaths.js:311` (`choosePath`)
- The choice is asked once, on a quiet pass after it opens, and can be made at any time afterwards in the guide (below). Declining costs nothing and the offer stays. `src/systems/arcanePaths.js:333` (`nextPathChoice`); `src/systems/martialPaths.js:395` (`nextMartialChoice`)
- A path **acts** only while both its skills stay mastered and Master Skills is in force, and its powers sleep while you are mentoring; in a refereed fight (a duel) the powers sleep as every loot and set power does. Merely holding a path you no longer qualify for grants nothing — the masteries are read at every ask. `src/systems/arcanePaths.js:384` (`pathAwake`); `src/systems/arcanePaths.js:391` (`pathHeld`); `src/systems/martialPaths.js:457` (`martialAwake`)
- An arcane path's **signature spell** is sold only to that path's holder, is cast in the path's two schools (an effect outside the pair is priced, taught and strengthened by the stronger of the pair), and its record sits in its own index band (−46101…−46115). A signature spell in a book whose path is not held is just the plain bundle the spell maker could make — no rider. `src/systems/arcanePaths.js:54` (`SIGNATURE_INDEX_BASE`); `src/systems/arcanePaths.js:402` (`pathCastSchool`)
- A martial path's **signature technique** answers on the Weapon Technique key whenever the weapon in hand belongs to one of the pair's families and carries no technique line of its own; a pair that includes Archery has two forms (a bow's and a blade's/fist's) sharing **one cooldown**. Signatures are never loot lines — no item rolls one. `src/systems/martialPaths.js:463` (`signatureFor`); `src/systems/martialPaths.js:97` (`FORMS`)

### Path resets and skill swaps — the two paid ways back
- A **specialisation reset** costs a flat **200,000 gold per family**, whatever your level — twenty times the Firmament's whole-sky respec price. It clears only that family's path; your masteries stay (a mastery is permanent), and your spellbook stays — a signature spell whose path you no longer hold is read as the plain bundle, with no rider. `src/systems/arcanePaths.js:59` (`SPEC_RESET_GOLD`); `src/systems/specReset.js:67` (`resetSpec`); `src/systems/specReset.js:52` (`specResetRefusal`)
- The reset is paid as the Firmament's respec is paid: coins first, then letters of credit, and online any remainder comes off the **Empire's account** (one account for every region). 200,000 coins would weigh 500 kg, so no purse carries it alone. Offline only coins and letters can pay, and the refusal says so. `src/systems/specReset.js:46` (`resetGold`); `src/systems/specReset.js:52` (`specResetRefusal`)
- After a reset the guide offers every path your masteries open again — including the one you just gave up; the one-time "choose a path" ask is not repeated. A reset can only be bought while you hold a path of that family, and the guide warns when no *other* path could open for you (a reset would only buy back the same one). `src/systems/specReset.js:67` (`resetSpec`); `src/ui/growth/specGuide.js:169` (`resetModel`)
- A **skill swap** costs a flat **100,000 gold**: one class skill you have **not** mastered leaves its career group, and one miscellaneous skill takes its place **in the same group** (primary for primary, major for major, minor for minor) — so the 2/2/1 slots and every mastery already spent stand as they were. A mastered skill can never be swapped out. `src/systems/skillSwap.js:42` (`SKILL_SWAP_GOLD`); `src/systems/skillSwap.js:55` (`skillSwapRefusal`); `src/systems/skillSwap.js:100` (`swapSkill`)
- A swap moves **no skill values and no levels**: your level and your progress to the next stand; if the swap lowers your class skills' sum below the sum your level stands on, the class's skills must make that ground up before the next level comes any closer, and a swap that raises the sum past it pays no levels — no sequence of swaps can take a level past what the class's own lists could hold. Paid the same way as the reset (coins, letters, online the Empire's account). `src/systems/skillSwap.js:15` (`SWAP-CYCLE` header); `src/systems/skillSwap.js:100` (`swapSkill`)
- The swapped career becomes the character's own copy, marked `swapped` and saved whole — an heir keeps it — while the household still wears the original class's name and terms. `src/systems/skillSwap.js:78` (`swappedCareer`)

## Gates
- **Masteries**: a skill must reach 100; the mastery choice then needs a free slot in that skill's career group (2 primary / 2 major / 1 minor). No level, Renown, account or quest gate. `src/systems/masterSkills.js:151` (`masteryRefusal`); `src/systems/masterSkills.js:113` (`MASTERY_SLOTS`)
- **Specialisations**: both of a path's two skills mastered (each first at 100), Master Skills in force (automatic online), no path of that family already held. No Renown or registered-account gate in the path law. `src/systems/arcanePaths.js:294` (`pathChoices`); `src/systems/martialPaths.js:362` (`martialChoices`)
- **Reachability**: because only one minor skill can ever be mastered, a path whose two skills are both minor skills of your class can never open for that class unless a skill swap first moves one of them into a higher group; the guide says when one swap would open a path. `src/systems/masterSkills.js:131` (`masterableCount`); `src/systems/skillSwap.js:84` (`swapOpens`)

- **Skill swap**: an unmastered class skill to give up, a miscellaneous skill to take, and 100,000 gold on the same purse terms. `src/systems/skillSwap.js:55` (`skillSwapRefusal`)

## Online vs offline
- Master Skills: **always on online, no switch**; offline it is opt-in (off by default), asked once at the first primary mastery, changeable in the skill screen but never inside a dungeon. A skill above 100 reads as 100 while it is off, and its real value returns when it is on — so an offline character's climb is preserved, not lost, and counts in full when played online. `src/systems/masterSkills.js:108` (`masterSkillsActive`); `src/systems/masterSkills.js:203` (`masterCappedSkill`)
- The 5-point level pool and the health floor are **online only** (LEVEL-EVEN); offline the pool is a 4–6 draw and health is the accumulated rolls, low rolls and all. `src/systems/advancement.js:349` (`bonusPoolFor`); `src/systems/levelEven.js:35` (`evenHpFloor`)
- Weapon and school payoffs past 100 are **online only**; offline the weapon past 100 is Daggerfall's (to-hit alone), while the softcap's cost ladder, quarter-weight and milestones are Master Skills' law in both modes whenever it is in force. `src/systems/weaponMastery.js:28` (`MARTIAL2` header); `src/systems/skillSoftcap.js:51` (`SOFTCAP_TIERS`)
- Leveling system: a character **born online is classic-leveled**; the Oblivion "virtue" bar is only ever chosen offline, and online its dials are the room's, not the player's — the one thing of it that stays the player's online is that the mod is enabled for their character at all. `src/systems/oblivionLeveling.js:127` (`ONLINE_LEVELING_SYSTEM`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:511` (`'oblivion-remaster-leveling'`)
- Paying for a reset or a swap: online the Empire's single account can cover what coins and letters cannot; offline only the region's own coins and letters count. `src/systems/specReset.js:46` (`resetGold`)
