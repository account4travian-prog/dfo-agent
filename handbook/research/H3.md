# H3: Survival basics

## Player-facing summary
- Your carry limit is floor(Strength × 1.5) kg — and online, carrying past three quarters of it costs you speed and fatigue: Roleplay & Realism's encumbrance effects are forced on for every online player, so the heavier past 75% you are (up to 120% counted), the slower you walk and the more fatigue each game minute drains. `src/combat/formulas.js:101` (`maxEncumbrance`); `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrRealism.js:177` (`rrEncumbranceEffect`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:432` (`roleplay-realism` room keys)
- Resting is how health, fatigue and magicka come back: per hour of rest you regain a Medical-based share of health, one eighth of your fatigue pool and one eighth of your magicka pool. A bandage (a Roleplay & Realism: Items item, live online) heals on use instead: the lesser of Medical ÷ 3 and 40% of your maximum health. `src/systems/rest/rest.js:48` (`healthRecoveryRate`); `src/systems/rest/rest.js:64` (`fatigueRecoveryRate`); `src/systems/rest/rest.js:76` (`spellPointRecoveryRate`); `src/systems/roleplayRealismItems/rriRealism.js:102` (`bandageHeal`)
- Where you sleep matters: a bed or a lit campfire pays the full rest and clears sleep debt fastest; sleeping rough (the rest window anywhere else) is the last resort — on Hard it pays half, can be interrupted twice as often, and leaves you stiff for 4 hours. `src/systems/survival/rest.js:41` (`REST_KIND`); `src/systems/survival/rest.js:44` (`REST_COST`)
- If your fatigue hits zero you collapse: safe and alone, you lose an hour and wake partly restored; in water or with enemies about, each drain to zero instead costs you a tenth of your health — the port deliberately does not insta-kill you the way classic DFU did. `src/systems/rest/rest.js:126` (`exhaustionOutcome`); `src/systems/rest/rest.js:97` (`EXHAUSTED_SPENT_SHARE`)
- Hunger, thirst, sleep, wetness and temperature are tracked every game minute by the Climates & Calories survival arc, which each player sets for themselves — Off, Boons (the default), Casual or Hard — and only Hard takes attributes, health and sickness from you. `src/systems/survival/needs.js:55` (`NEED`); `src/systems/survival/difficulty.js:109` (`SURVIVAL_DEFAULT`); `src/systems/features.js:1647` (`mod-climates-calories` row)
- Food is worth minutes of satiety (an apple 60, a cooked meal about 200–240) and it spoils in stages — Fresh, Stale, Mouldy, Rotten, Putrid — losing half its worth per stage until Putrid food cannot be eaten at all. `src/systems/survival/food.js:34` (`FOOD`); `src/systems/survival/food.js:173` (`eatLaw`)
- A waterskin holds 2.0 kg of water; each drink is 0.1 kg and takes 40 off your thirst, and the game drinks for you automatically once you reach Thirsty if a skin with water is in your pack. `src/systems/survival/food.js:49` (`WATERSKIN_CAPACITY_KG`); `src/systems/survival/food.js:52` (`DRINK_RELIEF`); `src/systems/survival/needs.js:752` (`drinkWater`)
- Diseases are permanent until cured (a few run a 3–18 day course), tick once per day, and drain attributes and pools a little more each day; poisons work by the minute, each with its own onset delay and duration. `src/systems/diseases.js:66` (`DISEASE_DATA`); `src/systems/poisons.js:61` (`MIN_MINUTES_TO_POISON`)
- You carry one light at a time — Torch, Lantern, Candle or Holy Candle. A lit light burns one condition point every 20 real seconds and is consumed when it dies, except the Lantern, which survives empty and can be refuelled with a bottle of oil. Online, leaving a dungeon by day douses your lit light for you (Roleplay & Realism's auto-extinguish, at its shipped default). `src/systems/playerTorch.js:69` (`TORCH_TICK_SECONDS`); `src/systems/playerTorch.js:144` (lantern survival in `tickPlayerTorch`); `src/systems/useItem.js:428` (oil refuel arm in `useItem`); `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrRealism.js:199` (`rrDouseOnDungeonExit`)
- A lantern can be hung at your belt in its own inventory slot, where it lights your way without taking a hand; only one lantern hangs at a time. `src/systems/beltLantern.js:22` (`beltLanternOf`); `src/systems/beltLantern.js:36` (`hangBeltLantern`)

## Rules and numbers

### Inventory, weight and encumbrance
- Layer (this subsection): the ceiling, item weights and carried total are the port's own formulas and run online as written; the charge for carrying is Roleplay & Realism's (forced online) and the one stacking exception is Roleplay & Realism: Items' — each marked on its own bullet below. `src/combat/formulas.js:101` (`maxEncumbrance`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:432` (`roleplay-realism` room keys); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:415` (`roleplay-realism-items` room keys)
- Maximum encumbrance is `floor(Strength × 1.5)` kg, read off live Strength. `src/combat/formulas.js:101` (`maxEncumbrance`)
- Enchantments with Increased Weight Allowance raise the entity's ceiling: the allowance multiplier's percentage is added on top, truncated. `src/combat/formulas.js:114` (`entityMaxEncumbrance`)
- An item's unit weight is its template base weight, material-adjusted: weapons and plate armour go through the material weight function, leather armour through the truncated leather formula, and chain is unchanged. `src/systems/inventory.js:598` (`unitWeightInKg`)
- A carried waterskin weighs its water as well: the water's kg is added to the item's weight. `src/systems/inventory.js:598` (`unitWeightInKg`)
- Templates flagged `hasNoEncumbrance` (the flag ships on horse, cart, arrows, maps and quest letters) contribute 0 to encumbrance, though their shown weight is unchanged. `src/systems/inventory.js:622` (`effectiveUnitWeightInKg`)
- A stack's weight is its effective unit weight times its stack count. `src/systems/inventory.js:631` (`itemWeight`)
- Carried weight is the total weight of your items plus your loose gold pieces at 0.0025 kg each. `src/systems/inventory.js:661` (`carriedWeight`); `src/systems/inventory.js:650` (`GOLD_PIECE_WEIGHT_KG`)
- CORRECTED (online layer): the base note that exceeding MaxEncumbrance costs nothing does not run online. Online, Roleplay & Realism's encumbrance effects are forced: past 75% of your (allowance-included) MaxEncumbrance, the load starts charging. The base figure is still displayed (character sheet, inventory) and used by gates such as how much of an item a window can hand you. `src/scenes/corpseMarker.js:51` (header note — the base reading, superseded online); `src/systems/inventory.js:671` (`canHoldAmount`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:432` (`roleplay-realism` room keys); `src/systems/modSettings.js:629` (`encumbranceEffects` default)
- The online charge, exactly: with load fraction encPc = min(carried ÷ maxEncumbrance, 1.2), the excess is encOver = max(encPc − 0.75, 0) × 2 (`ENC_EFFECT_SCALE_FACTOR`); at a full 100% load encOver is 0.5, at the 120% cap it is 0.9, and at or under 75% there is no charge at all. `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrRealism.js:177` (`rrEncumbranceEffect`); `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrRealism.js:33` (`ENC_EFFECT_SCALE_FACTOR`)
- Speed: your live Speed is cut by trunc(permanent Speed × encOver) — up to half your permanent Speed at a full load, 90% at the cap — never taking live Speed below 2; the cut is folded into your stats and recomputed every magic round. `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrRealism.js:177` (`rrEncumbranceEffect`); `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrInstall.js:92` (`registerEntityFold` — `roleplay-realism-encumbrance`)
- Fatigue: each magic round (one per elapsed game minute) the load also spends fatigue: trunc(encOver × 100) raw units, capped at your current fatigue − 100, charged on the port's 0.75 exertion scale with the fraction carried (so a light overload is not free); it does not run while you rest, while you are dead, or on a fast travel's catch-up rounds, and it only ever reads the player's own load. `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrRealism.js:177` (`rrEncumbranceEffect`); `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrInstall.js:77` (`registerMagicRoundHook` — `roleplay-realism-encumbrance`); `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrInstall.js:198` (`encumbranceOf` guards); `src/systems/worldTick.js:302` (`claimMagicRounds`)
- The charge's denominator is the entity ceiling — live Strength × 1.5 plus Increased Weight Allowance's share (the `entityMaxEncumbrance` above), not the bare formula. `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrInstall.js:197` (`encumbranceOf`)
- One more place weight bites (port law, online as written): climbing/parkour reach. Past half of your carry capacity (carried ÷ max), a vault's hand reach shortens, by up to 0.3 (PARKOUR_LOAD_CUT) at a full pack; the free share is 0.5 of capacity. This is separate from RR's speed/fatigue charge above and both run online together. `src/player/parkour.js:264` (`PARKOUR_LOAD_FREE`); `src/player/parkour.js:298` (`parkourReach`); `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrInstall.js:92` (`registerEntityFold` — `roleplay-realism-encumbrance`)
- Online, climbing itself is Roleplay & Realism's rule (whole mod, shipped on): with a weapon drawn (other than bare hands), a mantle or clamber is refused with "You can't climb whilst holding your weapon.", and the classic climbing chance is zeroed the same way. `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrRealism.js:80` (`rrParkourRefusal`); `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrRealism.js:83` (`rrClimbingChance`); `src/systems/modSettings.js:633` (`climbingRestriction` default)
- Equipped items, enchanted ingredients and quest items never stack; ordinary stackable templates (e.g. Rations) do. One mod exception, live online: bandages stack, under Roleplay & Realism: Items' bandaging. `src/systems/inventory.js:192` (`isStackable`); `src/systems/inventory.js:193` (`isStackable` — RRI arm); `src/systems/roleplayRealismItems/rriRealism.js:99` (`isRriStackable`)
- Survival gear weights and prices: Camping Equipment 5.0 kg / 150 gold, Campfire 3.0 kg / 40, Rations 1.0 kg / 20, Waterskin 0.5 kg / 10, Skillet 5.0 kg / 60. `src/systems/survival/items.js:39` (`SURVIVAL_TEMPLATES`)

### Resting: rates

### Resting: kinds, gates and interruptions
- A rest is one of three kinds: Bed (a rented room, your own house, or your ship), Camp (within reach of a lit fire), or Rough (everything else). `src/systems/survival/rest.js:41` (`REST_KIND`); `src/systems/survival/rest.js:62` (`restKind`); `src/systems/survival/camp.js:54` (`BY_FIRE_REACH`)
- Bed and Camp rests pay the full hour's recovery, roll 1 resting-encounter check per minute, and carry no stiff morning, in every tier. `src/systems/survival/rest.js:44` (`REST_COST`)
- A Rough rest on Hard pays 0.5 of the hour's recovery, rolls 2 encounter checks per minute, and leaves you stiff for 4 hours; on Casual and Boons it pays 1, rolls 1, and leaves no stiffness. `src/systems/survival/difficulty.js:137` (`SURVIVAL_RULES` — `roughRest`)
- Stiff means Speed and Agility −5 until the stiff hours pass. `src/systems/survival/rest.js:51` (`STIFF_PENALTY`); `src/systems/survival/needs.js:301` (`survivalStatMods`)
- Sleep pays off sleep debt by kind: a bed or camp hour pays 1.5 hours of debt per hour asleep; a rough hour pays 0.5, and on Hard a rough night can never pay the debt below Tired. `src/systems/survival/needs.js:259` (`paySleep`)
- Rest is refused outright with enemies nearby, and cannot be started while swimming or not grounded. `src/systems/rest/restSession.js:231` (`restDecision`)
- The survival rest gate (Hard tier only) refuses sleep when the felt temperature word is Freezing or Deadly cold without a fire or a roof — "It is too cold to sleep here. Find a fire or shelter." — and refuses at Scorching anywhere — "It is too hot to sleep here." `src/systems/survival/rest.js:123` (`restBlock`); `src/systems/survival/env.js:100` (`survivalGateOn`)
- Starting a rest while poisoned or diseased asks first: the box says what ails you, "Resting will not cure it.", "Rest anyway?" `src/systems/rest/restWarning.js:13` (`restAilmentLines`)
- In town, camping in the open is the crime of Vagrancy: the first press is refused with the illegal-camping warning, confirming still registers the crime and spawns the watch; inside a building you need a rented room with hours left, your own house or ship, or a guild hall that lets you rest (taverns excluded). `src/systems/rest/restSession.js:293` (`canRest`); `src/systems/rest/restSession.js:104` (`ILLEGAL_REST_WARNING`)
- A timed rest can be set for at most 99 hours. `src/systems/rest/restSession.js:369` (`MAX_REST_HOURS`)
- Loitering is capped by the LoiterLimitInHours setting, default 3 hours (slider range 3–12), and loitering never goes through the town camping refusal or the Vagrancy crime. `src/systems/rest/restSession.js:96` (`loiterLimitHours`); `src/systems/rest/restSession.js:293` (`canRest`)
- The classic rest window paces a rested hour at 0.75 real seconds, advanced in 10-classic-minute sub-ticks; loiter runs slower at 1.25 real seconds per hour. `src/systems/rest/restSession.js:88` (`MINUTES_PER_TICK`); `src/systems/rest/restSession.js:89` (`REST_WAIT_PER_HOUR`)
- Online, rest is an act at a fire, tent or bed, not the window: you hold a 6-real-second channel, and the rest breaks if an enemy appears, you take a blow, or the fire/tent/Bedroll you rest at goes away while you hold. `src/systems/rest/restAct.js:42` (`REST_CHANNEL_SECONDS`); `src/systems/rest/restAct.js:174` (`channelBroken`)
- Online, a night is 8 hours on your own character clock; a foe spawning breaks a running night at its next sub-tick. `src/systems/rest/restAct.js:35` (`NIGHT_HOURS`); `src/systems/rest/restAct.js:143` (`ambushNight`)
- Online, party members within 15 m rest with a member who rests at a fire, tent or bed; there is no ready-vote online. Joining is each player's own setting, even online — the "Rest with my party" pref (shipped on, the lane names it the player's own) decides whether you rest with your party or alone. `src/systems/rest/restAct.js:44` (`REST_ACT_TEXT.noVote`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:163` (`restWithParty` in `ONLINE_PLAYERS_OWN_PREFS`); `src/systems/uiPrefs.js:154` (`restWithParty` default); `src/scenes/world.js:20212` (`restsWithParty`)
- A rested night cannot be taken again until 120 of your own clock-minutes have passed. `src/systems/rest/restAct.js:38` (`NIGHT_INTERVAL_MINUTES`)
- Layer note (this subsection): the rest kinds, gates and interruptions are the port's own rest/survival code, online as written — except two marked rules: the party-join pref is the player's own (above), and clicking a bed to sleep is Roleplay & Realism's (below). `src/systems/rest/restSession.js:293` (`canRest`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:163` (`restWithParty` in `ONLINE_PLAYERS_OWN_PREFS`)

### Exhaustion
- Layer (this subsection): the port's own rest code, online as written; no mod replaces the collapse or the spent-health drain. `src/systems/rest/rest.js:126` (`exhaustionOutcome`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:308` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS`)
- When fatigue reaches 0 with no enemies nearby and dry feet, you collapse and one hour passes: you recover one hour's health, fatigue and magicka rates and tally Medical. `src/systems/rest/rest.js:126` (`exhaustionOutcome`)
- When fatigue reaches 0 in water, or on dry feet with foes about, there is no collapse and no death: each drain to nothing (one per game minute while the condition lasts) costs max(1, ceil(10% of maxHealth)) health, with a HUD warning line instead of a box. `src/systems/rest/rest.js:97` (`EXHAUSTED_SPENT_SHARE`); `src/systems/rest/rest.js:114` (`spentDamage`); `src/systems/rest/rest.js:126` (`exhaustionOutcome`)

### The survival needs (Climates & Calories arc)
- Layer (this subsection and the two following it): the port's own survival arc — `src/systems/survival/` is DFO's own code, not a vendored mod's, and the lane leaves its tier pref to the player; nothing in the forced or whole tables replaces a needs, tier or food rule. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:140` (SURV-TIERS note over `ONLINE_PLAYERS_OWN_PREFS`); `src/systems/features.js:1645` (`mod-climates-calories`)
- Hunger stages by minutes since the last meal: Peckish at 240, Hungry at 720, Starving at 1440. `src/systems/survival/needs.js:55` (`NEED`); `src/systems/survival/needs.js:210` (`hungerStage`)
- Thirst runs 0–150: Thirsty at 50, Parched at 80, Dehydrated at 100, and it starts costing health past 120 in a tier that wounds. `src/systems/survival/needs.js:55` (`NEED`); `src/systems/survival/needs.js:217` (`thirstStage`)
- Thirst rises at 100/360 per minute at a comfortable temperature, scaled up by felt heat (felt 40 is about four times as fast). `src/systems/survival/needs.js:67` (`THIRST_PER_MINUTE`)
- Sleep debt: you may be awake 16 hours free; after that debt grows 1 hour per hour awake, capped at 24. Stages: Tired at 4, Drowsy at 8, Exhausted at 12 hours of debt. `src/systems/survival/needs.js:55` (`NEED`); `src/systems/survival/needs.js:223` (`sleepStage`)
- Wetness runs 0–300: Damp at 5, Wet at 30, Soaked at 100, Drenched at 200; warmth, shelter and a fire dry you. `src/systems/survival/needs.js:55` (`NEED`); `src/systems/survival/needs.js:229` (`wetStage`)
- Exposure counts the minutes the felt temperature has stood past 30 (either way); attribute drain starts past that and health harm past 50. `src/systems/survival/needs.js:55` (`NEED` — `EXPOSURE_AT`, `DAMAGE_AT`)
- Per-minute fatigue drains from the needs (raw units): heat/cold 6 per full 20 degrees of felt temperature, Starving 4, Parched 6, Dehydrated 12, Exhausted (sleep) 8, bare feet 4; an hour spent fed pays 64 back. `src/systems/survival/needs.js:72` (`DRAIN`)
