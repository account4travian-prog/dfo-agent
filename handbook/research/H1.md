# H1: The opening and Privateer's Hold
Commit: 0df2d87eb · Researcher: Atlas swarm (H1; online-layer re-check by Atlas swarm) · Date: 2026-10-21

## Player-facing summary
- A new game follows the classic start sequence: the front door leads into the title, the opening film and the classic start window (Load Game / Start New Game / Exit), then character creation. `src/main.js:143` (front-door routing comment)
- A new character starts inside Privateer's Hold, the dungeon on the configured start cell — shipped as map cell 109, 158, with Start In Dungeon on. `src/systems/settings/settingsDefaults.js:177` (`StartCellX`/`StartCellY`/`StartInDungeon`)
- The first thing the game tells you (its own welcome tutorial page) is, in paraphrase: you wake in a small cave, take what you need from your pack, find the way out and make for the nearest town. `src/systems/tutorialCatalogue.js:61` (`welcome` page)
- Five short tutorial pages — the "welcome tour" — play when a new character first stands in the world: Welcome, Moving Around, Using Things, The Bars and the Compass, and The Menu and Saving; they are tracked per player (per device prefs), not per character, and can be re-read in the menu. `src/systems/tutorialCatalogue.js:647` (`TUTORIAL_TOUR`); `src/systems/tutorials.js:9` (TUT1 header)
- Two quests start automatically for every new character, in this order: S0000977 (the curse quest the retired DFU tutorial used to start) and _BRISIEN, the main quest's first quest. `src/scenes/quest/questBridge.js:201` (`GAME_START_QUESTS`)
- You leave the Hold through its exit door and land outside on the entrance landing, facing out; the monument to Julian LeFay stands beside that way out, and Gothway Garden — described in code as the first town a new character walks into — is the nearest town, its road coming in from the Hold at the town's north entrance. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:10383` (`returnLanding`); `src/world/lefayMonument.js:8` (LEFAY1 header); `src/systems/gothwayBoards.js:4` (GOTHWAY-BOARDS header)
- Online, the Hold is a one-way door: once you have left, its public room refuses re-entry ("You cannot go back into Privateer's Hold."), although an instanced copy of it can be entered any time. `src/systems/startDungeon.js:29` (`HOLD_ONCE_TEXT`); `src/scenes/worldModes.js:9645` (`tryEnterDungeon`)
- Dying online never ends the run: in the Hold you respawn where you started (the dungeon's start/enter marker), and anywhere else you wake at the nearest temple, town or graveyard — or, if you died in another dungeon, just outside its door. You come back with half your health and lose a tenth of the gold you were carrying; banked gold is untouched. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:9781` (`onlineRespawn`); `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:126` (`RESPAWN_HEALTH_FRACTION`); `src/systems/deathPenalty.js:35` (`DEATH_GOLD_DIVISOR`)
- Online, the room's ground mods are forced on for everyone — World of Daggerfall among them, which is why the Hold's camp always stands online — while your skin, your tutorial popups and your skip-the-film choice stay your own. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:320` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS`, `world-of-daggerfall`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:143` (`tutorialPopups` in `ONLINE_PLAYERS_OWN_PREFS`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:150` (`skipStartVideo`)

## Rules and numbers

### The start and the opening
- The shipped start cell is map pixel X 109, Y 158, and the shipped default is to start inside its dungeon. `src/systems/settings/settingsDefaults.js:177` (`StartCellX`, `StartCellY`, `StartInDungeon`)
- The start cell is configurable: the classic start, the tutorial-dungeon test and the online respawn exceptions all read the same two settings keys, so a custom start cell moves the start. `src/systems/startDungeon.js:13` (`isStartCell`)
- The dungeon on the start cell is the tutorial dungeon. `src/systems/startDungeon.js:22` (`isStartDungeon`)
- Privateer's Hold itself is identified by its map id, 187853213, independently of the configured start cell. `src/world/dungeon/dungeonTextures.js:91` (`PRIVATEERS_HOLD_MAP_ID`); `src/systems/startDungeon.js:26` (`isPrivateersHold`)
- In the classic skin, the menu's BEGIN leads into the classic sequence: title, opening film, then the start window (Load Game / Start New Game / Exit). `src/main.js:143` (FD1 comment)
- The start video can be skipped by the player's own "skip start video" preference, and the classic sequence can be entered directly (the old bare-URL behaviour). `src/main.js:206` (`skip`/`skipStartVideo`); `src/main.js:159` (`?begin` comment)
- Online, the boot drops the URL power flags before anything reads them — among them `?spawn`, `?region` and `?loc`, which would choose where a fresh character stands; a shared world does not let a link pick your start. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:102` (refused-flags comment); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:106` (`ONLINE_REFUSED_FLAGS`)
- At world boot, a classic new game with Start In Dungeon on and a dungeon at the start location enters that dungeon as the spawn itself (no exterior spawn first). `src/scenes/world.js:30200` (classic boot arm)
- That first entry is made with the Hold held open — the new game's start is the one ordinary way into Privateer's Hold online. `src/scenes/world.js:30200` (`startInDungeon({ holdOpen: true })`)
- Every new character starts two quests, in order: S0000977, then _BRISIEN (the main quest's first). `src/scenes/quest/questBridge.js:201` (`GAME_START_QUESTS`)
- DFU's old _TUTOR__ tutorial quest is no longer started; it is retired by name, and DFO's own tutorial stands in its place. `src/scenes/quest/questBridge.js:195` (TUT1 comment); `src/scenes/quest/questBridge.js:201` (`RETIRED_TUTORIAL_QUEST`)
- The welcome tour is exactly five pages: welcome, movement, interacting, hud, menu. `src/systems/tutorialCatalogue.js:647` (`TUTORIAL_TOUR`)
- The tour plays when a new character first stands in the world, in both the exterior host and the world host. `src/scenes/exterior.js:1608` (`startTutorialTour`); `src/scenes/world.js:6945` (`startTutorialTour`)
- Tutorial read-state and the popup on/off switch are the player's (device prefs), not the character's: a second character is not re-taught pages already read. `src/systems/tutorials.js:16` (THE SHELF, NOT THE SAVE comment); `src/systems/tutorials.js:29` (`TUTORIAL_POPUPS_PREF`, `TUTORIALS_SEEN_PREF`)
- Each later tutorial page pops once, the first time the player reaches that system, and every page can be re-read from the menu. `src/systems/tutorials.js:9` (TUT1 header)
- Layer for this group: port. The start cell and Start In Dungeon are the port's shipped settings defaults, and the online lane forces no Startup setting (its one forced DFU setting is AllowMagicRepairs); the skin the classic sequence plays in is the player's own online, not a forced one. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:178` (`ONLINE_FORCED_SETTINGS`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:123` (OVH3: the skin is the player's, online too)

### Inside the Hold and the way out
- The standalone dungeon scene defaults its dungeon name to "Privateer's Hold" when no dungeon is named. `src/scenes/dungeon.js:106` (`dungeonName`)
- Leaving a dungeon is by its exit door: activating the exit runs the transition to the exterior. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:10367` (`exitDungeonNow` call in `tryExitDungeon`)
- The exit lands the player on the entrance door they came in by (the dungeon-entrance landing), positioned at that door facing its outward normal. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:10383` (`returnLanding`); `src/scenes/worldModes.js:10384` (`exitDungeonNow`)
- If the pack holds a small cart and the Dungeon Exit Wagon Prompt setting is on, the exit door first asks (a Yes/No prompt) before the exit completes; with the setting off the exit is immediate. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:10333` (exit-door wagon prompt arm in `tryExitDungeon`)
- Online, a dungeon's dead foes and emptied containers respawn on their own clocks: 20 minutes in a normal dungeon, 40 minutes in an elite one; offline, nothing respawns. `src/systems/dungeon/dungeonRespawn.js:10` (`DUNGEON_RESPAWN_MS`); `src/systems/dungeon/dungeonRespawn.js:11` (`ELITE_DUNGEON_RESPAWN_MS`); `src/systems/dungeon/dungeonRespawn.js:15` (`dungeonRespawnMs`)
- A wild (zone) dungeon or a Super dungeon is the exception to that pair: both respawn on the wire's own 30-minute clock. `src/net/wire.js:519` (`RESPAWN_MS`); `src/systems/dungeon/dungeonRespawn.js:15` (`dungeonRespawnMs`)
- The Hold's exterior block is CUSTAA30.RMB, a 1×1 exterior block. `src/world/worldOfDaggerfall/wodPrivateersHold.js:39` (`PRIVATEERS_HOLD_BLOCK`)
- Where the World of Daggerfall camp stands at the Hold's exterior, it places seven possible foes (thieves, assassins and rogues); each stands only on a roll of 21–30 out of 0–30 (9 chances in 30). `src/world/worldOfDaggerfall/wodPrivateersHold.js:121` (`HOLD_FOES`); `src/world/worldOfDaggerfall/wodPrivateersHold.js:161` (`rollHoldFoes`)
- The Hold's interior layout, its interior foes and its loot are not defined in `src/`: no file in the code tree specifies them (they come from the game's dungeon data), so their contents are not found in source. `src/systems/startDungeon.js:1` (the only code-side definition of the tutorial dungeon is its cell and map id)
- Layer for this group: port, with one mod piece. Entry, exit and the respawn clocks are the port's own code; the Hold's exterior camp is World of Daggerfall content — it stands only while that mod's one switch is on, and online the lane forces that switch on for the whole room, so the camp and its foes are always there online. `src/world/worldOfDaggerfall/worldOfDaggerfall.js:51` (`wodOn`); `src/scenes/exterior.js:468` (`holdOn`, WOD4 comment); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:320` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS`, `world-of-daggerfall`)

### The LeFay Monument and the nearest town
- The monument to Julian LeFay stands outside Privateer's Hold, beside the exit landing — the spot chosen is the open ground nearest the way out, so it is the first thing a character climbing out of the Hold sees; it was moved there from Gothway Garden. `src/world/lefayMonument.js:8` (LEFAY1/HOLD-MONUMENT header); `src/world/lefayMonument.js:42` (`LEFAY_TOWN`)
- Its placement keeps the way out clear: no part of its clear disc (5.8 m radius of open ground around its middle) may sit within 3.2 m of the exit-landing line, which runs from 4 m behind the landing to 8 m ahead of it. `src/world/lefayMonument.js:80` (`MONUMENT_CLEAR_M`); `src/world/lefayMonument.js:93` (`MONUMENT_LANDING_M`, `MONUMENT_DOOR_BACK_M`, `MONUMENT_WALK_M`)
- The monument is three octagonal granite steps, a marble pedestal with a bronze plaque on each face, and a gilt-tipped marble obelisk 6.95 m to its apex. `src/world/lefayMonument.js:23` (WHAT comment); `src/world/lefayMonument.js:243` (`OBELISK`)
- The plaque (1.0 m × 0.8 m, one per face) is engraved with the name Julian LeFay, the years 1965–2025, and the epithet "Father of The Elder Scrolls" (short naming phrases only, as labelled in code). `src/world/lefayMonument.js:245` (`PLAQUE`); `src/world/lefayMonument.js:48` (`LEFAY_TEXT`)
- Interacting with the plaque offers two rows: "Throw flowers" and "Read the inscription". `src/world/lefayMonument.js:66` (`LEFAY_ROWS`)
- A character's laid-flower count is kept, and the newest 48 flowers laid stay where they lie across a load. `src/world/lefayMonument.js:362` (`MONUMENT_FLOWERS_KEPT`)
- Gothway Garden is the first town a new character walks into out of Privateer's Hold; the road from the Hold enters at its north side, where two bounty boards stand at the north entrance, 5 m inside the border and 1.5 m beside the road's edge. `src/systems/gothwayBoards.js:4` (GOTHWAY-BOARDS header); `src/systems/gothwayBoards.js:20` (`NORTH_IN_M`, `NORTH_ASIDE_M`)
- No code in `src/` states the walking distance from the Hold's exit to Gothway Garden: not found in source. `src/systems/gothwayBoards.js:4` (the only code-side statement of the Hold–Gothway relation, and it gives no distance)
- Layer for this group: port. The monument is the port's own world code, placed by the hosts; no mod in the lane's room or whole tables stands, moves or gates it. `src/world/lefayMonument.js:8` (LEFAY1 header); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:308` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS`)

### Death and respawn online
- Online, death replaces the classic ending: the death sequence plays (camera sink, fade, sound), then the player wakes at a safe point instead of the death video and title menu. `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:1` (D-ONLINE1 header)
- The death sequence itself: the camera falls at 2.5 units/s toward a target a quarter-capsule below the feet, the HUD fades over 2 seconds, and the reset fires 3 seconds after death. `src/systems/playerDeath.js:33` (`DEATH_FALL_SPEED`, `DEATH_TIME_BEFORE_RESET`, `DEATH_FADE_DURATION`)
- On the death screen, an online player respawns automatically after 60 seconds, or at once on Enter. `src/ui/deathScreen.js:63` (`ONLINE_RESPAWN_SECONDS`); `src/ui/deathScreen.js:208` (`holdSeconds`)
- Dying inside Privateer's Hold online is the special case: the player respawns at the Hold's start (enter) marker — where they started — not outside it and not in a town. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:9781` (`onlineRespawn`); `src/scenes/worldModes.js:9791` (`startSpawn({ preferEnterMarker: true })`)
- That Hold respawn still pays the ordinary online death costs (the death penalty is applied on that path too). `src/scenes/worldModes.js:9781` (`onlineRespawn`, `applyDeathPenalty` call)
- Dying in any other dungeon online respawns just outside that dungeon's door, on its own map pixel. `src/scenes/world.js:14848` (`respawnOnlinePlayer`, dungeon arm)
- Dying above ground (or in the Hold, on the exterior host's path — see Open questions) respawns at the nearest of three safe kinds in the current region: a temple, a town (city, hamlet or village), or a graveyard (a Graveyard location of the small Cemetery dungeon type). `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:30` (`SAFE_KINDS`); `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:51` (`nearestSafeLocation`)
- If the region where the player died holds none of the three (for example the open sea), the search widens to the nearest in any region. `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:79` (`nearestSafeLocationAnywhere`)
- The landing is the safe place's own start marker (a town's gate, a cemetery's, a dungeon's door), not the middle of its map pixel. `src/scenes/world.js:14886` (respawn landing comment and flavour box)
- A short flavour line naming the kind of place is shown on waking, together with the gold-loss line when gold was lost. `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:118` (`respawnFlavorText`); `src/scenes/world.js:14886` (respawn overlay)
- Respawn health is half of maximum, floored at 1: `respawnHealth = max(1, floor(maxHealth × 0.5))`. `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:126` (`RESPAWN_HEALTH_FRACTION`); `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:136` (`respawnHealth`)
- Fatigue is floored the same way on revival: at least the respawn fraction of maximum fatigue, keeping any more the player already had. `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:379` (`reviveForPlay`)
- The gold penalty is one tenth of the gold carried on the person, rounded down (a purse of 9 loses nothing; 100 loses 10); the bank account and letters of credit are not touched. `src/systems/deathPenalty.js:35` (`DEATH_GOLD_DIVISOR`); `src/systems/deathPenalty.js:39` (`deathGoldLoss`)
- The death screen reads the loss once, as the player falls, and the respawn takes exactly that stated amount, capped at what the purse then holds; being Resurrected by another player spares the loss. `src/systems/deathPenalty.js:58` (`applyDeathPenalty`); `src/systems/deathPenalty.js:46` (AUDIT 28 B5 comment)
- The Open Zone's own death path is not an exception to the tenth: its purse is the death penalty's, unchanged. `src/systems/wild/wildDeath.js:26` (purse comment)
- One scoped exception does exist, outside the open world: a player-made level whose own rules set `deathPenalty` to 'none' takes no tenth inside that level, and the death screen's penalty flag reads that made-level rule. `src/systems/madeLevel.js:69` (`deathPenalty` rule); `src/scenes/dungeonContext.js:3229` (DeathScreen `penalty` flag)
- Revival ends the effects that would kill again before the player can act: poison, continuous damage and health transfer are cleared. `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:214` (`LETHAL_DRAINS`)
- Revival also releases a killer's holds: paralysis, silence, and fatigue and spell-point drains are ended. `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:250` (`DEATH_HOLDS`)
- Diseases are deliberately kept through a respawn (so dying is never a free cure — vampirism and lycanthropy are carried as diseases), but a stat a disease holds at zero is lifted back to the respawn fraction of its permanent value. `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:230` (DEATHLOOP1 KEPT comment); `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:334` (`liftZeroedStats`)
- Stored survival exposure and wetness are reset to 0 on revival, so a freezing death does not loop; the weather itself is unchanged. `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:293` (`REVIVED_SURVIVAL_RESET`)
- The minutes spent dead are not charged to the revived body (no stamina, needs or magic-round costs for that span on the shared clock). `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:379` (`reviveForPlay`, `skipDeadMinutes` call)
- A death ends every fight the player was in, and clears the committed-crime chase (the crime is set to None and an arrest in progress is abandoned). `src/scenes/world.js:14753` (`respawnOnlinePlayer`)
- Loading an online save that was made underground (in any dungeon except the tutorial's) also wakes the character above ground at the nearest temple, town or graveyard — with no health taken and a "carried out while you were away" line instead of a death line; the tutorial dungeon is the caller's stated exception. `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:155` (`undergroundWakeSpot` and ONLINE-UNDERGROUND-LOAD1 comment)
- Offline, death is the classic shape — the sequence runs to the death video and the title menu (load a save) — except that an offline Project Legacy "Enduring" rise respawns with the same one-tenth gold cost. `src/systems/playerDeath.js:1` (D1 header); `src/systems/deathPenalty.js:14` (BAL4 comment)
- Layer for this group: port, verified against the mods. The tenth is defined only in deathPenalty.js and the respawn fraction only in deathRespawn.js, and no mod file names either; the balance mods the lane holds whole online (PCAAO, Roleplay & Realism, Meaner Monsters, Unleveled Loot, Enemy Expansion) are combat, loot and foe systems, not death systems. `src/systems/deathPenalty.js:35` (`DEATH_GOLD_DIVISOR`); `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:126` (`RESPAWN_HEALTH_FRACTION`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:504` (`ONLINE_WHOLE_MODS`)
- The one mod touching death in this group is Project Legacy, and it is a player's-own mod: the lane lists it among the mods whose switches stay the player's online, its births and deaths one player's own characters' — so its Enduring rise is never a room rule, online or off. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:476` (`project-legacy` in `ONLINE_PLAYERS_OWN_MODS`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:450` (`ONLINE_PLAYERS_OWN_MODS`)

## Gates
- The Hold itself: gate is a new character on the classic start. A classic new game enters it at boot when Start In Dungeon is on (shipped default) and the start location has a dungeon. `src/scenes/world.js:30200` (classic boot arm)
- No level, Renown, account-registration, quest or setting gate beyond the classic start is stated in code for entering the Hold the first time: the entry is the new game's own, made with the Hold held open. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:9634` (`tryEnterDungeon`, `holdOpen`)
- Re-entry (online): refused at the Hold's public door, a Recall to an anchor inside, and a quest transfer, once the player is outside — the refusal line is shown and the entry returns false. Exceptions named in code: the new game's start, a save made inside it, and a staff landing. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:9645` (HOLD-ONCE arm)
- Re-entry (online, instanced): an instance of the Hold beside its public room can be entered any time; the character's Hold instance epoch is permanent (derived from the character, opened by no quest). `src/net/instanceRoom.js:65` (`holdEpochOf`)
- Offline: no re-entry refusal — the Hold behaves as DFU's does. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:9641` (HOLD-ONCE comment)
- The LeFay Monument: no gate — it stands on open exterior ground beside the Hold's exit for anyone who walks out. `src/world/lefayMonument.js:8` (LEFAY1 header)
- The welcome tour and tutorials: no character gate; they are keyed to the player's device prefs, so they appear for a player's first character and are already-read for later ones. `src/systems/tutorials.js:16` (THE SHELF, NOT THE SAVE comment); online, the lane leaves the `tutorialPopups` switch to the player by name, so that stays true inside a room. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:143` (`tutorialPopups` in `ONLINE_PLAYERS_OWN_PREFS`)
- Death respawn: online play only; the same death offline ends the run unless a Project Legacy rise applies. `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:1` (D-ONLINE1 header); `src/systems/deathPenalty.js:14` (BAL4 comment)
- Layer for this section: port throughout — no gate above comes from a mod, and the lane's forced tables add no level, Renown or account gate to the Hold, the monument or the tour. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:308` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:504` (`ONLINE_WHOLE_MODS`)

## Online vs offline
- Death: online respawns in place of the run ending (nearest safe place, half health, one-tenth carried-gold loss); offline runs the classic sequence to the death video and title menu, with a Project Legacy Enduring rise the one offline respawn — and it now pays the same tenth. `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:1` (D-ONLINE1 header); `src/systems/deathPenalty.js:14` (BAL4 comment)
- Death in the Hold: online (or an offline Legacy rise) respawns at the Hold's start marker inside the dungeon; the ordinary dungeon-death rule (wake at the dungeon's door) is deliberately not applied to the Hold, so dying cannot skip it. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:9781` (`onlineRespawn`); `src/scenes/world.js:14836` (D-ONLINE2 comment)
- Re-entering the Hold: online, its public room is entered once and then refused; an instance of it may be entered any time. Offline, entry is DFU's (no refusal). `src/scenes/worldModes.js:9641` (HOLD-ONCE comment); `src/net/instanceRoom.js:65` (`holdEpochOf`)
- Saving/loading position: online, leaving the page saves the character, and a save made underground loads back above ground at the nearest safe place (tutorial dungeon excepted); offline, a load restores the saved spot as DFU's does. `src/systems/deathRespawn.js:155` (ONLINE-UNDERGROUND-LOAD1 comment)
- Dungeon contents: online, a normal dungeon's foes and containers respawn after 20 minutes each on their own clocks; offline, nothing respawns. `src/systems/dungeon/dungeonRespawn.js:10` (`DUNGEON_RESPAWN_MS`); `src/systems/dungeon/dungeonRespawn.js:19` (`dungeonRespawnDue`)
