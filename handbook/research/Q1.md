# Q1: Quests and questlines
Commit: 0df2d87eb · Researcher: Atlas swarm (Q1) · Date: 2026-10-21

## Player-facing summary
- Quests come in four kinds: the classic one-off quests (guild, temple, commoner, noble lists), the main quest, one world quest (the Curse of Daggerfall), and 14 authored **questlines** — chained stories the Quests tab tracks as lines.
- The questlines are the game's introductions and its endgame stories: eight short introduction lines teach professions, gear, the sea, wagons, tavern games, the Arena, town raids and the Firmament; six story lines cover Dagon's Breaches, Elite Dungeons, the Elite Breach, Abyss Dungeons, the sea serpent and the Starfall festival.
- Online, waiting is short: a quest clock that only makes you wait (a letter coming, "come back in three days") is cut to about two real minutes. A clock that is a real time limit keeps its full length — but it runs only on time you actually play, so no deadline ever expires while you are logged off.
- Courier letters pace the questlines: a line's next step starts an hour of your character's time after the last step ended, a new line never begins within an hour of another line's start or letter, and a letter itself only reaches you in a town, outdoors, by day.
- Failing a questline step costs nothing permanent: the step is offered again a day later. A seasonal step caught by the festival ending is set aside fail-free and resumes next season.
- The main quest starts at character creation (Lady Brisienna's _BRISIEN) and is never shared with a party and never error-terminates; the Curse of Daggerfall (S0000977) also starts for every character, and online its night wraiths and ghosts are shared world foes everyone in the street can fight.
- Your guilds appear in the same window as ladders: each guild or order you belong to shows its quests rung by rung, rank by rank, with its flagship quests marked.

- The Weir Road pays **weirwalker** at step 3 (index 2); its first three steps are witnessed by a Redoubt taken, three Redoubts, and a Valkynaz's Redoubt. `src/net/questlineClaims.js:42` (`weirRoad`)

### Quest rewards on the sheet system (classic + line quests)
Layer: **port** — outcomes on a quest's sheet name a pay, and the pay profile decides gold and gear. `src/systems/questlines/qlRewards.js:41` (`PAY_PROFILES`)
- Pay profiles: standard = full gold + a gear piece; lesser = 0.75× gold + a piece; greater = 1.25× gold + a piece one tier-step up; betrayal / own / none = no added piece (the script's own reward, or nothing). `src/systems/questlines/qlRewards.js:41` (`PAY_PROFILES`)
- An outcome's gold factor is clamped between 0.75 and 1.5. `src/systems/questlines/qlRewards.js:51` (`OUTCOME_GOLD_MIN`); `src/systems/questlines/qlRewards.js:52` (`OUTCOME_GOLD_MAX`)
- A gear-paying outcome offers a choice of 3 pieces. `src/systems/questlines/qlRewards.js:54` (`REWARD_CHOICES`)
- A waiting reward is chosen by the player afterwards — the window directs them to choose it under Quests in the pause menu. `src/ui/questlines/qlJournal.js:60` (`ql.reward.waitingPause`)

### Letters and pacing
Layer: **port** — the runner's pacing and the quest machine's letter delivery. `src/systems/questlines/qlLineRunner.js:49` (`STEP_GAP_S`)
- Between one step's end and the next step's start a line waits 3,600 game seconds — one hour of the character's time. `src/systems/questlines/qlLineRunner.js:49` (`STEP_GAP_S`)
- A new line may begin only when no other line began within that same hour, no line's letter reached the player within it, and no begun line's first letter is still on its way — a character opening many lines gets their letters an hour apart, never in one breath. `src/systems/questlines/qlLineRunner.js:201` (`mayBegin`)
- A letter reaches the player only in a town, outdoors, by day (the GivePc delivery rule); a letter whose courier ran while the player was elsewhere is still "on its way" and holds the pace. `src/systems/questlines/qlLineRunner.js:66` (`letterOnItsWay`)
- Classic quest letters (for example Brisienna's, scripted at 7–14 days) are delays under the online clock law below: online they land on the short wait instead. `src/systems/quest/clock.js:152` (`ONLINE_DELAY_SECONDS`)

### Quest clocks online (deadlines vs delays)
Layer: **port, online branch** — REST8: clocks are read off their scripts as deadlines or delays; the reading is pinned by a hand table for the exceptions. `src/systems/quest/clock.js:358` (`ONLINE_DEADLINES`)
- A **deadline** (its end loses the quest, costs standing, or shuts a reward) keeps its full length online, runs on played world time, and fires as offline. `src/systems/quest/clock.js:376` (`questWaitsShort`)
- A **delay** (everything else — a letter coming, "come back in three days", a reward after a wait, a quest closing after its outcome) has its remainder cut once to ONLINE_DELAY_SECONDS = 1,440 quest-clock seconds (24 game minutes, about two real minutes of play), or its own remainder if less. `src/systems/quest/clock.js:152` (`ONLINE_DELAY_SECONDS`)
- Online, clocks charge only played time: one tick charges at most one played step of 1,800 seconds (30 minutes), time away is forgiven, and no clock expires while the player is away. `src/systems/quest/clock.js:97` (`PLAYED_STEP_MAX_SECONDS`)
- Of the 399 vendored quest clocks the reading splits 272 deadlines / 127 delays at this commit; the main quest alone holds 32 deadlines, and the hand table pins 13 named clocks as deadlines against the reading. `src/systems/quest/clock.js:358` (`ONLINE_DEADLINES`)
- Offline, none of this applies: DFU's clock runs untouched. `src/systems/quest/clock.js:376` (`questWaitsShort`)

### The main quest
Layer: **base quests, port start** — the vendored main-quest scripts, started by the port's new-game wiring. `src/scenes/quest/questBridge.js:201` (`GAME_START_QUESTS`)
- A new character always starts two quests, in this order: S0000977 (the curse) and _BRISIEN — _BRISIEN is the main quest's first quest. `src/scenes/quest/questBridge.js:201` (`GAME_START_QUESTS`); `src/scenes/quest/questBridge.js:953` (`initAtGameStart`)
- A "main quest name" is any S0000… quest or _BRISIEN. `src/systems/quest/questLists.js:48` (`isMainQuestName`)
- The main quest's sheet family holds 35 quests (from _BRISIEN through S0000999, including the curse S0000977), each with objectives and outcomes, failure outcomes among them. `src/systems/questlines/sheets/mainQuest.json:2` (`MainQuest`)
- The main-quest spine never error-terminates: S0000999, S0000977 and _BRISIEN are protected quests. `src/systems/quest/machine.js:340` (`PROTECTED_QUESTS`)
- The main quest is never shared with a party — it is the same one thread for everyone already — and a questline's step is never shared either; both are refused at both ends of a share. `src/systems/quest/questShare.js:106` (`prepareShareData`)

### The Curse of Daggerfall online
Layer: **port, online branch** — the curse is the one world quest: its foes are the world's, not a player's. `src/scenes/quest/questFoeHost.js:357` (`WORLD_QUESTS`)
- WORLD_QUESTS holds exactly one quest: S0000977, the Curse of Daggerfall, started for every character as the tutorial ends, finished or declined. `src/scenes/quest/questFoeHost.js:357` (`WORLD_QUESTS`); `src/scenes/quest/questBridge.js:201` (`GAME_START_QUESTS`)
- At night in Daggerfall's streets the curse stands a wraith every 21 minutes and a ghost every 31, one time in two; online those foes ride the cell as an encounter's do — every player present sees them, anyone can strike them and be hunted by them. `src/scenes/quest/questFoeHost.js:357` (`WORLD_QUESTS`)
- Every other quest's foes are private: a quest foe that is not a world quest's is its player's alone. `src/scenes/quest/questFoeHost.js:390` (`isPrivateQuestFoe`)
- A quest may join the world list only if no task counts its foes (no killed/injured tallies) — the curse qualifies because nothing in it counts its wraiths or ghosts. `src/scenes/quest/questFoeHost.js:357` (`WORLD_QUESTS`)

### Guild quest ladders and guild quest rules online
Layer: **port** — the window reads the guilds' own classic lists by rank; no new content. `src/systems/questlines/qlLadders.js:38` (`ladders`)
- Every guild, temple or order the player belongs to shows as a ladder: its quests grouped rung by rung from rank 0 up to the highest rank its list offers, promotions as the milestones, and a quest with a branch on its sheet marked flagship. `src/systems/questlines/qlLadders.js:38` (`ladders`)
- A list row whose floor is 10 or more is a reputation floor, not a rank, and is not a rung. `src/systems/questlines/qlLadders.js:17` (`RANK_FLOORS`)
- A rung is "reached" at the rank (or, for the Mages Guild and the knightly orders, the level) at which the guild offers its quests. `src/systems/questlines/qlLadders.js:38` (`ladders`)
- Guild quests are drawn from the guild's pool for the member's status, standing and rank. `src/systems/quest/questLists.js:192` (`getGuildQuestPool`)
- Online only, two Mages Guild quests wait for the standing their own headers name: "Guard the Guild" (N0B10Y03) is offered only at guild standing 10+, "Protect an Honored Mage" (N0B20Y02) at 20+ — because online that standing is the Roll's, kept by the service, and both quests can take large standing back. Offline the pool is DFU's, ungated. `src/systems/quest/onlineRepute.js:13` (`ONLINE_REPUTE_GATES`)
- "Guard the Guild" online also gets its own watch window: instead of the script's midnight-to-03:00 watch on the shared clock, the window opens 600 quest-clock seconds (10 game minutes) after the player first arrives at the Mages Guild hall and stands 3,600 (one game hour); leaving after it closes and returning opens a new watch — a missed watch is not a lost quest — and payment waits on the window being shut. `src/systems/quest/onlineGuard.js:28` (`ONLINE_GUARD_WINDOWS`)

### Failure, abandoning and restarting
Layer: **port** — the quest machine's shelf and the line runner's retry law. `src/systems/quest/machine.js:1634` (`shelveQuest`)
- A questline step that ends badly is offered again 86,400 game seconds later (a day of the character's time) — failure does not end the line. `src/systems/questlines/qlLineRunner.js:51` (`RETRY_GAP_S`); `src/systems/questlines/qlLineRunner.js:239` (`ended`)
- A step whose quest vanishes without its end (an error terminated it) is likewise offered again a day later, marked stalled. `src/systems/questlines/qlLineRunner.js:179` (`dropped`)
- A running seasonal step whose need stops holding (the Festival ends under a star step) is set aside fail-free — its clocks stopped, its items kept — and reclaimed where it stood when the need holds again. `src/systems/questlines/qlLineRunner.js:161` (`keepSeason`)
- Any quest can be abandoned and reclaimed: abandoning shelves it (clocks stopped, items kept) rather than deleting it; the journal's Abandon is refused only where the machine's refusal ladder says so (for example a raid's quest). `src/systems/quest/machine.js:1634` (`shelveQuest`); `src/scenes/quest/questBridge.js:797` (`canAbandon`)
- A quest can also be restarted from its first step (the journal's Restart), which throws the old copy away and starts a fresh one. `src/scenes/quest/questBridge.js:801` (`restart`)
- A classic quest whose deadline runs out ends as its script directs — with no reward, and with whatever standing loss the script itself names; online the deadline can only run out in played time (see clocks above). `src/systems/quest/clock.js:152` (`ONLINE_DELAY_SECONDS`)

## Gates
- Questlines open on their `opens` law: character level (2–12 by line), a finished earlier line (the Cycle chains Burning Doors → Covens' Hoard → Weir Road), online play, a registered account (never a guest) for every witnessed line and The Court Crafter, and the Starfall event for The Thirteenth of Frostfall. `src/systems/questlines/qlLines.js:256` (`lineOpens`)
- Steps gate individually on feature switches: Loot rarity (Smith's Secrets), Naval Combat (Harbormaster's Word), The King's Gambit (A Seat at the Table's second step), the revenant Renown (The Bay's Troubles' second step), the Starfall (two Thirteenth steps). `src/systems/questlines/qlLines.js:150` (`opens`); `src/systems/questlines/qlLines.js:125` (`QLTF01`)
- The main quest and the curse have no gate: both start at character creation. `src/scenes/quest/questBridge.js:201` (`GAME_START_QUESTS`)
- Two Mages Guild quests gate on guild standing online (10 and 20). `src/systems/quest/onlineRepute.js:13` (`ONLINE_REPUTE_GATES`)

## Online vs offline
- Waits: offline, quest clocks run on DFU's single clock (Brisienna's letter really takes its 7–14 days); online, delays are cut to about two real minutes and deadlines run on played time only, never expiring while away. `src/systems/quest/clock.js:152` (`ONLINE_DELAY_SECONDS`); `src/systems/quest/clock.js:97` (`PLAYED_STEP_MAX_SECONDS`)
- The endgame questlines (Cycle, Hour, Coil, Festival, Court Crafter) do not exist offline at all: their witnesses are the account service's receipts, so they open online only, most for registered accounts only. `src/systems/questlines/qlLines.js:61` (`opens`); `src/net/questlineClaims.js:39` (`QL_SERVICE_LINES`)
- The Curse of Daggerfall: offline its wraiths and ghosts are your quest's foes alone; online they are shared world foes in the street for everyone present. `src/scenes/quest/questFoeHost.js:390` (`isPrivateQuestFoe`)
- "Guard the Guild": offline the watch is the script's midnight–03:00; online it is an arrival-based window (opens 10 game minutes after you reach the hall, stands one game hour). `src/systems/quest/onlineGuard.js:28` (`ONLINE_GUARD_WINDOWS`)
- Two Mages Guild quests are standing-gated online only; offline the guild pool is DFU's. `src/systems/quest/onlineRepute.js:13` (`ONLINE_REPUTE_GATES`)
- Quest sharing exists online (party copies kept in step); the main quest and questline steps are excluded in both modes of play. `src/systems/quest/questShare.js:106` (`prepareShareData`)

## Open questions
- Per-step buildings for the questline quests: not found in source as a fixed list — each step's script names Place resources the quest machine resolves per character (a giver's hall, a dungeon, a town), so there is no static "buildings needed" table to cite; the lines' givers and needs above are what the registry itself fixes.
- The introduction lines' quest rewards are their scripts' own (the sheet pay profiles above govern how outcomes pay); the service pays the introductions nothing, and the source does not summarise a per-introduction reward beyond that.
- Whether a guest can hold the non-witnessed introduction lines (Smith's Secrets, Harbormaster's Word, Merchant's Road, Seat at the Table) offline indefinitely: their `opens` carry no account gate in the registry; the source does not say more.
