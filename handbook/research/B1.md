## Player-facing summary
- You can play at once as a **guest**: the game opens a guest account on this device and gives you a generated two-word name (a first name and a surname, drawn from the game's own name banks). `server-account/src/accounts.js:213` (`createGuest`); `server-account/src/guestName.js:130` (`guestName`)
- A guest is not a lesser character in the world, but almost nothing a guest does is *kept* by the account service: no cloud saves, no letters, no guild, no Marks (silver), no home, no professions Stores, and no recorded deeds (duels, gate kills, raid defences, Arena bouts, festival cakes all come back "not recorded" for a guest). Registering fixes all of that. Citations in Rules below.
- **Registering is an upgrade in place, not a new account**: your guest row gains a username and password, and keeps its id, its saves and everything hanging off them. `server-account/src/accounts.js:777` (`register`)
- A username is one word, 3–24 characters, starting with a letter, and can never be an email address; a password is 8–200 characters. `src/net/handleShape.js:66` (`HANDLE_RE`); `src/net/accountClient.js:84` (`PASSWORD_MIN_LEN`)
- There is no email reset. Email is completely optional and nothing is gated behind it. Your way back in is a **set of eight recovery codes**, shown exactly once at registration; each code works once, and the profile can mint a fresh set. `server-account/src/accounts.js:968` (`setEmail`); `server-account/src/password.js:342` (`RECOVERY_SET_SIZE`)
- The interface skin is your choice, online too: **Enhanced Plus** (the default) or **Classic**, with two extra looks — GrimoireUI over Classic, and DFO Grimoire over Enhanced Plus — chosen on the **Overhauls** page. `src/systems/uiSkin.js:39` (`SKIN_NAMES`); `src/systems/overhauls.js:145` (`OVERHAUL_PANELS` UI panel)
- Swinging a weapon defaults to **Click or Hold**, not the classic drag (Gesture): a click swings, holding the button keeps swinging. That is a port default, not an online force — the lane's only forced DFU setting is AllowMagicRepairs, so a player's own swing-mode choice stands online too. `src/systems/settings/settings.js:382` (`PORT_DEFAULTS`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:178` (`ONLINE_FORCED_SETTINGS`)
- The **Weapon technique** key defaults to the mouse's back side button (Mouse3); on a phone it is the **Tech** hold button; on a gamepad it has no default row and is bound in Controls. `src/systems/inputActions.js:410` (`DEFAULT_BINDINGS`); `src/ui/touch/touchButtons.js:48`
- Online, the room's rules are forced and your own settings file is not rewritten to match: magic repairs are always allowed, a named set of world mods and feature switches are always on (or, for medium dungeons, off), and cheat/debug URL flags are dropped at boot. Your purely local choices (skin, HUD layout, dials, survival tier, most mods) stay yours. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:178` (`ONLINE_FORCED_SETTINGS`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:308` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS`)
- Settings live on one screen, **Settings**, with nine tabs (Graphics, Gameplay, Combat, World, Interface, Audio, Controls, Accessibility, Mods & files); key bindings are rebindable under Settings › Controls › Key bindings, and every action has a primary and a secondary (gamepad) binding. `src/ui/settings/settingsMap.js:31` (`CATEGORIES`); `src/systems/inputActions.js:658` (`DEFAULT_SECONDARY_BINDINGS`)

## Rules and numbers

### The guest account
- First contact creates a real account row: an id, a generated guest name, and a session for this device; the row's handle stays null until the player registers. `server-account/src/accounts.js:213` (`createGuest`)
- An account's kind is decided by one test: it has a handle (`linked`) or it does not (`guest`). `server-account/src/accounts.js:123` (`accountKind`)
- A guest name always carries exactly one space; a chosen handle never carries one, so the two name spaces cannot overlap and a guest can never be mistaken for, or assigned, somebody's username. `src/net/handleShape.js:58` (`GUEST_NAME_RE`); `src/net/handleShape.js:66` (`HANDLE_RE`)
- The guest name is drawn uniformly from the game's own name banks (the same banks the chargen wizard and townspeople use), two parts for the first name and two for the surname. `server-account/src/guestName.js:89` (`drawName`)
- A guest's identity lives in the device's stored session secret (32 bytes of CSPRNG; only its SHA-256 hash is kept by the service). A guest row is, in the service's own words, "one storage clear from gone". `server-account/src/accounts.js:60` (`SECRET_BYTES`); `server-account/src/accounts.js:571` (`buyInsignia`)
- A session may sit unused for a year (SESSION_IDLE_S = 365 days) before a sweep may take it, guest or registered. `server-account/src/accounts.js:64` (`SESSION_IDLE_S`)
- Even signing in as a guest requires agreeing to the current Terms of Service and Privacy Policy versions: the guest route and the register route both refuse first (`terms-unaccepted` / `terms-stale`). `server-account/src/accounts.js:634` (`legalRefusal`)
- A guest *can* be muted or banned like any account row: a guest is a real row, so a mute is a flag on it, not on a stranger. `server-account/migrations/0001_accounts.sql:41`
- Layer (this subsection and the two that follow): the **account service** (`server-account/src`) — these are server-side rules enforced for every online session, not client settings and not lane rules; the online lane never touches account kind. Live online because the account service is the only place a session exists. `server-account/src/accounts.js:123` (`accountKind`)

### Registration
- Registering upgrades the guest row this session owns, in place: same id, same saves; it is not a new account. `server-account/src/accounts.js:777` (`register`)
- On a device with no session, the account card first opens a guest row, *keeps* that session, and then upgrades it — so a failed attempt (e.g. `handle-taken`) never strands the row or opens a second one. `src/ui/accountFlow.js:593` (`doRegister`)
- Username shape: one word, 3–24 characters, must start with a letter, no whitespace ever. `src/net/handleShape.js:66` (`HANDLE_RE`); the form's hint at `src/ui/accountFlow.js:88` (`FIELD_SPEC.handle`)
- A username can never be an email address (other players see it); refused `handle-email` at the form and at the service. Refused `handle-taken` if the name is held — the unique index, not a pre-check, is the authority. `server-account/src/accounts.js:597` (`handleRefusal`); `server-account/src/accounts.js:777` (`register`)
- A username that reads as a guest's two-word name is refused by shape, so the two spaces can never overlap. `server-account/src/accounts.js:602` (`handleRefusal`)
- Password: at least 8 and at most 200 characters (counted in characters, not bytes); it is never trimmed — leading/trailing spaces are the player's to choose. `server-account/src/password.js:111` (`PASSWORD_MIN`); `server-account/src/password.js:112` (`PASSWORD_MAX`); `src/net/accountClient.js:84` (`PASSWORD_MIN_LEN`); `src/ui/accountFlow.js:564` (`pw`)
- Registering asks the player to tick both the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy; only registering asks — signing in to an existing account does not ask again. `src/ui/accountFlow.js:106` (`AGREEMENTS`); `src/ui/accountFlow.js:106` (`AGREEMENTS`)
- Signing in: username + password mints this device its own session; a wrong name costs exactly what a wrong password costs (the form is not a username oracle), and a banned account is told so only after the password checks out. `server-account/src/accounts.js:825` (`login`)
- Failed sign-ins are throttled: 10 failures per 15 minutes per username (`LOGIN_MAX`, `LOGIN_WINDOW_S`); a successful sign-in forgives the count. `server-account/src/accounts.js:654` (`LOGIN_MAX`); `server-account/src/accounts.js:655` (`LOGIN_WINDOW_S`)
- Every authenticated call is also bounded per account: 240 calls a minute (`ACCOUNT_MAX`, `ACCOUNT_WINDOW_S`) — a ceiling on abuse, not a pacer. `server-account/src/accounts.js:672` (`ACCOUNT_MAX`)
- Email is completely optional, is never required, and an account works forever without one; setting one only stores the address (max 254 characters, address-shaped). `server-account/src/accounts.js:968` (`setEmail`)
- Changing the password from inside a session requires the old password, signs out every *other* device, and keeps this one signed in. `server-account/src/accounts.js:904` (`changePassword`)
- Signing out is per-device by default; signing out everywhere is a separate, explicit choice. A session the service cannot be told about still expires on its own after the idle year. `src/ui/accountFlow.js:735` (`signOut`); `server-account/src/accounts.js:64` (`SESSION_IDLE_S`)
- Layer (this subsection, and Recovery codes below — the same layer): the **account service** decides (handle shape, throttles, code sets are all service-side); the port client only mirrors the shapes for its form's local refusals. No lane or mod involvement — live online because registration *is* the service. `server-account/src/accounts.js:777` (`register`); `server-account/src/password.js:342` (`RECOVERY_SET_SIZE`)

### Recovery codes
- Registration answers a **set of eight recovery codes** (`RECOVERY_SET_SIZE = 8`). That answer is the only time they are ever readable; the service keeps only their hashes. `server-account/src/password.js:342` (`RECOVERY_SET_SIZE`); `server-account/src/accounts.js:777` (`register`)
- One code's shape: four groups of five characters (20 characters, 100 bits), from Crockford's alphabet (no I, L, O or U), shown hyphenated. `server-account/src/password.js:259` (`CODE_GROUPS`); `server-account/src/password.js:260` (`CODE_GROUP_LEN`); `server-account/src/password.js:256` (`CODE_ALPHABET`); `server-account/src/password.js:263` (`mintRecoveryCode`)
- Typing a code is forgiving in exactly Crockford's ways: case, dashes and spaces are ignored, O reads as 0, I and L read as 1; nothing else is folded. `server-account/src/password.js:310` (`codeForHashing`)
- Each code works once and is spent alone: recovering with one leaves the set's others working, and the answer says how many are left (`codesLeft`). Spending your *last* code — or an old single code from before the set existed — mints a fresh set in its place, so a player is never left with no way back in. `server-account/src/accounts.js:866` (`recover`)
- Recovering sets a new password and **signs every device out, including this one**; the recovering device is then given a fresh session. `server-account/src/accounts.js:866` (`recover`); the card's own warning at `src/ui/enhanced/enhancedAccount.js:124`
- A new set can be minted from the profile ("New recovery codes"), but it requires the password *and* one of the current codes; the old set dies with the write, and no device is signed out. `server-account/src/accounts.js:946` (`newRecoveryCodes`); `src/ui/accountFlow.js:683` (`doNewCodes`)
- On the card, the codes are a stage of their own: the only way off it is pressing the acknowledgement ("I have written it down"); the codes are held in memory only, never written to storage, and the card offers "Save as a file" (`dfo-recovery-codes-<username>.txt`). `src/ui/accountFlow.js:20` area (the flow's header law); `src/ui/enhanced/enhancedAccount.js:127` (the `code` stage's tag, "Write these down"); `src/ui/enhanced/enhancedAccount.js:134` (its blurb)
