## Player-facing summary
- The Controls screen is the authority for a player's current bindings. The defaults below come from DFO's `DEFAULT_BINDINGS`; a saved/rebound key can differ.
- Chat supports public, party, guild, local and opt-in channels, plus utility and moderation commands. `/help`, `/lang`, `/emotes`, and `/breach help` expose context-sensitive lists.
- Many limits are account-wide. UTC daily caps, rolling service limits, world clocks, and pair-scoped Arena limits are different kinds of rules.
- The old caps page is based on `4bba28c6c`; source rules were rechecked at `0df2d87eb`. Preserve warnings where a schedule is not a personal cap.

### Chat commands and channels
- Channel commands: `/world` or `/g`; `/region` or `/r`; `/lc`; `/party` or `/p`; `/guild` or `/gu`; `/local`, `/l`, `/say` or `/s`; `/ooc`; `/trade` or `/tr`; `/lfg`. Trade and LFG require joining; speaking there joins the channel. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:38-51` (port); `src/net/chat.js:104-122` (port))
- Chat utilities include `/lang [code|off]`, `/roll` or `/dice`, `/me`, `/emotes`, gestures (`/wave`, `/bow`, `/nod`, etc.), `/ready`, `/leader`, `/travel`, `/unstuck [cancel]`, `/mentor`, `/mail` or `/mailbox`, `/bug`, `/breach`, `/find` or `/who`, and `/search [name]`. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:201-229,237-241,295-303,327-332` (port))
- Safety commands include `/block`, `/unblock`, `/blocked`, and `/report <name> [harassment|hate|sexual|threat|spam][: note]`. `/bug` opens the private report form; `/report` sends a player/conduct report. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:165-171,201-223` (port); `src/net/safety/safetyLaw.js:1-20` (port))
- `/breach help` lists Elite Breach commands. `/event` is a staff live-event tool and not an ordinary player command. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:174-186,228-241` (port))
- `/roll` supports dice expressions; it accepts at most 10 dice of up to 1,000 sides. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:31,306` (port); `src/net/dice.js:13-20` (port))
- The nearby chat range is 60 metres; the local channel is also the in-character channel. (`src/net/chat.js:138-151` (port))
- `/help` lists commands, `/lang` lists language channels, and `/emotes` lists gestures. The parser refuses unknown slash commands instead of broadcasting them as text. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:201-229,263-303,327-332` (port))

### Caps, timers and resets (reverified from source; counts are not all player earning caps)
- Renown service: at most 5,000 XP per report, 15,000 XP per account per clock hour; normal reports are no more frequent than once per 60 seconds. Court-writ credit uses a separate path. (`src/net/renown.js:254-280` (port); `server-account/src/renownTracks.js:1-20` (port; account-hour enforcement))
- Profession service: 600 requests per account per rolling 3,600-second window. This is an operation-rate limit, not a daily harvest cap. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:266-297` (port))
- Marks service: 120 operations per account per rolling 3,600 seconds; combat Marks share a 150/account/UTC-day cap; Bank of the Empire exchanges at most 300 Marks/account/day, at 8 gold per Mark. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:96-109` (port))
- Marks account/guild balance ceiling: 10,000,000. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:39-40` (port))
- Market: 60 new posts and 120 other operations per account per rolling hour; at most 30 active listings and 20 active orders. Listings last 72 hours; orders last seven days. (`src/net/marketLaw.js:31-47` (port))
- Gathering node slots reset at 00:00 UTC; each slot is once per character per day. Bantha has 48 tree slots per pixel/day. No general daily harvest counter was found. (`src/net/nodeLaw.js:125,147-153,391-396` (port))
- Rare hides cap at 3/account/day; unconfirmed dungeon vein claims cap at 4/account/day; Court writs cap at 3/account/day. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:272,579,1106` (port))
- Motherlodes: 3 world lodes per UTC day, at most 20 strikers each; an account gets its Marks reward once/day. (`src/net/motherlodeLaw.js:38-58` (port); `server-account/src/motherlodes.js:1-20` (port))
- Marks from gathering finds cap at 30/account/day; corpse/pile/search finds combine to 20/account/day. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:75-85` (port))
- Festival Soul Cakes cap at 120 found/account/UTC day, with 1,500 find requests/day; Starfall claims cap at 9 stars/account/day, and those rewards also share the combat Marks ceiling. Event dates are configuration data and can change; do not hard-code 2026 festival dates as a standing rule. (`src/net/festivalLaw.js:24-40,72-73` (port); `server-account/src/stars.js:50,81-82` (port); `src/net/starfall/starfallLaw.js:35-40` (port))
- Dagon's Breach: one gate per two-real-hour game day; entry window is 20:00-22:00 game time; a registered account claims each gate at most once. (`src/net/gate/gateLaw.js:29-39,160` (port); `server-account/src/marks.js:1-20` (port; `claimGate`))
- Town raids share the two-real-hour game-day schedule; one receipt/account/raid, with no separate daily raid count found. The schedule has at most 32 raids per game day. (`src/net/raid/raidLaw.js:40,247-249` (port); `server-account/src/marks.js:1-20` (port; `claimRaid`))
- Sea serpent rises every 4 real hours, at dawn watch, and one account receipt is allowed per serpent. (`src/net/serpent/serpentLaw.js:8,39` (port); `server-account/src/marks.js:1-20` (port; `claimSerpent`))
- Bounty board posts 4 per town per game day; a character can hold 4 active bounties, which expire after one game day (1,440 classic minutes). (`src/systems/bounty/bountyBoard.js:28-42` (port))
- Arena seasons last eight weeks; the same rated pair has 5 counted matches/UTC day and 10/season, after which matches are unrated. (`src/net/arena/arenaLaw.js:82-97,421-429` (port))
- Same Royal Tourney pair: 3 counted bouts/UTC day; registration closes 10 minutes before battle. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:1122-1123,1312-1313` (port))
- Seat influence caps: 60 Watch/account/day, 900 gate influence/account/seat week, 400 Renown-derived influence/account/week, 2,000 total influence/account/seat week; Turning occurs Sunday at 18:00 UTC. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:230-245,254-284` (port))
- Elite dungeon respawns use 40 minutes; ordinary dungeon respawns 20 minutes. These are per foe/container world timers, not account cooldowns. (`src/systems/dungeon/dungeonRespawn.js:10-15` (port))
- Abyss Dungeon: one shared instance; 48-hour lifespan, 3-minute collapse after defeat, then 2-hour cooldown; census threshold is 2 accounts. (`src/net/superDungeon/sdLaw.js:25-39` (port))
- The wealth budget defaults to measure-only (`enforce=false`), so it is not an active gold-earning cap by default. (`bible/06-Systems/Integrity-Arc.md:1-20` (design record; live status not independently established))
- Legacy heir limit: 1/4 of carried gold, capped at 9,900; the inheritance is permanent family state, not a daily reset. (`src/systems/legacy/legacyLaw.js:23-28` (mod: Project Legacy))

## Gates
- The chat command parser and controls are available as soon as the player is in the online client; individual channels can require an account/guild/party or an explicit opt-in. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:38-51,165-229` (port); `src/net/chat.js:104-138` (port))
- Professional service, market, festival rewards and several account receipts require a registered account; exact per-service checks live in the account worker. (`server-account/src/profession.js:1-20`; `server-account/src/market.js:1-20`; `server-account/src/festival.js:1-20` (port))
- Daily and weekly timers roll on UTC service boundaries; world game-day schedules are separate. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:1-20` (port); `src/net/gate/gateLaw.js:1-20` (port))

## Online vs offline
- DFO Online uses `PORT_DEFAULTS` and its input action registry for online defaults; Daggerfall Unity's `settingsDefaults.js:1-20` is a later fallback only for settings not established by DFO. (`src/systems/settings/settings.js:54-63` (port); `src/systems/inputActions.js:231-417` (port))
- The chat channels, Marks service, account caps, Arena, Open Zone and Abyss Dungeon are port systems with no equivalent offline multiplayer/account rule. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:38-51`; `src/net/marksLaw.js:39-109`; `src/net/arena/arenaLaw.js:26-30`; `src/net/superDungeon/sdLaw.js:19-23` (port))
- Oblivion leveling's `Enabled` switch remains the player's setting, but newly created online characters use classic leveling; don't infer online levelling from the classic alternate's enable switch alone. (`src/systems/oblivionLeveling.js:1-20` (mod: Oblivion Remaster Like Leveling); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:484-511` (port))

(`src/characters/enemy/enemyAttack.js:1-20` `meleePass`; `src/ai/tactics.js:44,354`; `src/ai/foeBlows.js:25-43` (base + Enhanced AI mod))
