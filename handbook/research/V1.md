## Player-facing summary
- Online, a journey over land is travelled, never skipped: the travel map's trips are walked journeys (Travel Options), and the map's own fast-travel door refuses a land trip outright. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:342` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:306` (`ONLINE_LAND_TRAVEL_REFUSAL`)
- Walking is free. In the Overworld Travel View you pick a place and walk the real route; roads are favoured over tracks, and tracks over open ground. `src/systems/travel/travelRoute.js:45` (`ROUTE_COST`)
- Journey speed is a dial from ×5 to ×100; ×100 runs only on a road or track, and off them the cap is ×60. In a town's streets a routed leg runs at no more than ×5. `src/systems/travel/travelPace.js:29` (`PACE_STEPS`); `src/systems/travel/townWalk.js:41` (`TOWN_PACE_MAX`)
- The paid instant land trip online is a hired carriage: a driver stands at a walled city's gate, the service is labelled **Fast Travel**, and the fare is 1 gold per whole land-travel day plus 1 more, plus inn charges if you stop at inns. `src/systems/immersiveTravel.js:46` (`IT_SERVICE_LABEL`); `src/systems/modSettings.js:1356` (`General.DailyCarriageFee`)
- A Portal Stone costs 5 Welkynd Shards at any shopkeeper's service row, is consumed one use at a time, and holds a portal open for 30 seconds that anyone nearby can step through. `src/systems/gate/gateSpoils.js:104` (`PORTAL_STONE_SHARDS`); `src/systems/portalStone.js:40` (`PORTAL_OPEN_MS`)
- Horses are bought at a Stable (three offered per town per day, priced by their stats); your own cart or wagon is bought at a Wagon Yard and hauls cargo behind its horse team. `src/systems/merchantYards.js:82` (`STABLE_HORSES_OFFERED`); `src/systems/wagon/wagonKinds.js:40` (`WAGON_KINDS`)
- By sea there are three distinct things: passage on a friendly moored ship (25 gold per 24 map pixels, plus 25), a ship captain's fast-travel service from a dock (15 gold a day for the ship plus 10 a day for the captain, each by the day plus one), and your own ship from a Shipyard (hulls 4,000–200,000 gold). `src/systems/travel/travel.js:164` (`calculateTripCost`); `src/systems/modSettings.js:1362` (`ShipTravel.DailyShipCost`); `src/systems/comeSailAway/comeSailAwayHulls.js:7` (`HULL_PRICES`)
- Mages Guild teleport is a paid service only if its setting is on — and online that setting reads its shipped default, off; at rank 8 and above the guild's own teleport is free. `src/systems/modSettings.js:1283` (`Teleportation.EnablePaidTeleportation`); `src/ui/travel/travelMapOptions.js:188` (`TELEPORT_RANK_FREE`)
- Party travel is online-only: `/leader` travels to your party leader, `/travel` answers a leader's call; each traveller pays their own normal fast-trip fare, the trip takes no world time, and it cannot cross the Open Zone boundary or the Veil. `src/systems/party/partyTravel.js:433` (`chat arm: /leader, /travel`); `src/scenes/world.js:26972` (`wildTeleportRefused` / `veilRefused` party check)
- The Open Zone (the Wrothgarian Mountains) cannot be entered or left by fast travel, teleport or carriage; inside it, a fast trip costs 2,500–15,000 gold by the ring you arrive in, with a 10-minute cooldown, and travel speed is capped at ×40 on a road and ×20 off it. `src/systems/wild/wildZone.js:409` (`wildJourney`); `src/systems/wild/wildZone.js:63` (`WILD_TRAVEL_FEES`); `src/systems/travel/travelPace.js:68` (`WILD_PACE_CAP`)

## Rules and numbers

### Which layer runs online (read this first)
- Layer: **mod — Travel Options, forced by the room.** Online, the room forces Travel Options on, with Cautious and Inns trips player-controlled (walked) and ship travel only from ports, all at the mod's shipped defaults; this is why a map trip online is travelled and never Daggerfall's fast travel. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:350` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS['travel-options']`)
- Layer: **mod — Immersive Travel, forced by the room, held whole.** Online, the room forces Immersive Travel on and forces `General.DisableNormalTravel` off (the port's shipped default; the mod itself ships it on), so the travel map keeps Travel Options' walked journeys and the driver's trip is the paid fast travel over land. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:365` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS['immersive-travel']`)
- Layer: **mod — Immersive Travel, whole at shipped defaults.** Every Immersive Travel rule key except the map's looks (dot sizes, drawn roads/tracks, dock display) reads its shipped default online, so the fares and destination rules below are the room's, not the player's. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:504` (`ONLINE_WHOLE_MODS`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:515` (`ONLINE_WHOLE_MODS['immersive-travel']`)
- Layer: **mod — Basic Roads (roads-hazelnut), forced by the room.** The road network that routes are planned over is the room's: the mod is forced on with road-bed smoothing and rivers on, so every online player walks the same roads on the same ground. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:309` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS['roads-hazelnut']`)
- Layer: **port — the floor under the room's switches.** If a land trip ever reaches the fast-travel door online, it is refused with the words "Online, journeys over land cannot be skipped. Turn on Travel Options and try again." rather than teleported. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:306` (`ONLINE_LAND_TRAVEL_REFUSAL`); `src/scenes/world.js:16170` (`fastTravelTo` fork)
- Layer: **player's own mods — Horse Cart and Cargo, and Come Sail Away.** Both are listed as the player's own online: whose horse, wagon and boat stand where is the owner's, and peers only see them move; they change no shared rule. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:450` (`ONLINE_PLAYERS_OWN_MODS`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:471` (`horse-cart-and-cargo`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:478` (`come-sail-away`)

### Walking and the Overworld Travel View
- Layer: **port, running the Travel Options mod's journey** — the walked route, its route planner and its pace law are the port's own systems, live online because Travel Options is room-forced (above). `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:350` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS['travel-options']`)
- Walking on foot costs no fare; a route is planned over the streamed world, pixel by pixel. `src/systems/travel/travelRoute.js:45` (`ROUTE_COST`)
- The route planner scores a road step at 1, a track step at 1.6, and an open-ground step at 3.5 (times √2 on a diagonal), so it favours a longer road over a cross-country shortcut. `src/systems/travel/travelRoute.js:45` (`ROUTE_COST`)
- The travel-speed ladder is ×5, ×10, ×20, ×30, ×40, ×60, ×100 for both dials (journey speed, and the slowest rate while enemies are near). `src/systems/travel/travelPace.js:29` (`PACE_STEPS`)
- ×100 is the road's top alone; off a road or track the top is ×60, and leaving the road caps the running rate at ×60 without erasing the player's setting, which returns on the next road. `src/systems/travel/travelPace.js:33` (`ROAD_PACE_MAX`); `src/systems/travel/travelPace.js:34` (`OPEN_PACE_MAX`)
- The defaults are ×60 for journey speed and ×5 for the near-enemies dial. `src/systems/travel/travelPace.js:38` (`TRAVEL_PACE_DEFAULT`)
- A routed leg through a town's streets runs at no more than ×5, and goes through open gates; a gate shut for the night stops the journey there. `src/systems/travel/townWalk.js:41` (`TOWN_PACE_MAX`); `src/systems/travel/townWalk.js:24` (`gatePassage`)
- A Cautious journey can avoid an encounter with a chance of Luck + Stealth − 50, capped by the Cautious setting's maximum. `src/systems/travel/travelOptions.js:253` (`avoidEncounterChance`); `src/systems/travel/travelOptions.js:99` (`AVOID_ENCOUNTER_OFFSET`)
- Cautious travel's speed penalty is a percentage off the journey's speed: 20 by default, so ×0.8, with the setting ranging 5–40. `src/systems/travel/travelOptions.js:171` (`cautiousTravelMultiplier`); `src/systems/modSettings.js:1231` (`CautiousTravel.SpeedPenalty`)
- A chase is drawn on the Overworld within its band's reach: the bands are drawn 6 pixels about the viewer. `src/systems/travel/regionChase.js:7` (`BAND_REACH_PX` note); `src/systems/travel/regionChase.js:38` (`REGION_BANDS_NEAR_PX`)
- The classic travel-time walk underneath the journey counts a map pixel at 255 minutes on foot, 51 minutes by ship; a horse halves the land rate (modifier 128 of 256) and a cart takes three-quarters (192 of 256). `src/systems/travel/travel.js:71` (`travelPixelMinutes`); `src/systems/travel/travel.js:131` (`calculateTravelTime` transportModifier)
- Reckless (not Cautious) travel halves the calculated minutes. `src/systems/travel/travel.js:141` (`calculateTravelTime`)
