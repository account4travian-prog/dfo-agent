# E2: Economy
Commit: 0df2d87eb · Researcher: Codex · Date: 2026-10-21

## Player-facing summary
- Gold is your character's save currency; Marks are account-service currency. You can sell Marks for gold at the Bank of the Empire, but gold cannot buy Marks. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:8-18,39-44,99-100`; layer: port.)
- Marks come from witnessed activities, gathering finds and loot finds; several faucets have separate daily limits, while combat rewards share one 150-Mark daily cap. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:45-75,88-100`; layer: port.)
- The global Market uses Marks for ordinary sales and buy orders, with a 1% listing fee, 5% sale tax, courier fees for cross-region delivery and a seat-holder Tithe when a seat controls the board. (`src/net/marketLaw.js:7-16,31-55,103-122`; layer: port.)
- Use the Work tab for Court writs: deliver the requested material from your Stores for Marks and profession XP; you can fill up to three per day. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:50-55,75-78`; `src/net/professionLaw.js:125-126`; layer: port.)
- Online homes cost 5,000–250,000 gold, are priced by house footprint and town size, and you can own up to three. (`src/net/homeLaw.js:24-26,38-68`; layer: port.)
- Soul Cakes and Weir Keys are account-held event/late-game tokens; five Welkynd Shards buy a Portal Stone at a shop. They do not exchange through the gold or Marks ledger. (`src/net/festivalLaw.js:5-15`; `server-account/src/eg.js:10-20`; `src/systems/gate/gateSpoils.js:76-105`; layer: port.)

## Rules and numbers

### Gold and Marks
- Marks are account-held and minted or moved by server-account witnessed actions; the Bank only exchanges Marks into gold at 8 gold per Mark, up to 300 Marks exchanged per UTC day. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:8-18,39-44,99-100`; `server-account/src/marks.js:39-45,63-74`; layer: port.)
- Marks never buy Marks through gold; the Bank exchange is one-way, and the account's Marks balance and a guild Marks treasury each cap at 10,000,000. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:14-18,39-42`; layer: port.)
- Combat faucets (gate, raid, serpent, Elite Gate Redoubt and Starfall star) share one 150-Mark daily account cap. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:88-100` `MARKS_COMBAT`, `combatStrike`; layer: port.)
- A gate gives 50 Marks (100 during Daedric Incursion), a town raid gives 30, and a sea serpent gives 40 or 20 if you did not stand; each still draws on the shared combat cap. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:48-52,60-73,75-82`; layer: port.)
- Court writs pay 1.2 times requested units times the material's Marks value, up to three deliveries per day; filling a writ grants profession XP equal to twice its Marks pay. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:1113` `writPay`; `src/net/marksLaw.js:50-55,75-78`; `src/net/professionLaw.js:125-126` `writXp`; layer: port.)
- A Motherlode pays 10 Marks once per day; gathering finds have a 10% chance to give 2–5 Marks, capped at 30 Marks per day; loot finds have corpse/pile/search chances of 5%/12%/15%, give 1–4 Marks and cap at 20 per day. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:56-66,75-84`; layer: port.)
- A guild deed can pay its treasury 25 Marks when three members with at least seven days of tenure claim the same raid or gate; the deed faucet pays at most four times per guild per day. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:56-59,75-81`; layer: port.)
- A Weir Redoubt pays 20 Marks once per account per Arena week under the shared combat cap; Starfall's held star pays 30 under that same cap. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:69-74,84-98`; `src/net/eliteGates/egPay.js:20-23`; layer: port.)
- Marks operations are limited to 120 account acts per hour; a single Marks move to or from a guild treasury is capped at 1,000,000. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:101-109`; layer: port.)
- The interface may call Marks “silver,” but the source ledger and Market currency identifier are Marks (`marks`). (`src/net/marksLaw.js:189`; `src/net/marketLaw.js:126-135`; layer: port.)

### The Market
- Market access requires a registered account and all three `BOARD_OPEN`, `PROFESSIONS_OPEN` and `MARKS_OPEN` services open. (`server-account/src/market.js:9-19`; layer: port.)
- A listing lasts 72 hours; a buy order lasts seven days. One account can have 30 live listings and 20 live buy orders; a price ranges from 1 to 1,000,000 Marks, and a listing/order's total worth cannot exceed the 10,000,000 Marks balance cap. (`src/net/marketLaw.js:31-47`; layer: port.)
- Posting a listing burns a fee equal to 1% of its total value, rounded up and at least 1 Mark; cancelling does not refund that fee. (`src/net/marketLaw.js:103-105`; layer: port.)
- A sale pays 5% tax, rounded down; the seller receives price minus tax and any Tithe. (`src/net/marketLaw.js:49,106-122` `MARKET_TAX_PCT`, `saleTax`, `sellerGets`; layer: port.)
- A seat-holder's Market Tithe is a separate percentage, currently 0% absent a seat-held board; the cap is 10% for a palace and 15% for a crown. (`src/net/marketLaw.js:49-53,115-122`; `src/net/townSeatLaw.js:805-809` `TITHE_CAP`, `titheOf`; layer: port.)
- A local buyer takes a listing immediately; a buyer elsewhere pays courier cost and waits for delivery. Courier fees are at least 2 Marks, use 20 units per load and 1 Mark per 25 route pixels (rounded up); delivery takes 15 minutes plus 1 minute per 10 pixels begun. (`src/net/marketLaw.js:7-16,54-55,175-182` `courierFee`, `courierSeconds`; layer: port.)
- Each account may post at most 60 listings/orders per hour and make at most 120 other Market operations per hour. (`src/net/marketLaw.js:43-47`; layer: port.)
- Market goods may be listed in Marks or gold; buy orders remain in Marks. Goods purchased for gold are walled from Marks trades, crafting, stations and writs; the Market cannot turn gold into Marks. (`src/net/marketLaw.js:124-142`; layer: port.)

### Writs and guild contracts
- Court writs request material and quantity from the character's Stores and pay Marks based on the material's value; a character can fill three a UTC day. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:125-126`; `src/net/marksLaw.js:50-55,75-78`; `src/net/professionLaw.js:1113`; layer: port.)
- Guild writs can be active for seven days; each guild may have 20 live writs, and the guild Stores hold up to 50,000 units of one material. (`src/net/writLaw.js:27-47,78-85`; layer: port.)
- Guild contracts allow up to five open contracts, with pay capped at 50 Marks per deed and at most 500 deeds; no more than three contracts are paid per day. (`src/net/writLaw.js:153-165`; layer: port.)

### Homes and room rentals
- An account may own up to three homes; online home entry can be Private, Party, Public or Guild, defaulting to Private. (`src/net/homeLaw.js:24-29`; layer: port.)
- Home price is 300 gold per square metre times the town-size factor, rounded to the nearest 100 gold, with a 5,000-gold minimum and 250,000-gold maximum. (`src/net/homeLaw.js:38-68,88`; layer: port.)
- Selling a home returns 85% of its purchase price, truncated to whole gold. (`src/net/homeLaw.js:92-94` `HOME_SALE_SHARE`, `homeSaleRefund`; layer: port.)
- A room rental can be priced at 1–10,000 gold per room per day, for 1–30 days; a home can offer up to eight rooms and an account can hold three rentals. (`src/net/homeLaw.js:244-262`; layer: port.)
- Gold in a character's purse is save-local; the online Bank of the Empire is the shared account across regions. (`src/net/realm/realmGoldLaw.js:36-44`; `src/net/realm/realmGoldLaw.js:36-44` `EMPIRE_ACCOUNT_REGION`; layer: port.)

### Other currencies and tokens
- Soul Cakes are held by the account service and represented in the pack by a client-side item stack; the festival's cake accounting and spending are service-side. (`src/net/festivalLaw.js:5-15`; `server-account/src/festival.js:1-15`; layer: port.)
- Weir Keys are account-backed: the client refusal text says the player needs a Weir Key to enter the Spire again after rehearsing that day. (`src/net/accountClient.js:171`; `server-account/src/eg.js:10-20`; layer: port.)
- Five Welkynd Shards buy a Portal Stone at a shop; a shard is an item, not a service balance. (`src/systems/gate/gateSpoils.js:76-105`; `src/systems/portalStone.js:8-9,66-70,113-126`; layer: port.)
- Marks, save gold, Soul Cakes, Weir Keys and Welkynd Shards are distinct currencies or items; the sources define no general exchange path among them. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:8-18`; `src/net/realm/realmGoldLaw.js:36-44`; `src/net/festivalLaw.js:5-15`; `server-account/src/eg.js:10-20`; `src/systems/gate/gateSpoils.js:76-105`; layer: port.)

## Gates
- Marks, Market and account-backed currencies require a registered account; the Market also depends on the three service switches being open. (`server-account/src/marks.js:100-107`; `server-account/src/market.js:9-19`; layer: port.)
- The Market's tab is part of its board interface; Court writs are in the Work tab; bank exchange is at a Bank of the Empire counter; homes are bought at home/building interactions. (`src/net/marketLaw.js:7-16`; `src/net/professionLaw.js:8-15`; `src/net/marksLaw.js:14-18`; `src/net/homeLaw.js:24-68`; layer: port.)
- The research source set does not establish a single character-level gate for owning a home or using the Market; cite any additional level/quest conditions only after checking the current interaction gate. (`src/net/homeLaw.js:24-94`; `src/net/marketLaw.js:31-62`; layer: port.)

## Where it shows in the interface
- The Market's board has material and item sales, buy orders and order history; exact current labels are in the Market UI. (`src/net/marketLaw.js:68-88`; `src/ui/marketTab.js:2-15,36-40`; layer: port.)
- The pause/social interface exposes account Marks and guild treasury actions; the Bank of the Empire supplies the Marks-for-gold exchange. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:99-109`; `src/ui/bankWindow.js:23-27,55-67`; `src/ui/socialPanel.js:64-65`; layer: port.)
- Homes and rentals are reached through home interactions; entry policy can be Private, Party, Public or Guild. (`src/net/homeLaw.js:24-29,244-262`; `src/net/homeLaw.js:24-29,38-68`; layer: port.)

## Online vs offline
- Marks, the global Market, Court writs, online homes and account-held festival/Weir balances are DFO port systems; classic offline Daggerfall uses gold and regional banks, not these service-side Marks rules. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:8-18`; `src/net/marketLaw.js:7-16`; `src/net/realm/realmGoldLaw.js:36-44`; layer: port.)
- Online gold and Marks are intentionally separated: the client save owns gold, while the service owns Marks and account-level currencies. (`src/net/marksLaw.js:8-18,39-44`; `src/net/realm/realmGoldLaw.js:36-44`; layer: port.)
