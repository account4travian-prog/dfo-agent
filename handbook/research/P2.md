# P2: Renown and the Firmament
Commit: 0df2d87eb · Researcher: Atlas swarm (P2) · Date: 2026-10-21

## Player-facing summary
- Renown is DFO Online's own level, 1 to 50, kept per character by the account service and never written into your save; offline play earns no Renown at all. `src/net/identityToken.js:256` (`RENOWN_MAX`); `src/net/renownTracker.js:5`
- You earn Renown XP from kills, quests, defending a town in a raid, slaying the sea serpent, Arena wins, Starfall stars and court writs — every source pays three quarters of its full rate, and a foe or quest is read at most 3 levels above your Renown, so a high-level Daggerfall character cannot blow through the early Renown levels. `src/net/renown.js:189` (`RENOWN_RATE_PCT`); `src/net/renown.js:183` (`RENOWN_OVER_MAX`)
- A kill pays everyone who struck the foe in the last 30 seconds, not only the killing blow, and a party earns more per head: +10% per partymate in the room beyond the first, counting at most 8. `src/net/renownTracker.js:67` (`RENOWN_ASSIST_MS`); `src/net/renown.js:185` (`RENOWN_PARTY_BONUS_PCT`)
- Earning is bounded per account: at most 15,000 Renown XP in a clock hour across all your characters, and no single report carries more than 5,000. `src/net/renown.js:273` (`RENOWN_XP_HOUR_MAX`); `src/net/renown.js:272` (`RENOWN_XP_REPORT_MAX`)
- Online only, each Renown level past the first adds +3 maximum Health and +2 maximum Spell Points (at Renown 50: +147 Health, +98 Spell Points); the bonus is a layer put on when you come online and taken off when you leave, so the same character plays offline exactly as before. `src/net/renown.js:240` (`RENOWN_HP_PER_LEVEL`); `src/systems/renownLayer.js:73` (`setRenownLayer`)
- Renown unlocks things: elites in your own world at Renown 5, revenants and founding a player guild at Renown 10, and past Renown 15 the world's foes gradually hit harder and stand tougher against you. `src/systems/eliteFoes.js:79` (`ELITE_FOE_MIN_RENOWN`); `src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:116` (`REVENANT_MIN_RENOWN`); `src/systems/heroThreat.js:48` (`HERO_THREAT`)
- The Firmament is a passive star tree (518 stars) introduced by the questline Written in the Stars at character level 3: choose one of seven starts, then spend Stars — 2 for every Renown level past the first, 98 at Renown 50. `src/systems/questlines/qlLines.js:213` (`introFirmament`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:39` (`STARS_PER_RENOWN`)
- A second purse, Craft points (1 for every 8 profession ranks across your ten tracks, up to 125), can be spent on the tree's outer life ring alone — gathering, crafting, trade and travel stars — never on a combat star. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:44` (`CRAFT_RANKS_PER_POINT`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:13`
- The whole sky is budget-capped channel by channel: no build, however chosen, adds more than +30% to a weapon blow's general channels (+50% with its conditions), +30% to spell damage, +15 to an attribute, or takes more than 25% off a spell's price. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:191` (`BLOW_CAP`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:89` (`STAT_DEFS`)
- Taking stars back is free below Renown 10, then costs 10 Marks (silver) per star; a full respec — every star and your sign — costs 10,000 gold. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:356` (`REFUND_FREE_BELOW_RENOWN`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:361` (`FIRM_RESPEC_GOLD`)
- The Firmament's combat powers sleep offline, in a duel, in an Arena bout and while you mentor, and they never strengthen a blow or a spell against another player. `src/systems/firmamentPowers.js:59` (`firmamentAwake`); `src/systems/firmamentPowers.js:22`

## Rules and numbers

### Renown: the track and the curve
Layer: **port** — Renown is the port's own online system, kept by the account service; no mod in the online lane names it (the lane was audited in full: no entry in `ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS`, `ONLINE_WHOLE_MODS` or `ONLINE_PLAYERS_OWN_MODS` touches Renown or the Firmament). `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:25`
- Renown runs from level 1 to a cap of 50. `src/net/identityToken.js:256` (`RENOWN_MAX`)
- The track is per character and is kept by the account service, never in the character's save; the level is the service's alone — it derives the level from the character's XP total and signs it into the identity token. `src/net/renown.js:24`; `src/net/renown.js:104` (`renownForXp`)
- The total XP needed to be at a level follows one integer formula, `renownXpFor`: level 2 = 100, level 5 = 690, level 10 = 5,510, level 15 = 23,350, level 20 = 68,200, level 30 = 319,950, level 40 = 972,770, level 50 = 2,318,660. `src/net/renown.js:93` (`renownXpFor`)
- A track holds at most the level-50 total (2,318,660 XP); past it nothing more is kept and the bar reads full. `src/net/renown.js:101` (`RENOWN_XP_MAX`)
- An account holds at most 60 Renown tracks, one per character; a new character past that is refused a track. `src/net/renown.js:275` (`RENOWN_TRACKS_MAX`)
- The client reports what it earned at most once a minute, or once every five minutes while the service's answer would say nothing new. `src/net/renown.js:280` (`RENOWN_REPORT_MS`); `src/net/renown.js:285` (`RENOWN_COALESCE_MS`)

### Renown: the rate and the ceiling (every source)
Layer: **port** — one rate and one ceiling in `net/renown.js`, taken by every source below, on the client and on the service alike. `src/net/renown.js:195` (`renownRate`)
- Every source pays 75% of its full-rate XP, floored to a whole XP in this one place. `src/net/renown.js:189` (`RENOWN_RATE_PCT`)
- A foe or a quest is read at most 3 levels above the character's Renown: at Renown 1 a level-30 foe pays as a level-4 foe, and the ceiling rises one-for-one with Renown. `src/net/renown.js:183` (`RENOWN_OVER_MAX`); `src/net/renown.js:199` (`renownCeiling`)

### Renown XP: kills
Layer: **port** — paid by the player's own client through the tracker, at the moment a foe dies. `src/scenes/world.js:20915`
- A kill is worth 10 XP per foe level at the full rate (a level-3 foe pays 22 after the rate; a level-30 foe read at the ceiling pays 225 at high Renown). `src/net/renown.js:174` (`RENOWN_KILL_XP_PER_LEVEL`); `src/net/renown.js:202` (`renownKillXp`)
- A foe's level is read no higher than 30, whatever the foe. `src/net/renown.js:176` (`RENOWN_KILL_LEVEL_MAX`)
- The kill pays every player who struck the foe within the last 30 seconds, whoever struck the last blow — the rule that lets dungeon joiners earn from the host's foes. `src/net/renownTracker.js:67` (`RENOWN_ASSIST_MS`)
- A foe that never pays: the city watch (anyone's), and your own ally (for example a summon that turns). A kill the law calls murder earns nothing, and a scripted quest kill is no blow of yours. `src/net/renownTracker.js:78` (`neverPays`)

### Renown XP: the party bonus
Layer: **port** — applied to the kill's already-rated XP in the kill handler. `src/scenes/world.js:20915`
- Each partymate in the room beyond the first adds 10% to every kill's XP, for every member — the party earns more per head, never a share of one purse. `src/net/renown.js:185` (`RENOWN_PARTY_BONUS_PCT`); `src/net/renown.js:228` (`renownPartyXp`)
- The bonus counts at most 8 partymates present, and only partymates who actually fought the foe count toward it. `src/net/renown.js:187` (`RENOWN_PARTY_COUNT_MAX`); `src/scenes/world.js:20915`

### Renown XP: quests
Layer: **port** — paid when a quest completes, sized to the character as Daggerfall sizes the quest itself. `src/scenes/world.js:20922`
- A quest is worth 100 XP plus 40 per character level at the full rate — 75 and 30 per level after the rate — read against the same 3-over ceiling as foes. `src/net/renown.js:178` (`RENOWN_QUEST_XP_BASE`); `src/net/renown.js:179` (`RENOWN_QUEST_XP_PER_LEVEL`); `src/net/renown.js:206` (`renownQuestXp`)
- A quest's character level is read no higher than 30. `src/net/renown.js:181` (`RENOWN_QUEST_LEVEL_MAX`)

### Renown XP: raids, the serpent, Starfall, the Arena, writs
Layer: **port (account service)** — these are credited by the service from the relay's or the Arena's signed word, once per account per event, and charged against the same hourly bound as a report. `server-account/src/renownTracks.js:121` (`reportRenownXp`)
- A town defended (a raid cleansed, signed by the relay) is worth 3 quests at the ladder's top quest level, read against the ceiling: 585 XP at Renown 1, 1,395 at Renown 10, 2,925 at Renown 27 and up. `src/net/renown.js:214` (`RENOWN_RAID_QUESTS`); `src/net/renown.js:215` (`renownRaidXp`)
- A sea serpent slain is worth 6 quests on the same terms — twice a town defended. `src/net/renown.js:221` (`RENOWN_SERPENT_QUESTS`); `src/net/renown.js:222` (`renownSerpentXp`)
- A Starfall star recorded pays the character that fought it the same Renown as a raid (3 quests at the track's level before it), once per star per account. `server-account/src/stars.js:82`
- A won Arena ladder bout is worth 1 quest read at its tier's top level (tier levels run 3, 5, 7 … 21); a tier's champion bout is worth 2 quests, the Grand Champion 3, and a rated win over another player 1 quest read at level 30 — each read against the winner's Renown ceiling and credited once per account. `server-account/src/arena.js:94` (`ARENA_RENOWN_QUESTS`); `server-account/src/arena.js:92` (`ARENA_RENOWN_TIER_LEVEL`); `server-account/src/arena.js:100` (`arenaRenownXp`)
- Measured Arena pays at Renown 30: a tier-1 bout 165, a tier-10 bout 705, the Grand Champion 2,115, a rated players' win 975; at Renown 1 every bout past the Pit pays 195 and the Grand Champion 585. A loss, a draw and an unrated bout pay no Renown. `server-account/src/arena.js:83`
- A filled court writ pays Renown as well as Marks: 25 × tier × units ÷ 10 at the full rate, taken at the 75% rate (a tier-2 writ of 30 units pays 112). `src/net/professionLaw.js:1118` (`writRenown`)

### Renown: the service's bounds
Layer: **port (account service)** — the client asserts the XP, so the service bounds what it can ever mean. `src/net/renown.js:272`
- One report carries at most 5,000 XP. `src/net/renown.js:272` (`RENOWN_XP_REPORT_MAX`)
- An account earns at most 15,000 Renown XP in a clock hour, across all its characters — a second character is not a second allowance; XP past the bound in that hour is not kept or re-sent. `src/net/renown.js:273` (`RENOWN_XP_HOUR_MAX`)

- Elites and champions can stand in your own world from Renown 5. `src/systems/eliteFoes.js:79` (`ELITE_FOE_MIN_RENOWN`); `src/systems/eliteFoes.js:82` (`eliteRenownOpen`)
- Revenants are held until Renown 10: under it, none are made, stand, spread or mark. `src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:116` (`REVENANT_MIN_RENOWN`); `src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:259` (`revenantHeld`)
- Founding a player guild takes Renown 10 and 10,000 gold, checked against the founding character's own track. `src/net/guild/guildLaw.js:28` (`GUILD_FOUND_RENOWN`); `src/net/guild/guildLaw.js:27` (`GUILD_FOUND_GOLD`)
- Firmament refunds are free below Renown 10 (see Refunds below), and each Renown level past the first gives 2 Stars to spend in the Firmament. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:356` (`REFUND_FREE_BELOW_RENOWN`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:39` (`STARS_PER_RENOWN`)
- The hero's threat: past character level 10 and Renown 15 the world answers — each Renown step from 15 to 50 makes foes' blows land 1% harder and foes stand 0.5% tougher, compounding (character level adds its own, larger steps beside it). `src/systems/heroThreat.js:48` (`HERO_THREAT`)

### The Firmament: the two purses
Layer: **port (account service)** — the tree, its purses and every change are the account service's; the law file is read identically by the service and the client, and no client can print a point. `server-account/src/firmament.js:14`; `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:39`
- Stars: 2 for every Renown level past the first — 0 at Renown 1, 98 at Renown 50 — and a Star may be spent anywhere in the sky. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:39` (`STARS_PER_RENOWN`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:50` (`starsFor`)
- Craft points: 1 for every 8 profession ranks summed across the character's ten tracks (a track's rank counts at most 100), so ten Master tracks give 125. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:44` (`CRAFT_RANKS_PER_POINT`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:52` (`craftPointsFor`)
- A combat-ring star costs Stars; a life-ring star is paid from the Craft purse first, then from any Stars left over — an allocation fits when its combat stars cost no more than the Stars and all its stars together no more than both purses together. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:252` (`firmCostOf`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:258` (`fits`)
- The whole sky holds 518 stars; the two purses together (98 + 125 = 223 at most) buy well under half of it, so every character's sky is a choice. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:36`

- Choosing a start is free (it costs no point), and each sign's start gives a small raw gift of its own attributes (the Mage +2 Intelligence; the Shadow +1 Agility and +1 Intelligence; the Thief +2 Agility; the Steed +2 Speed; the Warrior +1 Strength and +1 Endurance; the Ritual +2 Willpower). `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:252` (`firmCostOf`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentTree.js:95` (`SKY`)
- The INNER SKY (out to radius 800) is combat: each sign's attribute spoke, the ring road at radius 400, seven combat clusters per sign — wheels, forks and chains of small stars round named notables — and a combat keystone at the end of the sign's own radial. `src/net/firmament/firmamentTree.js:73` (`FIRM_RADII`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentTree.js:95` (`SKY`)

### The Firmament: small stars are shares, key stars are raw

### The Firmament: the budget (caps per channel, and the blow caps)
Layer: **port** — every channel's sum over the whole sky is capped, and the blow's and the spell's channels are capped again as a group at the moment they are read; keystones pay their prices after the cap, so a price is never swallowed by it. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:89` (`STAT_DEFS`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:208` (`blowPct`)
- Any attribute (Strength, Intelligence, Willpower, Agility, Endurance, Speed, Luck): at most +15 from the whole sky (Personality likewise, as a life channel). `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:90`
- Weapon damage channels — all weapons, melee, bows, fists, short blades, long blades, axes, blunt weapons, and against the undead, daedra and animals: each at most +30%. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:92`
- Damage against unaware foes: at most +40%; damage while below half Health: at most +25%. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:103`
- Spell damage: at most +30%; saving throws against your spells: at most −10; a spell's price: at most 25% less (the general channel), casting speed at most +15%. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:106`; `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:108`
- What lands on you: blows at most 30% lighter (and at most 15% heavier as a price), spells and other harm the same; Armor Rating at most +10. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:111`
- Maximum Health and maximum Spell Points: at most +75 raw, and at most +15% by share; Health and Spell Point regeneration at most 1.0% of the maximum per round; a kill restores at most 6% Health or 6% Spell Points; evasion at most 10%; absorbing a Destruction spell at most 15%. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:114`; `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:119`
- A skill: at most +15 raw for a combat skill and +20 for a life skill, and at most +20% / +25% by share; resistance to an element (fire, frost, poison, shock, magic): at most +20; one school's spell price: at most 20% less. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:163`; `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:166`
- Life channels: carry at most +40% weight; profession yield at most +30%; profession experience at most +35%; a steadier herbalism hand or more fishing time at most +40%; travel fatigue and fall damage at most 40% less; reputation with every social group at most +10; speed under sail at most +15%. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:129`; `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:168`; `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:170`
- THE BLOW CAPS, read at the blow: the general channels of one blow (all-weapons, melee, its family, its foe-kind) together add at most +30% and take away at most −20%; with its conditions (an unaware foe, a wounded wielder) the whole blow is capped at +50%. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:191` (`BLOW_CAP`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:192` (`BLOW_FLOOR`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:194` (`BLOW_TOTAL_CAP`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:208` (`blowPct`)
- THE CAST CAP, read at the cast: a spell's price is reduced by at most 25% across all its channels together; a school's own channel counts only where every effect of the spell is that school's. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:196` (`CAST_COST_CAP`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:227` (`castCostPct`)

### The Firmament: allocating, refunds and the respec
Layer: **port (account service)** — a change names the whole tree the player wants; the service judges it against the purses it counts itself and charges once, under the change's own request id. `server-account/src/firmament.js:10`
- Every allocated star must be joined to your start through other allocated stars — there are no orphan stars; the window lights the shortest way to any star and pressing it allocates that whole way. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:289` (`treeFault`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:314` (`pathTo`)
- Taking a star back takes with it every star that only reached the start through it. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:303` (`refundTakes`)
- Refunds are free below Renown 10; from Renown 10 a refund costs 10 silver per star taken back — silver meaning Marks, the service's own currency, burnt in the change itself. - THE RESPEC: the whole sky back at once — every star and the sign, to choose again — for 10,000 gold, whatever the Renown, and no silver; it is paid from a realm character's record on the service, so a character with no realm record cannot respec. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:361` (`FIRM_RESPEC_GOLD`); `src/net/firmament/firmamentBook.js:189` (`respec`)
- A build can be shared as a code (`FIRM-<sky>-<stars>`) and loaded into another player's window; a code made under a different sky (stars added since) is refused, not misread. `src/net/firmament/firmamentLaw.js:498` (`buildCode`)
- The service allows at most 120 Firmament changes an hour per account. `server-account/src/firmament.js:44` (`FIRM_OPS_MAX`)

### The Firmament: when the sky acts, and when it sleeps
Layer: **port** — the tree is in force online alone, and its combat channels narrow further in refereed places. `src/systems/firmamentLive.js:49` (`firmamentInForce`)
