# H2: Combat for every playstyle
Commit: 0df2d87eb · Researcher: Atlas swarm (H2) · Date: 2026-10-21 · Online-layer re-check: 2026-10-21 (rules below state what runs ONLINE; where the base rule is not the online rule, the override is given and the change is noted)

## Player-facing summary
- Every melee swing is directional, and direction is a trade: the overhead StrikeDown hits +4 damage but −10 to hit, while StrikeUp is −4 damage and +10 to hit. `src/combat/playerWeapon.js:50` (`SWING_MODS`)
- A melee blow only lands on a foe whose centre is within 2.5 m of your eye, inside your view, with clear line of sight. `src/combat/playerWeapon.js:47` (`WEAPON_REACH`)
- Big foes telegraph: a shape is drawn on the ground and a glint rides the foe's body while it winds up. Step out of the shape before it lands and the blow misses entirely — and the foe is left overreached and taking +30% damage for about a second. `src/ai/tells.js:75` (`TELL.PUNISH_S`), `src/ai/tells.js:77` (`TELL.PUNISH_TAKEN`)
- Leaving the shape at the last moment (inside it 0.25 s before the landing, outside at the landing) is a perfect dodge: the punish window runs 1.5× longer. `src/ai/tells.js:78` (`TELL_LATE`), `src/ai/tells.js:79` (`TELL.PERFECT_WINDOW`)
- You can also break a wind-up: hits on a winding-up foe fill a poise meter instead of shoving it, and at full poise the foe staggers, helpless for 0.8–1.4 s and taking +25% damage. Blunt weapons weigh heaviest on poise; daggers least. `src/ai/tells.js:23` (`TELL.POISE_W`), `src/ai/tells.js:55` (`TELL.STAGGER_TAKEN`)
- Armour online does not make blows miss: under the combat overhaul the room forces on, armour reduces the damage of a blow that lands, by a percentage set by the armour's material and the blow's kind. `src/combat/pcaao.js:857` (`reduce`), `src/combat/pcaao.js:863` (`UNARMED_ROWS`)
- Shields are not a button, but a held shield is not just armour either: every blow that lands on you rolls a block for it (30–55% base by shield kind, bent by your stats), and a blocked blow is reduced by the shield's own, stronger reduction row — provided the shield beats the armour under it. `src/combat/pcaao.js:988` (`pcaaoShieldBlockChanceCalculation`), `src/combat/pcaao.js:1013` (`pcaaoCompareShieldToUnderArmor`)
- Bows shoot arrows that fly and can be dodged by movement; half the arrows that strike a foe survive in its pack, and arrows that hit walls stick and can be picked back up (up to 40 kept). `src/combat/arrowFlight.js:56` (`ARROW_LODGE_CHANCE`), `src/combat/arrowFlight.js:60` (`STUCK_ARROWS_MAX`)
- How long you held the draw matters: a snap shot (under 0.2 s) is −40 to hit and a short draw scales damage down, while holding past 5 s tires the shot away (down to × 0.25 at 9 s and beyond). `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrRealism.js:45` (`rrAdjustWeaponHitChanceMod`), `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrRealism.js:58` (`rrAdjustWeaponAttackDamage`)
- Spells cost spell points with a hard floor of 5 per cast, leave your hands 0.2 s after you start the cast, and you cannot start the next cast for 0.7 s (both scaled by your Speed and any cast-speed gear). `src/systems/spellcost.js:35` (`CAST_COST_FLOOR`), `src/combat/fpsSpellCasting.js:77` (`CAST_RECOVER_S`)
- Better weapons can carry a technique line — Volley on a bow, Whirlwind on a long blade, Haymaker on gauntlets — fired with its own key (hold to aim), costing fatigue, on a cooldown of 8–16 s, and always a multiplier on the normal damage formula, never flat damage. `src/combat/technique/techniqueRoster.js:96` (`TECHNIQUES`)

### Melee — swings, directions, reach, frames
- Melee reach is one flat number for every weapon: `DEFAULT_WEAPON_REACH` 2.25 m + `SPHERE_CAST_RADIUS` 0.25 m = `WEAPON_REACH` 2.5 m; nothing in the source writes a per-weapon Reach. `src/combat/playerWeapon.js:45` (`DEFAULT_WEAPON_REACH`), `src/combat/playerWeapon.js:47` (`WEAPON_REACH`), `src/combat/playerWeapon.js:371` (Reach comment)
- The hit test is: foe's centre within reach of the eye, inside the camera view, and clear line of sight through the level collider. `src/combat/playerWeapon.js:89` (`playerMeleeCanHit`)
- Swing direction modifiers (`SWING_MODS`, damage / to-hit): StrikeUp −4 / +10. `src/combat/playerWeapon.js:50` (`SWING_MODS`)
- Swing direction modifiers: StrikeDownRight −2 / +5. `src/combat/playerWeapon.js:50` (`SWING_MODS`)
- Swing direction modifiers: StrikeDownLeft +2 / −5. `src/combat/playerWeapon.js:50` (`SWING_MODS`)
- Swing direction modifiers: StrikeDown +4 / −10. `src/combat/playerWeapon.js:50` (`SWING_MODS`)
- Swing direction modifiers: StrikeLeft and StrikeRight 0 / 0; classic does not apply swing modifiers to unarmed attacks. `src/combat/playerWeapon.js:50` (`SWING_MODS`)
- Click-mode attacks pick a random direction from UpRight, Left, Right, DownLeft, Down, DownRight; gesture swings are read from the drag in 15° radial sections and can also produce Up and DownLeft/Down/DownRight. `src/characters/weaponStates.js:162` (`ATTACK_THRESHOLD`), `src/combat/playerWeapon.js:50` (`SWING_MODS`)
- A gesture must travel `ATTACK_THRESHOLD` 0.05 (share of the longest screen dimension) to register as a swing, and a gesture is tracked for at most `MAX_GESTURE_SECONDS` 1.0 s. `src/characters/weaponStates.js:162` (`ATTACK_THRESHOLD`)
- Every melee strike animation is 5 frames (Idle 1); damage lands on hit frame 2, i.e. 40% through the strike (`MELEE_HIT_SHARE`). `src/characters/weaponStates.js:175` (`MELEE_NUM_FRAMES`), `src/characters/weaponStates.js:178` (`HIT_FRAME_MELEE`), `src/characters/weaponStates.js:182` (`MELEE_HIT_SHARE`)
- Swing frame time is read through the port's swing law — `SWING_BASE_FRAME` × tempo × handling, bounded per frame — but online the Speed it reads is not your live Speed: RRI's weaponBalance arm answers first and converts it (next bullet). The base frame is (3 × (115 − 50)) / 980 ≈ 0.199 s, so an unmodified 5-frame strike at effective Speed 50 takes ≈ 1.0 s. `src/characters/weaponStates.js:45` (`SWING_BASE_FRAME`), `src/characters/weaponStates.js:102` (`swingFrameSeconds`), `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrInstall.js:131` (Items arm asked first)
- ONLINE CHANGE — the effective Speed a held weapon swings at: live Speed minus `min(liveSpeed, 98) × ((150 − Strength)% × base weight × 3.4) / 90`, truncated — a heavier weapon and a weaker arm swing slower, and the law's own heft term is NOT applied on top (the weight is already counted). Bare hands read live Speed plain. Layer: RRI mod, live online because weaponBalance ships on and RRI is the room's whole (see the layer section). `src/systems/roleplayRealismItems/rriRealism.js:232` (`rriMeleeWeaponAnimTime`), `src/systems/roleplayRealismItems/rriRealism.js:22` (`SPEED_REDUCTION_FACTOR`), `src/systems/roleplayRealismItems/rriKits.js:47` (`rriAnimTimeOverride`)
- Roleplay & Realism's own weaponSpeed arm (a Speed/Strength blend: two hands 50/50, a dagger or short blade 90/10, anything else 80/20) is forced on online but never answers while RRI's arm does — the registration asks Items first and only falls through when it declines. `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrRealism.js:112` (`rrMeleeWeaponAnimTime`), `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrInstall.js:131` (registration order)
- Tempo is a straight line pivoted on Speed 50: frame time × 1.4 at Speed 0, × 0.6 at Speed 100 (Speed 100 swings 1.67× as often as Speed 50) — online this curve reads the adjusted Speed above, not the raw stat. `src/characters/weaponStates.js:49` (`SWING_TEMPO_AT_0`), `src/characters/weaponStates.js:54` (`swingTempo`)
- Heft is the port's law for the unmodded path only: weapon weight past a free 2.5 kg costs `SWING_HEFT_PER_KG` 0.045 (4.5%) of frame time per effective kg, capped at +35%, the weight felt as (150 − Strength)% of base. Online it does not apply to a held weapon — RRI's conversion above replaces it. `src/characters/weaponStates.js:61` (`SWING_HEFT_PER_KG`), `src/characters/weaponStates.js:67` (`swingHeft`)
- Handling by kind: dagger, bare hands and claws × 0.9; staff × 0.97; long blades × 1.0; mace × 1.03; flail and battle axe × 1.05; warhammer × 1.06. `src/characters/weaponStates.js:78` (`SWING_HANDLING`)
- A two-handed weapon multiplies its handling by `SWING_TWO_HANDED` × 1.06 on top of its kind. `src/characters/weaponStates.js:88` (`SWING_TWO_HANDED`)
- Every weapon that swings with the Short Blade skill handles as a dagger (× 0.9), whatever sprite draws it — tanto, shortsword and wakizashi included. `src/characters/weaponStates.js:95` (`SWING_SHORT_BLADE`)
- Named swing-pace modifiers (mastery, martial paths) can quicken a swing by at most `SWING_PACE_MOST` 50% combined, and no swing frame goes under the floor in `swingFrameSeconds`. `src/characters/weaponStates.js:102` (`swingFrameSeconds`)
- You fight with either hand: H switches hands, and the hand used is the whole combat identity of the swing (weapon, skill tallied, hit sound). `src/systems/inputActions.js:255` (`SwitchHand` default KeyH)
- The off hand's blow is a share of the rolled damage set by the two-hands law (`handsLaw.js`), applied before wear, HUD and listeners see it. `src/combat/formulas.js:597` (`calculateAttackDamage`, `blowShare`)

### Melee — the hit roll and damage
- ONLINE CHANGE — the hit roll is PCAAO's, not the classic formula's: chance to hit starts at ceil(the attacker's weapon skill × 1.5) for EVERY attacker, player or foe (the classic core started at the skill itself). The final chance is still clamped to 3–97 before a d100 roll. Layer: PCAAO mod, forced on whole online. `src/combat/pcaao.js:1092` (`pcaaoAttackDamage`), `src/combat/pcaao.js:409` (`pcaaoSuccessfulHit`), `src/combat/pcaao.js:407` (`PCAAO_HIT_CHANCE_MIN`)

- A weapon attack then adds the weapon's material term (next bullet), and a bow's is adjusted by draw time (see Archery); the struck part, armour, stat, skill and adjustment terms are added inside `pcaaoSuccessfulHit`. `src/combat/pcaao.js:1151` (weapon to-hit in `pcaaoAttackDamage`), `src/combat/pcaao.js:409` (`pcaaoSuccessfulHit`)
- ONLINE CHANGE — weapon material to hit: material modifier × 2 + 2, not the classic × 10 (and not Roleplay & Realism's × 3, whose registration the PCAAO core bypasses). On the material ladder −1, 0, 0, 1, 2, 3, 3, 4, 5, 6 (materials 0–9) that is +0, +2, +2, +4, +6, +8, +8, +10, +12, +14 — a daedric weapon gives +14, not +60. `src/combat/pcaao.js:331` (`pcaaoWeaponToHit`), `src/combat/formulas.js:169` (`WEAPON_MATERIAL_MODIFIER`)
- ONLINE CHANGE — a weapon below the metal a foe needs no longer deals 0: with soft material requirements on, damage is multiplied by 1 − min(shortfall × 0.2, 0.9) — shortfall 1 × 0.8, 2 × 0.6, 3 × 0.4, 4 × 0.2, 5 or more × 0.1 — and a player whose blow falls to 45% or less of itself is told "This Weapon Is Not Very Effective Against This Creature." ### The spellbook and the spellmaker
- Your spells live in the spellbook window; the `CastSpell` action is labelled "Spellbook" in the controls list, readies the chosen spell, and the cast itself is the attack click. `src/systems/inputActions.js:545` (Magic action labels), `src/systems/inputActions.js:642` (cast-is-the-attack-click note)

### Weapon techniques — how lines roll
- A technique is a line on a weapon (`{ id: 'technique', param, value }`), rolled at a loot door's end like every affix, and read by the card, the reforge and the hone the same way. `src/combat/technique/techniqueRoster.js:54` (`TECHNIQUE_BANDS`)
- The seven families, by the skill the piece swings with: archery, thunderlock, longBlade, shortBlade, axe, blunt, handToHand; a bare-handed fighter's technique rides the worn Gauntlets (template 103). `src/combat/technique/techniqueRoster.js:26` (`GAUNTLETS_TEMPLATE`), `src/combat/technique/techniqueRoster.js:41` (`techniqueFamily`)
- Roll chance per mille at a loot door, by the piece's tier: magic 120 (12%), rare 300 (30%), legendary 450 (45%) — one roll at the door's end. `src/combat/technique/techniqueRoster.js:59` (`TECHNIQUE_PER_MILLE`)
- The line's value band, `+N%` on the technique's own multiplier, by tier: magic +5–15%, rare +15–30%, legendary +30–50%. `src/combat/technique/techniqueRoster.js:54` (`TECHNIQUE_BANDS`)
- The value acts as multiplier × (1 + value / 100) (`techniqueMult`); every technique number is a multiplier on the normal `CalculateAttackDamage` for the weapon in hand — a miss is still a miss. Online, the "normal formula" a technique multiplies is the PCAAO core's damage, not the classic core's. `src/combat/technique/techniqueRoster.js:70` (`techniqueMult`), `src/combat/pcaao.js:1047` (`pcaaoAttackDamage`)
- A technique never moves a body faster than `TECHNIQUE_MAX_SPEED` 12 m/s. `src/combat/technique/techniqueRoster.js:94` (`TECHNIQUE_MAX_SPEED`)
- A leap or a dash technique launches from the ground alone, except one marked airborne (the Flying Kick), which also goes from the air. `src/combat/technique/techniqueRoster.js:90` (mechanics comment)
- Technique aiming: rain and pierce aim a place or a lane, leap and shadowstep aim a foe or the ground, the swing techniques need no aim; aimed techniques are held to aim and fire on release. `src/combat/technique/techniqueRoster.js:140` (`techniqueParts`, `aims`)

## Online vs offline
- The whole re-checked layer above is the online game: PCAAO's core, RRI's spans and swing conversion, Roleplay & Realism's archery, and Enemy Expansion/MUDEX foe rows are the room's, forced for every client. Offline, a player's own mod switches decide — with PCAAO off, the classic `formulas.js` core is the game: skill (not skill × 1.5) to hit, material × 10, Strength / 5, Dodging / 4, criticals on the hit roll only, and the classic span tables. `src/combat/pcaao.js:1231` (`installPcaao`), `src/combat/formulas.js:384` (`calculateSuccessfulHit`), `src/combat/formulas.js:88` (`damageModifier`)
- Which foes stand, and the rows they are minted from, are the host's online: Enemy Expansion's tables with MUDEX's read after them; a peer fights the owner's foes. `src/systems/enemyExpansion/dexInstall.js:52` (`dbAssets`), `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:402` (`daggerfall-enemy-expansion`)
- Spell pricing differs online past skill 100: online, a school skill past 100 prices a spell at its at-100 cost × 100 / skill (half at 200, never nothing); offline and up to 100, the classic (110 − skill) term stands, which reaches zero at 110. `src/systems/spellcost.js:44` (`SPELL_COST_SKILL_REFERENCE`), `src/systems/spellcost.js:49` (`skillScaledCost`)
- Port cost modifiers and free-cast doors (sets, sigils, Spellweave) apply only to a player's own cast; a foe's cast is priced with `portMods: false`, so a player's gear never makes a foe's spell cheaper. `src/systems/spellcost.js:204` (`calculateCastCost`)
- Telegraphed blows are judged per client in multiplayer: a peer foe's blow lands or is dodged by where your own feet are on your own client, with the same perfect-dodge late sample. `src/ai/puppetBlows.js:143` (puppet blow verdict)
- A technique never strikes a player's body with its technique shape or weight: against a player body it takes the plain swing's test and number. `src/combat/playerWeapon.js:810` (technique swing resolution)
- Stuck, retrievable arrows and the 50% lodge chance are this game's departures from DFU, where an arrow that meets geometry is simply lost and BowDamage keeps every shaft that hits. `src/combat/arrowFlight.js:56` (`ARROW_LODGE_CHANCE`)
- Swing speed is this game's own law, a declared departure from DFU: DFU's frame formula made Speed 100 swing four times a second; here Speed runs on a bounded straight line (× 1.4 at 0 to × 0.6 at 100) with weapon heft and handling folded in. `src/characters/weaponStates.js:45` (`SWING_BASE_FRAME`), `src/characters/weaponStates.js:54` (`swingTempo`)
