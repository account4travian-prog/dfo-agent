# T1: Towns, shops, banks and talk
Commit: 0df2d87eb · Researcher: Atlas swarm (T1) · Date: 2026-10-21

## Player-facing summary

A town is where the game stops being a road and becomes a place: named buildings you can enter, counters that buy and sell, a bank that holds your gold, a tavern that rents you a bed, and people who will talk to you if they like you enough. Offline, all of this runs on the classic rules — each building keeps its own hours, each region keeps its own bank account and its own prices, and a shopkeeper's patience is the only economy. Online, the port changes the shape of town life in ways you can feel: the shops, guild hall, bank, library and palace never close (nobody can advance a shared clock for you), the city gates never shut at night, every bank in the world is one account — the Bank of the Empire — and every town carries a Prosperity score that moves what its counters ask and pay. Two mods are also silently part of the rules online: Roleplay & Realism: Items prices worn gear by its condition, and Roleplay & Realism sets the bank's loan limit — which the Empire then cuts to a tenth, so online you can borrow 1,000 gold per level, not the offline fortune. Talk is a skill check dressed as a conversation: your tone, your Personality and the town's opinion of you decide whether a stranger answers, refuses, or marks the building you asked about on your map.

## Rules and numbers

### Shops: what they stock and what they buy

Layer: base, with one **mod** rule (Roleplay & Realism: Items) that is live online because the lane forces the mod on and holds it whole (see the lane section at the end).

- Nine building kinds are stocked storefronts: Alchemist, Armorer, Bookseller, ClothingStore, FurnitureStore, GemStore, GeneralStore, PawnShop, WeaponSmith. `src/systems/shopStock.js:178` (`isShop`)
- Only three shops repair: Armorer, GeneralStore, WeaponSmith. `src/systems/shopStock.js:189` (`isRepairShop`)
- Each shop buys only its own listed item groups (for example the PawnShop takes almost everything; the Bookseller takes books and paper goods). `src/systems/shopStock.js:148` (`SHOP_BUYS_GROUPS`)
- Online, no shop buys back endless-stock items (the Materials Bag, campfire kits, rations), nor collectible cards or packs, trade goods, or boat parts and deeds — those have their own markets. `src/systems/shopStock.js:160` (`shopBuysItem`), `src/systems/shopStock.js:285` (`isEndlessStock`)
- Shelf stock is rolled per shop type from (item group, chance) pairs; an item appears when its rarity is no greater than the shop's quality and a Dice100 roll beats chance × 5 × (21 − rarity) / 100. `src/systems/shopStock.js:92` (`SHOP_ITEM_GROUPS`), `src/systems/shopStock.js:320` (`stockShopShelf`)
- Shelves restock when the game day changes. `src/systems/shopStock.js:269` (`createStockedDate`)
- **Mod rule (Roleplay & Realism: Items), online:** found loot's condition is randomized to 20–75% when it drops. `src/systems/roleplayRealismItems/rriRealism.js:122` (`randomConditionLootItems`)
- **Mod rule (Roleplay & Realism: Items), online:** on a shop shelf, armor and weapons from a low-quality shop start worn — condition floor 25% at quality ≤ 3, 40% at ≤ 7, 60% at ≤ 13, 75% at ≤ 17, and always new (100%) at quality 18+. `src/systems/roleplayRealismItems/rriRealism.js:160` (`storeQualityLow`)

### Prices and haggling

Layer: base formula; **mod** condition pricing (Roleplay & Realism: Items) live online; **port** departures (MERC-CAP, REALM P0.4, MERC-SLOPE, ESSENTIALS-HALF, ECON1) live online.

- The counter's base cost law: an item's cost is its base value (minimum 1) scaled by the regional price adjustment ÷ 1000 (result minimum 1), then doubled around the shop-quality term — quality 20 asks about 20% over the regional band, quality 0 about 20% under. `src/systems/shopStock.js:823` (`calculateCost`)
- **Mod rule (Roleplay & Realism: Items), online:** the base value in that law is replaced by the item's condition-scaled value — base value × max(condition%, 20%) — because the mod's `conditionBasedPrices` dial ships on and the lane holds the mod whole online. A piece at 50% condition is priced off half its value; nothing prices below a fifth. `src/systems/roleplayRealismItems/rriRealism.js:139` (`conditionCostBase`), `src/systems/roleplayRealismItems/rriRealism.js:154` (`conditionBasedPricesOn`)
- Your haggle starts from the merchant's level: 5 × (shop quality − 10) + 50, rolled the same way for Mercantile and for Personality. `src/systems/shopStock.js:905` (`calculateTradePrice`)
- Buying: the price is floor(((192 × dm >> 8) + (dp >> 8)) × cost / 256), where dm is the merchant's margin against your (100 − Mercantile) and dp against your (100 − Personality) — in effect Mercantile carries about three quarters of the haggle and Personality about a quarter. `src/systems/shopStock.js:905` (`calculateTradePrice`)
- Selling: the price is floor(((179 × dm >> 8) + (51 × dp >> 8)) × cost / 256) with the margins inverted — the weights are a little worse for you than when buying (about 70% / 20%). `src/systems/shopStock.js:905` (`calculateTradePrice`)
- **Port rule, online (MERC-CAP):** Mercantile and Personality are clamped to 0–100 in the haggle online, so out-of-range skill values cannot bend the price past the classic band. `src/systems/shopStock.js:878` (`ONLINE_HAGGLE_MAX`), `src/systems/shopStock.js:907` (`calculateTradePrice`)
- **Port rule, online (REALM P0.4):** a shop pays at most half of what it would ask its best haggler for the same piece. `src/systems/shopStock.js:836` (`ONLINE_SALE_SHARE`)
- **Port rule, online (MERC-SLOPE):** under that half, your own sale offer is weighted halfway toward the best haggler's offer (weight 0.5), and a sale pays at least 1 gold whenever the half and your offer both reach 1. `src/systems/shopStock.js:893` (`ONLINE_SALE_SKILL_WEIGHT`)
- **Port rule, online (LAW-3):** a counter never pays more for a piece than the price that counter itself sold it for — counter-bought pieces carry a mark of what was paid. `src/systems/tradeModes.js:280` (`markCounterBought`)
- **Port rule, online (ESSENTIALS-HALF):** essentials cost half online, rounded up, minimum 1, and free stays free: tavern rooms, food and drink, temple disease cures, travel fares, and potions at any counter. `src/systems/shopStock.js:850` (`ONLINE_ESSENTIALS_PRICE_SCALE`), `src/systems/shopStock.js:853` (`essentialPrice`)
- Every purchase asks at least 1 gold per piece; sales have no such floor. `src/systems/tradeModes.js:418` (`MIN_PRICE_PER_PIECE`), `src/systems/tradeModes.js:456` (`getTradePrice`)
- Identifying an item costs (25 × its value) >> 8 — roughly a tenth of its value — and is free on Witches Festival; identifying does not haggle. `src/systems/tradeModes.js:121` (`IDENTIFY_COST_MULTIPLIER`), `src/systems/tradeModes.js:151` (`calculateItemIdentifyCost`)
- Holiday halvings in the Buy mode: the Merchants Festival halves everything bought outside a guild, Tales and Tallow halves inside the Mages Guild, and the Warriors Festival halves weapons outside a guild; a holiday half and the online essentials half never stack. `src/systems/tradeModes.js:213` (`buyHolidayHalvesPrice`), `src/systems/tradeModes.js:224` (`buyItemPrice`)
- Each region's price index starts at 750–1250 (per mille). `src/systems/shopStock.js:665` (`initialRegionPrice`)
- The index walks daily: ±2% steps (× 51/50 up, × 49/50 down), tilted by (the Merchants' power − the region's power) ÷ 5, and clamped to 250–4000; below 500 the region reads PricesLow, above 2000 PricesHigh. `src/systems/shopStock.js:705` (`priceWalkStep`), `src/systems/shopStock.js:736` (`PRICE_ADJUSTMENT_MIN`)
- **Port rule, online (ECON1):** the regional index is the world's, not yours — a pure function of the shared world day, computed identically on every client; your own region-price table is never written online. `src/systems/shopStock.js:677` (`regionPriceAdjustment`), `src/systems/worldTick.js:236` (`worldRegionPrice`)

### Prosperity (online only)

Layer: **port** — the whole town economy is the port's online layer; offline there is no town card and counters price as the classic game.

- Prosperity applies to MAPS towns of type TownCity, TownHamlet and TownVillage only. `src/net/townEconLaw.js:32` (`isEconTown`)
- A town's Prosperity is a 0–100 score built from: a base of 50; a size term (city +6, hamlet/town +2, village −2); a market cycle; its seat's standing and edict; a raid term; the players' share; and its sea lanes. `src/net/townEconLaw.js:111` (`townProsperity`)
- The market cycle is two sine waves over real UTC days — amplitude 12 with an 11-day period and amplitude 6 with a 29-day period — phased by the town's map pixel, so two towns are never in step. `src/net/townEconLaw.js:42` (`TOWN_CYCLE`)
- A holding guild's seat moves its town by (Standing − 50) ÷ 5, plus its edict: market-day +6, festival +8, open-gates +3, levy −6, curfew −4. `src/net/townEconLaw.js:44` (`TOWN_STANDING_DIV`), `src/net/townEconLaw.js:46` (`TOWN_EDICT_TERMS`)
- A raid at the town's gates costs −15 while it stands. `src/net/townEconLaw.js:48` (`TOWN_RAID_TERM`)
- Each sea lane a port town holds adds +2, up to 3 lanes. `src/net/townEconLaw.js:53` (`TOWN_SEA_LANE`), `src/net/townEconLaw.js:56` (`townSeaTerm`)
- The bands are: Booming ≥ 85, Thriving ≥ 65, Steady ≥ 35, Struggling ≥ 15, Destitute below 15. `src/net/townEconLaw.js:58` (`TOWN_BANDS`)
- What counters ASK follows the score directly: factor = 1 − 0.25 × (Prosperity − 50) / 50 — 0.75 at Prosperity 100, 1.25 at Prosperity 0. `src/net/townEconLaw.js:119` (`townBuyFactor`)
- What counters PAY falls away from the middle both ways: factor = 1 − 0.25 × |Prosperity − 50| / 50 — 0.75 at both extremes, 1.0 at 50, so a counter never pays above the region's usual in a swung town. `src/net/townEconLaw.js:123` (`townSaleFactor`)
- The asking factor (town × seat combined) never goes below 0.70, and what a counter pays never exceeds what it asks you. `src/net/townEconLaw.js:68` (`TOWN_FACTOR_FLOOR`), `src/net/townEconLaw.js:140` (`townShopFactor`)
- The factor touches Buy and Repair on the asking side, and Sell and SellMagic on the paying side; it is never routed through the regional price index. `src/net/townEconLaw.js:140` (`townShopFactor`)
- Players move a town only by witnessed acts, counted per town per real day: watching (weight 1, cap 30 per account), trading (4, cap 10), raiding (20, cap 1) and sea trade (2, cap 10). `src/net/townEconLaw.js:154` (`TOWN_ECON_SOURCES`), `src/net/townEconLaw.js:157` (`TOWN_ECON_WEIGHTS`), `src/net/townEconLaw.js:160` (`TOWN_ECON_CAPS`)
- The acts are kept for 14 days and decay × 0.8 per day; the players' share is 30 × A / (A + 150), capped at +30 — and no payment of gold moves a town at all. `src/net/townEconLaw.js:167` (`TOWN_ECON_DECAY`), `src/net/townEconLaw.js:172` (`TOWN_ECON_HALF`)
- Offline, none of this exists: a counter prices as the classic game and there is no town card. `src/systems/town/townEcon.js:9` (`TE1 header`)

### Banks

Layer: base account machinery; **port** Empire account online (EMPIRE-ACCOUNT, REALM P0.3); **mod** loan limit (Roleplay & Realism) live online under the port divisor.

- Offline, there is one bank account per region (62 regions), and gold banked in one region cannot be withdrawn in another. `src/systems/banking.js:145` (`createBankAccounts`)
- **Port rule, online (EMPIRE-ACCOUNT):** every branch is the same account — the Empire's, held at region index 17 (Daggerfall) — and every gold move (deposit, withdrawal, loan, house and ship money) is routed to it. `src/systems/banking.js:461` (`EMPIRE_ACCOUNT_REGION`), `src/systems/banking.js:464` (`goldRegion`)
- **Port rule, online:** when you join, the gold in all your regional accounts folds into the Empire account. `src/systems/banking.js:470` (`foldEmpireAccounts`)
- Loans stay regional even online — the due date, default and reputation live in the region that lent — but the gold lent and repaid moves through the Empire account. `src/systems/banking.js:822` (`borrowLoan`)
- Deposit takes your purse first; the wagon's gold is used only for the shortfall. `src/systems/banking.js:722` (`depositGold`)
- **Port rule, online:** a defaulter's deposit is garnished toward their defaulted loans before the rest lands. `src/systems/banking.js:622` (`garnishDeposit`), `src/systems/banking.js:735` (`depositGold`)
- Withdrawal is refused if the amount exceeds the account, and refused as TOO_HEAVY if the coins would overfill your carrying capacity — carried gold weighs 0.0025 kg per piece. `src/systems/banking.js:742` (`withdrawGold`), `src/systems/inventory.js:650` (`GOLD_PIECE_WEIGHT_KG`)
- A letter of credit is charged to the account at trunc(amount × 1.01) — a 1% commission — while the letter itself is worth the plain amount; the minimum letter is 100 gold. `src/systems/banking.js:119` (`LOC_COMMISSION`), `src/systems/banking.js:121` (`LOC_MINIMUM`), `src/systems/banking.js:774` (`withdrawLetter`)
- The balance test runs before the minimum test: asking for a 50-gold letter on an empty account is refused as not-enough-in-account, not as too small. `src/systems/banking.js:774` (`withdrawLetter`)
- Depositing letters deposits every letter you carry at face value; there is no partial deposit. `src/systems/banking.js:756` (`depositAllLetters`)
- Letters of credit weigh nothing and can pay for eligible transactions directly (purchases, court fines, the market); they become account gold again only when deposited. `src/systems/inventory.js:134` (`letterOfCredit`)
- A loan credits your account with the amount and debits the loan at amount + 10% immediately — the interest exists from the instant of borrowing, so repaying early saves nothing. `src/systems/banking.js:557` (`calculateBankLoanRepayment`), `src/systems/banking.js:822` (`borrowLoan`)
- The loan term is exactly one game year (360 days), and the minimum loan is 100 gold. `src/systems/banking.js:113` (`LOAN_REPAY_MINUTES`), `src/systems/banking.js:122` (`LOAN_MINIMUM`)
- Offline you may hold one loan per region at a time. `src/systems/banking.js:822` (`borrowLoan`)
- The base loan cap is 50,000 gold per character level. `src/systems/banking.js:117` (`LOAN_MAX_PER_LEVEL`)
- **Mod rule (Roleplay & Realism), online:** the mod replaces that cap with its per-level table — 2,000 / 4,000 / 6,000 / 8,000 / 10,000 / 20,000 / 30,000 / 40,000 / 50,000 by its `loanAmountPerLevel` dial — and the shipped default (index 4) is 10,000 per level. The lane forces the mod on and holds this dial at its shipped value online. `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrRealism.js:35` (`LOAN_VALUES`), `src/systems/roleplayRealism/rrRealism.js:148` (`rrLoanMaxPerLevel`), `src/systems/modSettings.js:644` (`loanAmountPerLevel`)
- **Port rule, online (EMPIRE-BANK):** the Empire divides whatever cap stands by 10 — so the effective online borrowing cap is **1,000 gold per level** (level 7 borrows at most 7,000). `src/systems/banking.js:550` (`EMPIRE_LOAN_DIVISOR`), `src/systems/banking.js:551` (`calculateMaxBankLoan`)
- **Port rule, online (REALM P0.3, the one-lender rule):** a default anywhere, or a loan standing anywhere, blocks borrowing everywhere in the Empire. `src/systems/banking.js:589` (`empireRefusal`)
- **Port rule, online:** on joining, debt above the Empire's cap is called in — paid from the Empire account, then your other regional accounts, then your purse; whatever cannot be paid falls due at once and defaults. `src/systems/banking.js:644` (`callInEmpireDebt`)
- The bank reminds you at 6, 3 and 1 months remaining on a loan. `src/systems/banking.js:125` (`LOAN_REMINDER_MONTHS`), `src/systems/banking.js:843` (`checkOverdueLoans`)
- An overdue loan is settled from the account first; a loan still standing after that is a default, recorded as the crime LoanDefault (crime amount 15), and your hasDefaulted flag is set once. `src/systems/banking.js:865` (`settleOverdueLoan`), `src/systems/crimes.js:23` (`CRIMES`)
- A house's price is its mesh radius × 1,280 gold, and the bank buys a deed back at 85% (truncated). `src/systems/banking.js:111` (`HOUSE_PRICE_MULT`), `src/systems/banking.js:134` (`housePrice`), `src/systems/banking.js:107` (`DEED_SELL_MULT`)
- Ships cost a flat 100,000 or 200,000 gold and sell back at 85% (truncated). `src/systems/banking.js:100` (`SHIP_PRICES`), `src/systems/banking.js:129` (`shipSellPrice`)
- House and ship purchases take your purse first and the account covers the shortfall; online both pay from and into the Empire account; a deed crossed in through customs is never bought back online. `src/systems/banking.js:357` (`purchaseShip`)

### Talk and topics

Layer: base — the talk engine is the classic system ported; no mod overrides it online, and the lane does not force one for it. The enhanced panel is the port's own interface skin over the same rules.

- Every mobile townsperson you can talk to stands as the region's People faction, so the reaction you meet is that faction's reputation toward you, plus your biography modifier, plus your standing with their social group. `src/systems/talk/talk.js:243` (`getPeopleOfCurrentRegion`), `src/systems/talk/talk.js:290` (`getReactionToPlayer`)
- The greeting follows the reaction: at 30 or above the warmest greeting, at 10 the friendly one, at 0 the neutral one, below 0 the cold one. `src/systems/talk/talkSession.js:34` (`MIN_LIKE_REACTION`), `src/systems/talk/talkSession.js:53` (`greetingTextId`)
- At a reaction below −20 the person refuses to talk at all. `src/systems/talk/talkSession.js:36` (`REFUSE_TALK_REACTION`), `src/systems/talk/talkSession.js:133` (`startMobileTalk`)
- There are three tones — Polite, Normal, Blunt — and the T key cycles them in the classic window; the enhanced panel shows them as a segmented control. `src/scenes/townTalk.js:86` (`TONE_NAMES`), `src/ui/enhanced/enhancedTalk.js:78` (`TONE_LABELS`)
- Each question is answered at one of three tiers, rolled per question: your reaction for it is trunc(Personality ÷ 5), plus a question-type modifier (+5 for asking about a local building, or about a quest place or organization), plus a tone modifier, plus a skill term. `src/systems/talk/talkTopics.js:462` (`QUESTION_TYPE_REACTION_MODS`), `src/systems/talk/talkTopics.js:485` (`reactionTier012`)
- The tone modifier reads your Etiquette (Polite) or Streetwise (Blunt) against the person's social group — Polite runs [−10, +5, +10, +15, −15] across Commoners, Merchants, Scholars, Nobility, Underworld; Blunt runs [+10, +5, −10, −15, +15]. - The skill term: a Dice100 roll against Etiquette or Streetwise — success adds +5, failure −10; the first Polite question tallies Etiquette and the first Blunt one tallies Streetwise for the session. `src/systems/talk/talkTopics.js:485` (`reactionTier012`)

## Online vs offline

- **Hours:** offline, every building keeps its table hours and shops close on Suns Rest; online, shops, the guild hall, the bank, the library and the palace are always staffed, and the city gates never shut.
- **Banking:** offline, 62 separate regional accounts, regional loans, and a 50,000-per-level cap; online, one Empire account everywhere, loans still regional on paper but drawn on the Empire, a one-lender rule across the whole map, and an effective cap of 1,000 gold per level (the Roleplay & Realism table's 10,000, divided by the Empire's 10).
- **Prices:** offline, the regional index is your own save's walk and worn gear prices at full base value unless you enabled the mod yourself; online, the index is the world's shared function of the day, condition-based pricing is always on, haggle skills are clamped to 0–100, a counter pays at most half its best ask, and essentials cost half.
- **Prosperity:** online only. Offline there is no town score, no town card, and no town factor on any price.
- **Talk:** the same engine both ways; the differences are the shared clock (you cannot wait out a refusal by resting to a friendlier hour as freely) and the enhanced panel being the default face online.
- **Town layout:** online, Beautiful Cities and Beautiful Villages are forced on so every player in the room walks the same streets; offline, the layout is your own mod choice.
