# T3: Law and crime
Commit: 0df2d87eb · Researcher: Atlas swarm (T3) · Date: 2026-10-21

## Player-facing summary
- Every region keeps its own legal standing for you, starting at 0; a crime is charged against the region where it happens. `src/systems/court.js:162` (`legalRepOf`)
- A crime costs legal standing by a fixed table (Theft −8, Murder −20, High Treason −75), and the region's People faction loses half as much. `src/systems/court.js:252` (`lowerRepForCrime`)
- The watch comes only when a crime is seen (or a seeing guard is nearby); a first minor offence in a region earns a warning from the living-world watch instead of an arrest. `src/systems/standing.js:194` (`WARNABLE_CRIMES`)
- If a guard's blow lands, you are asked to surrender. Surrender (or a fatal blow the watch accepts) takes you to court at 1 health. `src/scenes/arrestFlow.js:322` (`onGuardHit`)
- In court you plead Guilty (fine and days are halved) or Not Guilty and argue Debate (Etiquette) or Lie (Streetwise). `src/scenes/arrestFlow.js:476` (`startCourtFlow`)
- Banishment exists only for Murder, High Treason and Treason, lasts 30 days of the calendar you see, and can be lifted early by a pardon bought at that region's temple (2,500 gold the first time). `src/systems/standing.js:44` (`BANISHMENT_MINUTES`)
- A bad name repairs: serving or paying gives the charge back (Assault, Murder and the Treasons keep half as a mark), a temple penance buys back 5 points, and a standing below zero also drifts back 1 point every 7 days. `src/systems/court.js:532` (`MARKED_CRIMES`)
- In a town whose seat holder has proclaimed a Curfew edict, crimes cost double the legal reputation and the night watch stands 5 levels stronger. `src/net/townSeatLaw.js:965` (`CURFEW`)

## Rules and numbers

### The crime list and its weights
Layer: base (classic/Daggerfall Unity tables, ported in the port's leaf enum and court tables) — live online because no mod key in the online lane names law or crime, and the port's court code is the only court code. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:308` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS`)
- The crime enum holds 15 crimes: None 0, Attempted_Breaking_And_Entering 1, Trespassing 2, Breaking_And_Entering 3, Assault 4, Murder 5, Tax_Evasion 6, Criminal_Conspiracy 7, Vagrancy 8, Smuggling 9, Piracy 10, High_Treason 11, Pickpocketing 12, Theft 13, Treason 14, LoanDefault 15. `src/systems/crimes.js:18` (`CRIMES`)
- Legal-reputation loss per crime (`REPUTATION_LOSS_PER_CRIME`, by crime id 1–15): Attempted Breaking and Entering 10, Trespassing 5, Breaking and Entering 10, Assault 8, Murder 20, Tax Evasion 10, Criminal Conspiracy 2, Vagrancy 1, Smuggling 2, Piracy 2, High Treason 75, Pickpocketing 2, Theft 8, Treason 36, Loan Default 10. `src/systems/court.js:103` (`REPUTATION_LOSS_PER_CRIME`)
- The court penalty tables are indexed by crimeType = crime − 1, in the same order as the list above. `src/systems/court.js:106` (`PENALTY_PER_LEGAL_REP_POINT`)
- Base penalty per crime (`BASE_PENALTY`): 300, 200, 600, 1000, 10000, 200, 500, 100, 500, 500, 1200, 200, 200, 1000, 100. `src/systems/court.js:108` (`BASE_PENALTY`)
- Extra penalty per point of negative legal reputation (`PENALTY_PER_LEGAL_REP_POINT`): 5, 5, 6, 6, 10, 5, 5, 3, 8, 8, 9, 6, 0, 8, 0 — so Theft and Loan Default fines never grow with a bad name. `src/systems/court.js:107` (`PENALTY_PER_LEGAL_REP_POINT`)
- Minimum penalty per crime (`MIN_PENALTY`): 50, 10, 80, 10, 9000, 10, 10, 2, 10, 10, 160, 5, 5, 10, 4. `src/systems/court.js:109` (`MIN_PENALTY`)
- Maximum penalty per crime (`MAX_PENALTY`): 1000, 800, 1200, 1500, 12000, 12000, 1500, 700, 1200, 1200, 2000, 1000, 1000, 1500, 700. `src/systems/court.js:110` (`MAX_PENALTY`)
- Every crime write goes through one setter; a transformed lycanthrope is never tagged (the write becomes None). `src/systems/court.js:68` (`setCrimeCommitted`)
- A crime is charged (its reputation loss applied) once per chase, not once per guard wave; a worse crime committed during the chase is charged as itself. `src/scenes/arrestFlow.js:269` (`chargeOnce`)
- Leaving the location rect clears the active crime, so the watch stands down — but the reputation charge already taken is not refunded by leaving. `src/systems/court.js:652` (`clearCrimeOnLocationExit`)

### How each crime is committed
Layer: port (the port's own trigger code in its scenes/systems) — these are the writers that set `crimeCommitted` online; quests can also set any named crime. `src/systems/quest/actions.js:3196` (`SetPlayerCrimeAction.createNew`)
- Pickpocketing: a failed pickpocket attempt on a townsperson sets the crime and spawns the watch; success chance is the Pickpocket skill (plus 5 × (your level − target level) against a leveled foe), clamped 5–95. `src/systems/talk/talk.js:357` (`pickpocket`)
- A successful pickpocket pinches 1–6 gold 67% of the time and finds nothing valuable 33% of the time. `src/systems/talk/talk.js:328` (`pickpocket`)
- Theft (private property): taking from a stranger's cupboard or container rolls detection when the window closes; the chance of being seen is 100 − Pickpocket skill + building quality + load, clamped 5–95, where load = truncated basket weight + item count. `src/systems/theft.js:92` (`privatePropertyTheft`)
- If that theft roll detects you, the crime is Theft and the guards are called at once; if not, your Pickpocket skill tallies instead. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:9174` (`openLoot`)
- Theft (open shop): shoplifting from a shop's basket uses the same detection chance; being caught sets Theft and calls the guards immediately. `src/systems/theft.js:135` (`shopliftAttempt`)
- Items taken from another's keeping are marked stolen (`stolen: true`) as they land in your pack, and the mark rides the save and trades with the piece. `src/systems/stolenGoods.js:35` (`STOLEN_MARK`)
- Attempted Breaking and Entering: bashing a locked door and failing can levy it — only when noticed, on a nested 10% roll inside the failed bash. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:8118` (`tryEnter`)
- Breaking and Entering: bashing open an unlocked door levies the full crime on a flat 10% roll. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:8182` (`tryEnter`)
- Picking a lock tallies Lockpicking and, on success, the Thieves Guild tally; the lockpick attempt itself sets no crime in this code. `src/scenes/worldModes.js:8082` (`tryEnter`)
- Assault: striking a watch defender (a guard standing as the town's ally) sets Assault if you hold no crime already. `src/scenes/cityGuards.js:909` (`handleAttackFromPlayer`)
- Assault and Murder from street fights are set through the same crime setter in the world host's crime response. `src/scenes/world.js:22886` (`setCrimeCommitted`)
- Murder: killing a city watchman with your own blow sets Murder and sets `watchSlain` for that chase. `src/scenes/cityGuards.js:1004` (`damageGuard`)
- A watchman killed by a fall, a monster or another player does not brand you: the Murder tally sits inside the from-player gate. `src/scenes/cityGuards.js:932` (`damageGuard`)
- Vagrancy: pressing to camp/rest outdoors inside a town's rect registers Vagrancy and spawns the watch whether or not you go through with the rest; loitering never does. `src/systems/rest/restSession.js:276` (`canRest`)
- Loan Default: a bank loan past its due date first raids the account for what it can cover; a loan still standing after that defaults once and returns the LoanDefault crime. `src/systems/banking.js:865` (`settleOverdueLoan`)
- Loan reminders fire as the time left crosses 6, 3 and 1 months before the due date. `src/systems/banking.js:853` (`checkOverdueLoans`)
- Piracy: the first shot your guns land on a lawful ship (merchantman or navy) is Piracy against that ship's crown, charged once per ship; boarding and taking her charges it a second time. `src/systems/naval/navalLaw.js:91` (`lawOf`)
- Piracy at sea is charged to the nearest crown's region (Daggerfall, Wayrest or Sentinel), not to the townless sea region. `src/systems/naval/navalLaw.js:48` (`crownRegion`)
- Piracy also raises Notoriety in that crown's waters (0–100): firing +30, sinking +20, boarding +15, decaying 12 per game day. `src/systems/naval/navalLaw.js:32` (`NOTORIETY`)
- No writer in `src/` sets Trespassing, Smuggling or Tax Evasion directly; they are reachable through the quest SetPlayerCrime action, which accepts any crime named in the enum. `src/systems/quest/actions.js:3186` (`SetPlayerCrimeAction.pattern`)

### Legal standing by region
Layer: port (REP overhaul, `standing.js`/`court.js`) — a declared departure from Daggerfall Unity, shipped as the port's own law in both modes, with online-only clock branches noted below. `src/systems/standing.js:1` (`standing.js`)
- Legal reputation is per region and starts at 0. `src/systems/court.js:162` (`legalRepOf`)
- Legal reputation is clamped to −100…+100. `src/systems/court.js:164` (`LEGAL_REP_MIN`)
- A crime's charge also costs the region's People faction half the legal loss (truncated toward zero), propagated to its allies and enemies. `src/systems/court.js:255` (`lowerRepForCrime`)
- The fourteen standing words, by reputation: above 80 revered, 60 esteemed, 40 honored, 20 admired, 10 respected, 0 dependable, exactly 0 a common citizen, below 0 undependable, −10 a scoundrel, −20 a criminal, −40 a villain, −60 pond scum, −80 hated. `src/systems/legalBands.js:8` (`legalStandingWord`)
- Every reputation change with a cause is announced on the HUD with the delta and the band it leaves you in. `src/scenes/standingHost.js:104` (`legalNoticeLine`)
- Drift: every 112 game days all reputations walk one point toward zero (the classic NormalizeReputations). `src/systems/court.js:232` (`normalizeReputations`)
- Faster recovery (REP4): a standing below zero recovers 1 point every 7 days (10,080 minutes), lived and across an absence. `src/systems/court.js:169` (`RECOVERY_INTERVAL_MINUTES`)
- Online absence rule: time away pays only the recovery half — a negative standing drifts back, a positive standing is never worn down by an absence. `src/systems/court.js:217` (`normalizeReputations`)

- A witnessed (immediate) crime converts nearby townsfolk within 77.5 units: wandering guard NPCs first, then non-guards behind you (at least 105.469° from your facing) at 1-in-4 each. `src/scenes/cityGuards.js:133` (`GUARD_NPC_SPAWN_RANGE`)
- If nobody converts, the fallback spawner places 2–5 guards at 12.8–51.2 units. `src/scenes/cityGuards.js:139` (`GUARD_FALLBACK_MIN_DIST`)

- If only civilians saw it, guards arrive after a countdown of 5 + a roll of 0–5 seconds (Random.Range(5, 11)). `src/scenes/cityGuards.js:599` (`spawnCityGuardsNow`)
- A crime inside an open shop, tavern or residence brings 2–5 guards through that building's lowest outer door instead of the street; the street pool spawns nobody through a wall. `src/scenes/cityGuards.js:480` (`spawnCityGuardsNow`)

- The watch defends the town (port feature, on by default and the player's own switch online): a hostile monster hunting you inside a town, while you hold no crime, brings guards as your allies after the same 5–11 second arrival countdown. `src/systems/town/townWatch.js:54` (`TOWN_WATCH_ARRIVAL_MIN_SECONDS`)
- Defender waves are capped at 3 per incident, and defenders walk away after 10 seconds of quiet. `src/systems/town/townWatch.js:59` (`TOWN_WATCH_STAND_DOWN_SECONDS`)
- The town-watch feature's online state is the player's own (`online: 'player'`), initial value on. `src/systems/features.js:1589` (`town-watch`)

### The watch: first warning and the known-criminal stop
Layer: port (WATCH-KNOWS and REP1, in `standing.js`/`standingHost.js`) — the stop replaces Daggerfall Unity's per-minute levy online and offline; Daggerfall Unity's passive roll survives in code with no production caller since REP1. `src/systems/encounters.js:274` (`passiveGuardSpawns`)
- Warnable first offences are Attempted Breaking and Entering, Trespassing, Vagrancy and Pickpocketing. `src/systems/standing.js:194` (`WARNABLE_CRIMES`)
- A warning is given only by the living-world watch, only for a warnable crime, only once per region, and never to a known criminal; the crime is still charged, then let go. `src/scenes/arrestFlow.js:291` (`onGuardHit`)
- A known criminal is a character whose legal standing in the region is under −10, or who stands banished there. `src/systems/standing.js:155` (`knownCriminal`)
- The watch may stop a known criminal at most once per 2 game hours (120 minutes) in a region, and not during grace. `src/systems/standing.js:39` (`CHALLENGE_COOLDOWN_MINUTES`)
- Answering the law grants 1 game day (1,440 minutes) of grace from stops in that region. `src/systems/standing.js:41` (`CHALLENGE_GRACE_MINUTES`)
- The stop happens only when a wandering guard can actually see you on the street; the street is re-checked once a second. `src/scenes/standingHost.js:24` (`STANDING_LOOK_MS`)
- At the stop you may pay the fine on the spot (P), come quietly (S), or refuse (R); there is no Escape from the box. `src/scenes/standingHost.js:69` (`open`)
- The spot fine is the court's Criminal Conspiracy penalty at your standing, every unit taken in coin with no prison days, doubled if you are banished. `src/systems/standing.js:181` (`challengeFine`)
- Paying the spot fine also mends your name by up to 5 points, never past the watch's −10 line, and the People get their half of the mend. `src/systems/standing.js:173` (`settleChallenge`)
- Coming quietly charges Criminal Conspiracy once (if you hold no crime) and takes you to court as a voluntary surrender. `src/scenes/arrestFlow.js:812` (`surrenderToChallenge`)
- Refusing sets Criminal Conspiracy and calls the watch as a seen crime does — the chase. `src/scenes/standingHost.js:76` (`open`)
- The stop is never made of a transformed lycanthrope, an invisible character, a character already holding a crime, or one in a trial. `src/scenes/standingHost.js:44` (`frame`)

### Arrest and surrender
Layer: base interception (Daggerfall Unity's EnemyAttack/SurrenderToCityGuards) with the port's REP2 surrender rule. `src/systems/court.js:400` (`surrenderToCityGuards`)
- A guard's landed hit on a player with an active crime, before the surrender dialogue has been shown, withholds its damage, charges the crime, and opens the surrender box. `src/scenes/arrestFlow.js:299` (`onGuardHit`)
- Answering Y surrenders: your health is set to 1 first, then court opens. `src/scenes/arrestFlow.js:315` (`onGuardHit`)

- A transformed lycanthrope cannot surrender; its halt box offers F (frighten — a level-based roll against the striking guard) or N (fight on); a successful frighten clears the crime and routs the watch. `src/scenes/arrestFlow.js:356` (`beastHaltBox`)
- While a surrender box or trial stands, all damage to the arrested player is vetoed — in both modes, because online the world does not pause for the court. `src/scenes/arrestFlow.js:262` (`inCourt`)

### Court: how a sentence is priced
Layer: base court math (DaggerfallCourtWindow) with three port departures (REP2/REP3): banishment restricted to the banishable set, and a fine that no longer rises with good standing. `src/systems/court.js:445` (`startCourt`)
- The court closes at once if no crime is assigned. `src/systems/court.js:423` (`startCourt`)
- Punishment type: with negative standing and a banishable crime, two failed rolls (thresholds min(75, −rep) and min(75, −rep/2)) make banishment (type 0); otherwise the sentence is fine/prison (type 2). `src/systems/court.js:430` (`startCourt`)
- Execution (type 1) can never be produced: `startCourt` returns only 0 or 2, matching Daggerfall Unity's own note that an execution sentence cannot be given. `src/systems/court.js:443` (`startCourt`)
- The penalty amount is the base penalty minus per-rep × legalRep when your standing is negative, and the plain base penalty when it is 0 or better, clamped to the crime's min/max, then divided by 40 (truncated). `src/systems/court.js:447` (`startCourt`)
- Each unit of the divided penalty flips a coin: heads adds 40 gold to the fine, tails adds 3 days in prison. `src/systems/court.js:454` (`startCourt`)
- If your gold (coins plus letters of credit) is less than the fine, the fine is cut to your gold and the shortfall becomes extra days at 40 gold per day (truncated). `src/systems/court.js:464` (`startCourt`)
- A fine can be paid out of letters of credit as well as coins; the payment spends letters before coins when the purse alone cannot cover it. `src/systems/court.js:319` (`deductGold`)

### Court: the guild rescue
Layer: base (DaggerfallCourtWindow's rescue arms), checked after the penalty is computed and before any plea. `src/systems/court.js:132` (`guildRescue`)
- For Assault or Murder, a Dark Brotherhood member may be rescued; for Attempted Breaking and Entering, Trespassing, Breaking and Entering or Pickpocketing, a Thieves Guild member may be. `src/systems/court.js:147` (`guildRescue`)
- The rescue succeeds when your guild rank is at least a roll of 0–19. `src/systems/court.js:149` (`guildRescue`)
- A rescued member is released without pleading, with vitals refilled and the sentence's reputation refund, and is the one court exit that does not reposition you to the location entrance. `src/scenes/arrestFlow.js:456` (`startCourtFlow`)

### Court: pleas and verdicts
Layer: base plea cascade. `src/scenes/arrestFlow.js:473` (`startCourtFlow`)
- Pleading Guilty to a fine/prison sentence halves the fine and the days (each shifted right by 1), deducts the fine, then serves the remaining days or releases you. `src/systems/court.js:561` (`pleaGuilty`)
- Pleading Guilty to a banishment sentence changes nothing: the outcome is banished. `src/systems/court.js:562` (`pleaGuilty`)
- Pleading Not Guilty, you choose Debate (Etiquette) or Lie (Streetwise); the chosen skill tallies once. `src/systems/court.js:575` (`pleaNotGuilty`)
- The defence chance is legalRep + (skill + live Personality) / 2, truncated, clamped 5–95. `src/systems/court.js:587` (`pleaNotGuilty`)
- Winning the defence frees you: vitals refill fully, the whole charge is refunded (an acquittal leaves no mark), and you are put down at the location entrance. `src/scenes/arrestFlow.js:494` (`verdict`)
- Losing the defence with legalRep + a 1–100 roll under 25 doubles the fine; over 75 halves it. `src/systems/court.js:593` (`pleaNotGuilty`)
- Verbatim quirk, kept: after a failed Not Guilty plea the adjusted fine is never actually deducted — only the full days serve; the guilty plea is the only path that charges its fine. `src/systems/court.js:601` (`resolveGuiltyVerdict`)
- Every guilty exit (plea, failed defence, banishment) confiscates every stolen-marked piece you carry, worn, held or in your Materials Bag; an acquittal and a guild rescue take nothing. `src/systems/court.js:612` (`confiscateStolen`)
- Confiscation never touches a wagon or a home's storage, and honest goods left in a confiscated bag are moved into your pack. `src/systems/court.js:639` (`confiscateStolen`)

### What a sentence gives back
Layer: port (REP2 "the charge, with a mark"), replacing Daggerfall Unity's half-less-one refund. `src/systems/court.js:525` (`sentenceRefund`)
- Serving, paying or being rescued gives back the whole legal charge for lesser crimes, and the region's People get back their half. `src/systems/court.js:535` (`sentenceRefund`)
- The marked crimes — Assault, Murder, High Treason, Treason — keep half the charge as a lasting mark (rounded toward the player). `src/systems/court.js:532` (`MARKED_CRIMES`)
- An acquittal gives the whole charge back whatever the crime. `src/systems/court.js:538` (`sentenceRefund`)
- Banishment itself refunds nothing: being run out of the region repairs no reputation. `src/scenes/arrestFlow.js:527` (`finish`)

### Banishment and pardons
Layer: port (REP3 "timed or pardoned"), replacing Daggerfall Unity's permanent banishment for any crime. `src/systems/standing.js:16` (`standing.js`)
- Only Murder, High Treason and Treason can be banished for. `src/systems/court.js:534` (`BANISHABLE_CRIMES`)
- A banishment sets the region's banished bit and a term of 30 days (43,200 minutes) of the calendar the player sees. `src/systems/standing.js:44` (`BANISHMENT_MINUTES`)
- Online, the term runs on the world's calendar (the sky's): about 30 real hours, and it lifts whether or not you play. `src/systems/standing.js:25` (`banishmentEnd`)
- A pardon lifts the banishment at once, counts as answering the law (a day's grace), and steps that region's pardon price up. `src/systems/standing.js:145` (`grantPardon`)
- The first pardon in a region costs 2,500 gold; the nth pardon there costs n × 2,500. `src/systems/standing.js:65` (`PARDON_BASE_PRICE`)
- Pardons are bought at a temple, from the donation priest's offers, for the law of the temple's own region; the temple is a sanctuary the watch does not enter. `src/ui/guildServiceWindows.js:427` (`standingOffers`)
- While banished, the watch's stop still applies to you and its spot fine is doubled. `src/systems/standing.js:186` (`challengeFine`)

## Gates
- No character level, Renown or registered-account gate exists in the law code: the law first meets a player in the first town, when a crime is committed or a guard sees a known criminal. `src/scenes/standingHost.js:51` (`frame`)
- There is no court without a crime: the court flow refuses to open on crime 0. `src/systems/court.js:423` (`startCourt`)
- Location gate: the watch never spawns in a dungeon. `src/scenes/cityGuards.js:451` (`spawnCityGuardsNow`)
