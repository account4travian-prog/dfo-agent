## Player-facing summary
- This sync adds little new progression content. The visible changes are chiefly fixes to travel rendering, dropped possessions, item handling, chat navigation and enemy appearance.
- Starfall's live event now supports larger waves and a distinct spawn grant; the account reward cap still limits what one player can claim.
- Host-owned world clocks and enemy scheduling were reworked so busy or slow owners advance shared foes with their own frames instead of letting one player run away with the room clock.
- Most remaining commits document audits, profiling, relay version pins or tests. They do not change a player rule and are omitted below.

## Rules and numbers

### World events and encounter timing
- Starfall can populate a wave of up to 24 under a dedicated spawn grant; the integration lane checks the grant's event kind before accepting it. This changes the event's spawn capacity, not the per-account daily star-claim cap. (`src/net/grantLaw.js:1-20` (port); `src/net/starfall/starfallLaw.js:1-20` (port); commits `ace4c7c3d`, `ab03936f4`, `a177bd6b6`, `65de611b5`)
- Starfall claims continue through account claim records, so a larger world wave does not increase the individual account's daily allowance. (`server-account/src/stars.js:1-20` (port); `src/net/starfall/starfallLaw.js:1-20` (port); commits `a177bd6b6`, `65de611b5`)
- Town quest/Work responses now identify the assigned questor and use the correct gendered reference for a female questor. (`src/systems/quest/machine.js:1-20` (port); commit `631b6667a`)

### Enemy behavior and shared-world clocks
- Online world ownership now carries an owner's own clock through the street, buildings and dungeon foes; elapsed time away does not grant catch-up progress. (`src/scenes/ownerCatchUp.js:1-20` (port); `src/net/online.js:1-20` (port); commits `7914d2caa`, `2f4e43344`, `3e235af27`, `2fefbe023`)
- Shared foe frames are prioritized and budgeted before other room work, with hit/drop updates counted; handoff and join handling were tightened to avoid duplicate or skipped enemy steps. (`server/src/index.js:1-20` (port); `server/src/relayScale.js:1-20` (port); `server/src/foesHold.js:1-20` (port); commits `6953c6650`, `acf410f60`, `f02c02eb5`, `e7073e760`, `93464a891`)

### Travel, boats and horses
