# P3: Gear and loot
Commit: 0df2d87eb · Researcher: Atlas swarm (P3) · Date: 2026-10-21

## Player-facing summary
- Online, two systems decide what gear you find: the port's own **Loot Rarity** ladder (forced on for everyone online) and the **Unleveled Loot** mod (forced on, and held whole at its shipped defaults), which rolls the *material* of every random weapon and armour piece from Luck, shop quality and dungeon kind — never from your level. `src/systems/features.js:770` (`lootRarity` control); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:404` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS['unleveledLoot']`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:508` (`ONLINE_WHOLE_MODS['unleveledLoot']`)
- The ladder has seven tiers — Common, Magic, Rare, Legendary, Aetheric, Artifact, Gilded — but only Magic, Rare and Legendary are ever *rolled*; Aetheric is a boss's set gear, Artifacts are Daggerfall's own, and the one Gilded piece is a fixed spoil. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:100` (`RARITY_ORDER`); `src/systems/rarityTier.js:10` (`ROLLED_TIERS`)
- Rarity follows the *source*, not you: a corpse rolls at the dead thing's level, a treasure pile at its dungeon kind's grade, and Luck only leans on the odds. A level-3 character can find a Legendary on a Daedra; a rat essentially never drops one. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:205` (`corpseSource`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:311` (`rarityChances`)
- Most ordinary enemies use a much stingier "plain foe" ladder and carry at most 3 items in total, gold included (5 in an Elite Dungeon). `src/systems/foeLootCap.js:62` (`PLAIN_FOE_RARITY_WEIGHTS`); `src/systems/foeLootCap.js:53` (`PLAIN_FOE_LOOT_CAP`)
- Online, plain-foe loot also grows with the *roller's* Renown in five stages, and in a shared dungeon room everyone sees the same base loot list, rolled under the room's rules, with a private bonus layer on top for each player. `src/systems/renownLoot.js:43` (`RENOWN_LOOT_QUARTERS`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:538` (`asTheRoom`)
- Magic and Rare pieces carry rolled bonus lines (affixes); Legendaries are fixed named records. Rare and Legendary pieces drop **unidentified** — the Identify spell or the Mages Guild reveals them. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:475` (`AFFIX_COUNTS`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:61` (the IsIdentified law)
- At the Mages Guild's **Reforge** you can salvage found Magic+ pieces into **Welkynd Shards** (Magic 1, Rare 3, Legendary 8), reroll one line, hone a line up its band, and set, unset or extract socketed gems. `src/systems/reforge.js:54` (`SALVAGE_SHARDS`); `src/scenes/worldModes.js:6189` (the Reforge on the Mages Guild's Identify service)
- Repairs online cost a third of the classic formula's price, and magic items *can* be repaired online — the lane forces AllowMagicRepairs on. `src/systems/repairService.js:51` (`REPAIR_COST_SCALE`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:178` (`ONLINE_FORCED_SETTINGS`)
- Selling found gear online pays for the condition it was *found* at, not the condition you repaired it to — a smith's paid repair adds nothing to the sale price; only your own mends (kits, spells) count, and only a third of them. `src/systems/item/foundMark.js:32` (`foundSalePercentage`); `src/systems/item/foundMark.js:22` (`MEND_SALE_SHARE`)

## Rules and numbers

### Which layer runs online
Layer: **port + mods, all forced by the lane.** Loot Rarity is a Features-registry row declared `online: true`; Unleveled Loot and Roleplay & Realism: Items are room-forced and held whole; Roleplay & Realism's weapon-materials switch is room-forced.
- Loot Rarity's registry row: on by default (`initial: true`) and forced on online (`online: true`), so no online player can turn the ladder off. `src/systems/features.js:770` (`lootRarity` control)
- Unleveled Loot is room-forced on: `unleveledLoot: { Enabled: true }`. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:404` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS['unleveledLoot']`)
- Unleveled Loot is also held **whole** online (empty exception list): every one of its dials — including its ten material-swap switches — reads the shipped default, not the player's. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:508` (`ONLINE_WHOLE_MODS['unleveledLoot']`)
- Roleplay & Realism: Items is room-forced on with its loot arms: new weapons, new armour, loot rebalance and realistic enemy equipment. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:415` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS['roleplay-realism-items']`)
- Roleplay & Realism: Items is held whole online too, so its condition-based prices are the room's, not a player's choice. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:509` (`ONLINE_WHOLE_MODS['roleplay-realism-items']`)
- Roleplay & Realism's `weaponMaterials` switch is room-forced on, so its weapon-material rules run online. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:432` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS['roleplay-realism']`)
- While a shared room's base loot is being rolled, *every* mod key of *every* mod reads its shipped default (the PARTY-LOOT scope), so all clients compute the same list. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:538` (`asTheRoom`)

### Rarity tiers and the roll (the ladder)
Layer: **port** — Loot Rarity (`src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js`), forced on online by the registry row above; off, Daggerfall's own loot stands exactly.
- Seven tiers in order: Common (rank 0), Magic (1), Rare (2), Legendary (3), Aetheric (4), Artifact (5), Gilded (6). `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:100` (`RARITY_ORDER`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:104` (`RARITIES`)
- Only three tiers are rolled: Magic, Rare, Legendary. Aetheric, Artifact and Gilded are never produced by the drop roll. `src/systems/rarityTier.js:10` (`ROLLED_TIERS`)
- An item with no rolled tier derives one: an artifact is Artifact; any enchanted item (Daggerfall's own magic-item loot included) is at least Magic; the rest is Common. Daggerfall's own magic-item roll still runs and its products *are* Magic-tier items. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:122` (`rarityOf`)
- The roll is one draw in 1000 against three thresholds — the chance of reaching *at least* that tier, per mille: Magic = base 100 + 15 per source tier, capped 600; Rare = base 15 + 6 per tier, capped 260; Legendary = base 1 + 1.2 per tier, capped 45. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:287` (`RARITY_WEIGHTS`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:345` (`rollRarity`)
- Source multipliers on every threshold: a corpse ×1, a treasure pile ×1.3, a boss source ×2.5. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:292` (`SOURCE_MULT`)
- Luck multiplies every threshold before its cap: ×1 at Luck 50, +1% per Luck point above 50, −1% per point below, clamped between ×0.5 (Luck 0) and ×1.5 (Luck 100). `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:300` (`LUCK_PCT_PER_POINT`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:305` (`luckMult`)
- A corpse's source tier is the dead foe's own level; any Daedra, or any foe of level 18+, is a **boss** source (×2.5). `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:205` (`corpseSource`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:199` (`BOSS_LEVEL`)
- Worked example from those tables (labelled as an example): a level-18 boss corpse at Luck 50 rolls at least Magic 60% of the time (925 per mille, capped at 600), at least Rare 26% (capped), and Legendary 4.5% (capped). A tier-0 corpse at Luck 50: at least Magic 10%, Rare 1.5%, Legendary 0.1%. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:311` (`rarityChances`)
- A pile's source tier is its dungeon kind's grade, 0–18: Volcanic Caves and Dragon's Den 18, Desecrated Temple 15, Vampire Haunt 14, Coven 13, Barbarian Stronghold 11, Laboratory 10, Crypt and Orc Stronghold 9, Giant Stronghold 8, Harpy Nest 7, Human Stronghold and Ruined Castle 6, Prison, Spider Nest and Scorpion Nest 5, Mine and Natural Cave 4, Cemetery 3. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:174` (`DUNGEON_RARITY_TIER`)
- A treasure marker inside a town building (a tavern's or guild's) rolls at tier 4. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:197` (`INTERIOR_RARITY_TIER`)
- A Legendary, when one drops, is a fixed named record from the pools in the ladder file (the `LEGENDARIES`, `WARDROBE_LEGENDARIES` and `ARCANE_LEGENDARIES` lists) — a set name, a set affix signature and its own enchantment; records of the source's foe family weigh 5-to-1 in the pick. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:837` (`LEGENDARIES`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:273` (`SIGNATURE_WEIGHT`)
- Unique finds (items Daggerfall's roll cannot produce at all, e.g. the Dwarven Thunderlock) are a separate roll, once per loot list: nothing below source tier 4, then 0.35 per mille per tier point, capped at 6 per mille (0.6%), times the source multiplier. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:379` (`UNIQUE_WEIGHTS`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:397` (`uniqueFindChance`)
- The drought: every 25 items you take without a Legendary raises the Legendary threshold's multiplier by 0.2, up to ×3, until one drops. `src/systems/loot/lootDrought.js:36` (`DROUGHT_STEP`); `src/systems/loot/lootDrought.js:46` (`droughtMult`)
- One Rare or Legendary in twelve that a door ladders comes out **cursed** (never an Exalted one). `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:1686` (`cursePass`); `src/systems/foeLootCap.js:205` (`rollCorpseKit`)

### Plain foes: the capped ladder and the 3-item cap
Layer: **port** — `src/systems/foeLootCap.js`; Daggerfall caps nothing and has no ladder.
- A *plain* foe (no title of its own: not elite, champion, revenant, named, or a boss by the ladder's law) rolls its carried loot and its worn kit on a far lower table, per mille of at least the tier: Magic base 50 + 5 per level, cap 190; Rare base 6 + 1.2, cap 38; Legendary base 0.15 + 0.075, cap 3. `src/systems/foeLootCap.js:62` (`PLAIN_FOE_RARITY_WEIGHTS`)
- In plain terms at full strength: Magic tops out at 19%, Rare at 3.8%, Legendary at 0.3% — and at level 0 they are 5%, 0.6% and 0.015%. `src/systems/foeLootCap.js:62` (`PLAIN_FOE_RARITY_WEIGHTS`)
- A plain foe's body carries at most **3 items in total, gold included**; in an Elite Dungeon the cap is 5 and the foe rolls the ordinary ladder instead of the plain one. `src/systems/foeLootCap.js:53` (`PLAIN_FOE_LOOT_CAP`); `src/systems/foeLootCap.js:55` (`ELITE_DUNGEON_FOE_LOOT_CAP`)
- When the cap cuts a list, it keeps gold first (all coin stacks folded into one), then quest items, then Magic-or-better pieces by tier, then supplies (potions), then the rest, dearest first; arrows you shot into the body are not counted and are kept. `src/systems/foeLootCap.js:142` (`capLootList`)

### Renown-scaled loot and room loot (online branches)
Layer: **port, online-only branches** — offline there is no Renown and the unscaled numbers stand.
- Online, the LOOT-EASE easing of plain-foe loot (the plain ladder above and the share of a foe's items kept) is scaled by the *roller's* Renown stage: Renown 1–9 half the easing, 10–19 three quarters, 20–29 all of it, 30–39 five quarters, 40–50 half again. So at Renown 1 a level-0 plain foe's at-least-Magic chance is 4.5%, rising to 5.5% at Renown 40. `src/systems/renownLoot.js:43` (`RENOWN_LOOT_QUARTERS`); `src/systems/foeLootCap.js:77` (`plainFoeRarityWeights`)
- Offline, the easing stands whole (the "all of it" row) at every level of play. `src/systems/renownLoot.js:45` (`RENOWN_LOOT_OFFLINE_QUARTERS`)
- In a dungeon room, a container's base list is one list for everyone: every client computes it from the container's name on the room's terms (party level fixed at the container's birth, Luck 50, the first Renown stage, no finders, every mod read as the room's whole). Each player's own Luck and Renown then roll a private bonus layer over the base that only they see. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:538` (`asTheRoom`); `src/systems/renownLoot.js:43` (`RENOWN_LOOT_QUARTERS`)

### Item lines: affixes
Layer: **port** — rolled lines ride the item's `affixes` list and fold onto the wearer; they are not enchantments.
- A Magic piece rolls 1–2 affixes; a Rare rolls 3–4; a Legendary's lines are its record's fixed signature. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:475` (`AFFIX_COUNTS`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:722` (`rollAffixes`)
- Affix value bands by tier (Magic / Rare / Legendary): damage +5–12% / +10–25% / +20–40% on the weapon's own roll; armour +3–6 / +6–12 / +12–20 points off a blow's chance to land; one attribute +2–5 / +5–10 / +10–15; resistance +10–20 / +20–35 / +35–50 against one element (fire, frost, shock, poison, magic); one skill +5–10 / +10–20 / +20–30; carrying capacity +10–20% / +20–35% / +35–50%. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:449` (`AFFIX_RANGES`)
- The "active" lines use the same bands table: elemental damage on a weapon blow 1–3 / 3–6 / 6–10; leech (healing from a landed blow) 2–4% / 4–7% / 7–10%; thorns 1–3 / 3–6 / 6–10; focus (magicka cost off) 2–4% / 4–7% / 7–10%; slayer damage against one kind of foe +5–10% / +10–20% / +20–30%; plus garment lines (standing, warmth, dry) and casting lines (cast speed, potency, wellspring) at their own bands. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:449` (`AFFIX_RANGES`)
- A Rare also carries one of Daggerfall's own catalogue enchantments as flavour and a generated two-part name; because a Rare or Legendary carries a real enchantment, it drops **unidentified** under Daggerfall's own identification law until the Identify spell or the Mages Guild's service reveals it — its metal still shows, and its lines still work while unidentified. A Magic piece with only numeric lines has no enchantment and reads at once. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:61` (the IsIdentified law)
- A Legendary has a 100-per-mille (10%) chance to come out **Exalted**: one extra line of its own, on top of its record's signature. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:1479` (`EXALTED_PER_MILLE`)

### Materials: Unleveled Loot (the mod that runs online)
Layer: **mod — Unleveled Loot 1.1.2**, forced on and held whole online (see the layer section); it replaces Daggerfall's `RandomMaterial`/`RandomArmorMaterial` — the rolls behind every random weapon and armour piece in loot piles, enemy loadouts and shop stock — so materials do **not** scale to your level.
- The ten materials, in the mod's order: Iron, Steel, Silver, Elven, Dwarven, Mithril, Adamantium, Ebony, Orcish, Daedric. Its ten material-swap switches (which could turn any rolled material into another) read their shipped defaults online, because the mod is held whole. `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:69` (`UNLEVELED_MATERIAL_NAMES`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:508` (`ONLINE_WHOLE_MODS['unleveledLoot']`)
- The weapon-material roll: luckMod = ⌊Luck ÷ 10⌋; the roll starts at Range(0, 92) + luckMod, then falls in a dungeon by −15 + the dungeon's quality, or outdoors by −10 to −19; a passed luck check (Dice100 against 2 × luckMod) adds 4 × luckMod, or, in Orsinium or an Orc Stronghold on a second passed check, gives Orcish outright. `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:208` (`unleveledRandomMaterial`); `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:158` (`randomMat`)
- Reading the ladder (thresholds on that roll): over 90 rolls the high-tier table on a further luck check (Dice100 against 3 × luckMod), else Dwarven; over 83 Elven; over 75 Silver; over 35 Steel; otherwise Iron. `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:158` (`randomMat`)
- The high-tier table is a roll of 0–200: Daedric on 200 (1 in 201), Orcish on 193–199, Ebony on 181–192, Adamantium on 101–180, Mithril otherwise. `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:149` (`randomHighTier`)
- Dungeon quality by kind (what lifts the dungeon roll): Coven and Volcanic Caves 21; Desecrated Temple and Dragon's Den 18; Vampire Haunt and Barbarian Stronghold 15; Crypt and Orc Stronghold 12; Laboratory and Harpy Nest 10; Giant Stronghold 5; Human Stronghold, Prison and Ruined Castle = your level, capped at 15; anything else 0. `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:127` (`dungeonQuality`)
- Shop stock rolls on the shop instead: the roll restarts at −35 + 8 × the shop's level (building quality 1–4 → shop level 1, 5–8 → 2, 9–12 → 3, 13–16 → 4, 17+ → 5), with its own thresholds (over 99 Ebony, over 97 Adamantium, over 94 Mithril, over 88 Dwarven, over 80 Elven, over 70 Silver, under 10 Iron, else Steel), and Orsinium's shelves can carry Orcish. `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:140` (`shopLevel`); `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:173` (`shopMat`)
- Armour material: a roll of 1–90 + luckMod, lifted by the shop's quality ×6 in a shop or by the dungeon's quality in a dungeon; over 80 the piece is **plate** of the same material the weapon roll gives, over 50 **chain**, otherwise **leather**. `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:196` (`armMatSelector`)
- Corpse gold: on an enemy's death, every gold stack on the body is divided by *your* level (never below 1), then multiplied by a roll of 1 to ⌊Luck ÷ 10⌋. `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:309` (`unlevelDroppedLoot`); `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:324` (the gold division)
- Daedra and Orc corpses can add a piece of their own metal, when the mod's Daedric/Orcish switches stand at their shipped values: the roll is Range(0, 101) + (luckMod − 5) — a Daedra Lord needs over 70, a Seducer over 75, a Fire or Frost Daedra or a Daedroth over 80; an Orc Warlord over 80, a Shaman over 90, a Sergeant over 95, a plain Orc over 98. The piece is a weapon on a further roll over 50 (a Seducer's is always a weapon), otherwise armour, at 30–75% condition. `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:288` (`addDaedric`); `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:297` (`addOrcish`)
- The port's level ramp on those two drops (a port departure from the mod): below character level 20, a granted Daedric/Orcish drop keeps its metal only on a roll of Range(0, 20) under your level; a withheld drop takes a middle metal by level instead — Elven at 1–4, Dwarven 5–8, Mithril 9–12, Adamantium 13–16, Ebony 17–19. At level 20+ the mod stands as written. `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:278` (`METAL_RAMP_FULL`); `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:280` (`rampMetal`)

### Materials: value, weight of armour, condition
Layer: **base** — Daggerfall's own material tables, which the port keeps; they price and rate whatever the online rolls above produce.
- A weapon's or plate piece's value is its base price × 3 × its material multiplier: Iron ×1, Steel ×2, Silver ×4, Elven ×8, Dwarven ×16, Mithril ×32, Adamantium ×64, Ebony ×128, Orcish ×256, Daedric ×512. `src/characters/weapons.js:71` (`valueMultipliersByMaterial`); `src/systems/item/itemTemplates.js:108` (`materialValue`)
- A weapon's condition pool scales with its material too: ×4, ×6, ×6, ×8, ×12, ×16, ×20, ×24, ×28, ×32 down the same ladder, divided by 4 — so a Daedric weapon has 8× an Iron one's condition. `src/characters/weapons.js:72` (`conditionMultipliersByMaterial`); `src/characters/weapons.js:247` (`maxCondition`)
- Armour value by material: leather 3, chain 6, plate — Iron 7, Steel 9, Silver 9, Elven 11, Dwarven 13, Mithril 15, Adamantium 15, Ebony 17, Orcish 19, Daedric 21. Shields are material-blind: Buckler 1, Round 2, Kite 3, Tower 4. `src/systems/armorMaterials.js:38` (`PLATE_ARMOR_VALUES`); `src/systems/armorMaterials.js:39` (`materialArmorValue`)

### Sets: Sigil Sets
Layer: **port, online-only** — a set piece's tiers are asleep offline and in duels.
- A won piece of armour or a shield of Magic tier or better can carry a **set sigil**: the chance is 200 per mille, +40 per mille for each fighter in the party past the first, and the set is one of the four sets of the world at even odds. A weapon whose fresh sigil lands joins a set one time in three. `src/systems/sigil/sigilSets.js:489` (`rollSetSigil`); `src/systems/sigil/sigil.js:163` (`sigilChance`); `src/systems/sigil/sigilSets.js:481` (`SET_WEAPON_JOIN_IN`)
- Set bonuses wake at **2, 4 and 6 worn pieces** of one set (the body's seven pieces, the shield, and one weapon — two weapons of a set count once). `src/systems/sigil/sigilSets.js:54` (`SET_TIERS`); `src/systems/sigil/sigilSets.js:64` (`SET_PLACES`)
- A set's stage is the *lowest* stage among its worn pieces ("grow together"), and each piece's stage is the lower of its own sigil rank and the stage the wearer's Renown opens. `src/systems/sigil/sigilSets.js:495` (`rollSetJoin`)
- A boss's own set is minted whole at the **Aetheric** tier — never rolled by the ladder — and the Sigil Broker is its other source. `src/systems/rarityTier.js:10` (`ROLLED_TIERS`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:104` (`RARITIES`)

### Sockets and gems
Layer: **port** — part of the Loot Rarity system; nothing here exists with the ladder off.
- A laddered weapon, armour piece or jewel can carry empty **sockets**. The general pass: a Rare 150 per mille, a Legendary 300 per mille. Weapons have their own pass: a first socket at Magic 120, Rare 250, Legendary 400 per mille, then each further socket up to the piece's size at Rare 300 / Legendary 450 per mille. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:1729` (`SOCKET_PER_MILLE`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:1849` (`WEAPON_SOCKET_PER_MILLE`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:1850` (`MORE_SOCKET_PER_MILLE`)
- How many sockets a piece can hold: a short blade 1, a one-handed weapon 2, a two-handed weapon 3; armour and jewellery 1; a Magic weapon at most 1, a Rare or Legendary its full size; nothing else (no garments, no ammunition). `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:1793` (`WEAPON_SOCKETS`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:1794` (`socketMax`)
- The eight gem kinds: Ruby, Emerald, Sapphire, Diamond, Jade, Turquoise, Malachite, Amber — each in five grades: Chipped, Flawed, plain (Daggerfall's own), Flawless, Perfect. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:1736` (`GEM_IDS`); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:1740` (`GEM_GRADES`)
- Finding a gem: every loot door also rolls for one — 10 per mille + 2 per source tier, capped at 50 per mille, times the source multiplier (pile ×1.3, boss ×2.5) and your Luck multiplier. The grade follows the source's tier band (weights for Chipped / Flawed / plain / Flawless / Perfect): tier 0+ = 70/30/0/0/0; tier 5+ = 30/50/20/0/0; tier 10+ = 0/30/45/25/0; tier 15+ = 0/0/35/50/15; tier 20+ = 0/0/0/60/40 — and a boss reads 4 tiers deeper. No shop stocks a graded gem. `src/systems/gems.js:78` (`GEM_FIND`); `src/systems/gems.js:81` (`GEM_GRADE_BANDS`); `src/systems/gems.js:90` (`gemFindChance`)
- Named bosses carry gems in their spoils: the gate Warden 1, the Old Coil 1, an abyss boss 2, at the top grade band. `src/systems/gems.js:123` (`BOSS_GEMS`)
- A graded gem's price against its kind's plain price: Chipped ×0.25, Flawed ×0.5, plain ×1, Flawless ×2, Perfect ×4. `src/systems/gems.js:42` (`GEM_GRADE_PRICE`)
- What a set gem gives (plain grade shown; Chipped/Flawed scale down, Flawless/Perfect up, per the grade table): in a weapon — Ruby +3 fire on a blow, Sapphire +3 frost, Emerald leech 3, Diamond +6% damage, Jade +4 Willpower, Turquoise +4 Agility, Malachite +4 Strength, Amber +4 Speed; in any other piece — Ruby/Sapphire/Emerald/Diamond give +10 resistance to fire/frost/poison/magic, and Jade/Turquoise/Malachite/Amber give +4 to Willpower/Personality/Endurance/Luck. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:1765` (`GEM_GRADE_LINES`)
- Setting a gem is **free** (from the pack or at the Reforge, worn or not); unsetting it is free but the gem **shatters**; only the Reforge's **extraction** returns it whole, for gold by grade: Chipped 50, Flawed 100, plain 200, Flawless 400, Perfect 800. `src/systems/reforge.js:218` (`EXTRACT_PRICE`); `src/systems/reforge.js:298` (`extractPrice`)

### Salvage and Welkynd Shards
Layer: **port** — the Reforge's economy (`src/systems/reforge.js`); Daggerfall has no salvage.
- Salvaging a piece the ladder graded pays **Welkynd Shards**: Magic 1, Rare 3, Legendary 8, an Exalted Legendary 15. `src/systems/reforge.js:54` (`SALVAGE_SHARDS`)
- Never salvageable: a Common piece, Daggerfall's own (ungraded) magic items, a made/crafted piece, an Aetheric or Gilded piece, an artifact, a quest item, a bound, worn or locked piece, and a piece with a gem still set (extract or unset it first). Shards come only from what was *found*. `src/systems/reforge.js:59` (`salvageShards`); `src/systems/reforge.js:75` (`salvageRefusal`)
- A Welkynd Shard is a bound item (template 571): it stacks, is worth 250 gold on paper, and can never be sold, traded, dropped or listed. `src/systems/gate/gateSpoils.js:76` (`WELKYND_SHARD_TEMPLATE`); `src/systems/gate/gateSpoils.js:77` (`WELKYND_SHARD`)
- Five shards buy a **Portal Stone** at any shop's counter. `src/systems/gate/gateSpoils.js:104` (`PORTAL_STONE_SHARDS`)

### The Reforge and the Hone
Layer: **port** — at the Mages Guild (see Gates/Interface); the piece must be identified and not worn.
- **Reforge** — reroll one line of a known Magic or Rare piece, or an Exalted Legendary's own extra line: Magic costs 2 shards + 100 gold; Rare 4 shards + 400 gold; Exalted 10 shards + 2000 gold. Once a piece is reforged, only that same line can be reforged again. A plain (non-Exalted) Legendary cannot be reforged. `src/systems/reforge.js:143` (`REFORGE_PRICE`); `src/systems/reforge.js:149` (`reforgePrice`)
- **Hone** — raise one line within its own band, never lower: first hone Magic 1 shard + 50 gold, Rare 2 + 150, Exalted 4 + 500 — and the price **doubles with every hone the piece has already taken**. `src/systems/reforge.js:172` (`HONE_PRICE`); `src/systems/reforge.js:182` (`honePrice`)
- A gem's line is never reforged or honed — no roll made it. `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:1729` (`SOCKET_PER_MILLE`)

### Repair
Layer: **base formula, port price scale, mod override online** — see each bullet.
- Free at full condition; otherwise Daggerfall's base price is 10% of the item's base value (minimum 1) through the shop's price calculation — the classic price does **not** scale with how damaged the piece is — and the Fighters Guild's rank discount can reduce it. `src/systems/repairService.js:60` (`dfuItemRepairCost`)
- Online, Roleplay & Realism: Items' condition prices are held whole, so its override is the base that runs: the tenth is scaled by the damage instead — 0.6 × the missing condition fraction × base value (minimum 1). `src/systems/roleplayRealismItems/rriRealism.js:149` (`conditionRepairCostBase`); `src/systems/roleplayRealismItems/rriRealism.js:23` (`REPAIR_COST_FACTOR`)
- Every repair, offline and online, at every shop and guild, then costs **a third** of that formula's answer (floored at 1). `src/systems/repairService.js:51` (`REPAIR_COST_SCALE`); `src/systems/repairService.js:55` (`calculateItemRepairCost`)
- Timed repair (when Instant Repairs is off): 1000 condition points per day, minimum one full day, at the smith where you left the piece. `src/systems/repairService.js:74` (`calculateItemRepairTime`)
- An enchanted (magic) item is refused at the smith unless magic repairs are allowed — and online they always are: the lane forces `AllowMagicRepairs = True`. Horses and wagons are never repaired at a smith (a Wagon Yard mends wagons). `src/systems/repairService.js:132` (`repairRefusal`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:178` (`ONLINE_FORCED_SETTINGS`)

### Selling gear: condition prices and the found mark (resale credit)
Layer: **mod prices (Roleplay & Realism: Items, held whole online) read through a port online branch.**
- A counter's price for a piece runs through its condition: under RRI's condition-based prices a piece at 50% condition is priced off half its value, and nothing prices below a fifth (20%). (The condition-cost law itself is T1's citation: `src/systems/roleplayRealismItems/rriRealism.js:139` (`conditionCostBase`).)
- A piece the world handed over worn carries a **found mark** — the condition it was handed over at (RRI's rolls on piles, bodies and shelves, Unleveled Loot's death drops, a foe's worn kit). Online, a counter pays for a marked piece at its *mark*, not its current condition: a smith's paid repair adds nothing to the sale, at any counter, in any region, at any guild discount. `src/systems/item/foundMark.js:32` (`foundSalePercentage`); `src/systems/tradeModes.js:251` (`saleConditionPercentage`)
- Your *own* mends past the mark — a Repair Kit, the Repairs Objects effect, a Varla Stone — are credited at **one third** of what they hold above the mark; wear after the find still lowers the price. An unmarked piece (crafted, a reward, a new shelf's) sells at its own condition. `src/systems/item/foundMark.js:22` (`MEND_SALE_SHARE`); `src/systems/item/foundMark.js:32` (`foundSalePercentage`)
- A crafted piece sells for the plain piece its recipe makes — a counter pays nothing for its rolled Magic or Rare lines. `src/systems/tradeModes.js:372` (the Sell arm); `src/systems/loot/lootRarity.js:122` (`rarityOf`)

## Online vs offline
- **The ladder**: offline a player may switch Loot Rarity off (Daggerfall's loot stands exactly, nothing salvages or reforges); online the registry forces it on. `src/systems/features.js:770` (`lootRarity` control); `src/systems/reforge.js:75` (`salvageRefusal`)
- **Materials**: offline Unleveled Loot is the player's own mod toggle, and its ten material-swap switches are theirs; online it is forced on and held whole — the swaps read the shipped defaults for everyone. `src/systems/unleveledLoot.js:335` (`installUnleveledLoot`); `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:508` (`ONLINE_WHOLE_MODS['unleveledLoot']`)
- **Plain-foe loot**: offline the easing stands whole; online it scales with the roller's Renown (half at Renown 1–9 up to half again at 40–50). `src/systems/renownLoot.js:45` (`RENOWN_LOOT_OFFLINE_QUARTERS`); `src/systems/renownLoot.js:43` (`RENOWN_LOOT_QUARTERS`)
- **Room loot**: a shared dungeon room's containers are one base list for everyone online, rolled on the room's terms with every mod read whole; offline there is no room and no such roll. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:538` (`asTheRoom`)
- **Corpse loot is the owner's word**: a corpse's loot is rolled and granted by its owner's machine, so the owner's Unleveled Loot rolls are what a peer receives. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:404` (`ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS['unleveledLoot']`)
- **Repair of magic items**: offline it depends on the player's AllowMagicRepairs setting; online the lane forces it True for everyone. `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:178` (`ONLINE_FORCED_SETTINGS`)
- **Selling found gear**: offline a counter reads the piece's actual condition (the mod's prices stand); online it reads the found mark, plus a third of your own mends past it. `src/systems/tradeModes.js:251` (`saleConditionPercentage`); `src/systems/item/foundMark.js:22` (`MEND_SALE_SHARE`)
- **Sigil Sets**: asleep offline and in duels; online they wake at 2/4/6 pieces and grow with Renown. `src/systems/sigil/sigilSets.js:54` (`SET_TIERS`)
