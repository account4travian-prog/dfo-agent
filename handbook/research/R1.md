# R1: Revenants and companions
Commit: 0df2d87eb | Researcher: Luna | Date: 2026-10-21

## Player-facing summary
- A marked enemy that survives a consequential fight can become a remembered revenant; defeat it and choose **Spare** to recruit it as a sworn companion. (`src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:508-535,798-844` | mod: Revenants; online enabled through Unleveled Loot / Loot Rarity lane.)
- New revenants require Renown 10 and an enemy at least character level 3; you can have at most three active hostile hunts at once. (`src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:112-124,259,320-324` | mod: Revenants.)
- You can keep up to six sworn revenants in your retinue, but only companions within your active companion slots can stand beside you. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantCompanions.js:50-58,81-99`; `src/systems/companionSlots.js:1-40` | mod: Revenants / port.)
- Companions gain loyalty from winning fights with you and can learn stars, wear gear, and go on missions; their mission odds and returns depend on their stats and the chosen venture. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantCompanions.js:222-250,328-411`; `src/systems/revenant/revenantStars.js:92-203` | mod: Revenants.)
- A hostile revenant can grow in rank, adapt to your habits and steal an eligible item if it kills you. Those are hunt rules, distinct from a loyal companion's behavior. (`src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:915-990`; `src/systems/revenant/revenantFeud.js:1-120` | mod: Revenants.)

## Rules and numbers

### Becoming hunted and recruiting
- One quarter of eligible elites or champions receives the Unquiet mark; the roll and eligible foe checks are in `unquietMark.js`. (`src/systems/revenant/unquietMark.js:1-59` | mod: Revenants.)
- A new revenant needs Renown 10, a foe level of at least 3, and a consequential deed; at most three non-Dread revenants can be hunting the player at once. (`src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:112-124,508-535` | mod: Revenants.)
- A marked foe can become a revenant through the recorded deeds; an ordinary kill without a qualifying deed is not enough. (`src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:535-570,798-825` | mod: Revenants.)
- A marked newcomer rolls to flee once below 35% health: 50% chance, runs for 8 seconds at 2.5x walk speed, and escapes beyond 45 m or if it ends more than 12 m away. (`src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:133-158,743-790` | mod: Revenants.)
- Standard revenant return becomes due after 2-4 character-clock days; once due, the eligible encounter chance is 40%. (`src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:163-167,915-932` | mod: Revenants.)
- Vengeance ambition uses a 1-2 day return and 55% encounter chance; `revenantUnquiet.js` defines which personalities receive it. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantUnquiet.js:1-80`; `src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:915-932` | mod: Revenants.)
- Rank is capped at V; existing rivals gain ranks from recorded deeds, but rank is separate from a sworn companion's companion level. (`src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:124,798-844`; `src/systems/revenant/revenantStars.js:1-20` | mod: Revenants.)
- The final blow leaves your own defeated revenant available to judge; activating it offers Kill or Spare, and a successful Spare records a sworn revenant. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantFate.js:1-100`; `src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:816-844` | mod: Revenants.)
- The judgement can be lost after 90 seconds or beyond 60 m; cite the fate constants and steps before presenting those limits. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantFate.js:1-70` | mod: Revenants.)

### Sworn companions
- The sworn retinue cap is six, independent of active companion slots; a recruited revenant is assigned Away if no slot is free. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantCompanions.js:50,81-99` | mod: Revenants / port.)
- For each original revenant rank above I, sworn health is increased by 25% and ordinary damage by 10%; the companion-star system can further change its statistics. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantCompanions.js:57-58`; `src/systems/revenant/revenantStars.js:92-181` | mod: Revenants.)
- Companion loyalty ranges from 0 to 100; starts and event changes are defined by personality and combat/day events, so use the exact tables before quoting values. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantFeud.js:1-120`; `src/systems/revenant/revenantPersonality.js:1-90` | mod: Revenants.)
- A sworn revenant uses the companion body and combat path; its original rank and companion level are distinct fields. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantCompanions.js:81-103,380-411`; `src/scenes/crewAshore.js:1-80` | mod: Revenants / port.)
- Arts are identity-determined and only 35% of revenants are born with one; `revenantArts.js` defines the effects and cooldowns. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantArts.js:1-100` | mod: Revenants.)
- Companions gain XP and star points through the companion advancement logic; star cost, level cap and reset behavior must be copied from its exact UI/law lines before publishing. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantStars.js:92-203` | mod: Revenants.)

### Gear, missions and interface actions
- The Companions page offers actions for calling or sending away, Gear, Mission, Stars and Release; confirm literal button casing in the localized text map. (`src/ui/companionRoster.js:2-24,31-52` | mod: Revenants.)
- Missions are sent to one of the listed provinces with a venture; mission duration, odds, returns and refusal rules are in `revenantErrands.js` and `revenantCompanions.js`. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantErrands.js:71-126`; `src/systems/revenant/revenantCompanions.js:222-324` | mod: Revenants.)
- Companion gear is handled by gift/take functions and `revenantGear.js`; this review has not independently reconciled every equipment cap and item refusal in the earlier page. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantCompanions.js:328-369`; `src/systems/revenant/revenantGear.js:1-120` | mod: Revenants.)
- In the current UI implementation, the roster is on the Enhanced pause menu's Holdings rail. (`src/ui/companionRoster.js:2-5`; `src/ui/enhanced/enhancedMenu.js:1-40` | port / mod: Revenants.)
- A sworn companion follows through doors and can catch up by portal; exact catch-up distances should be verified at the crew placement constants before being reused. (`src/scenes/crewAshore.js:1-80` | port / mod: Revenants.)

## Gates
- Revenant mark and rival-creation path requires Renown 10 online and an eligible foe of character level 3 or higher. (`src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:112-124,259,508-535` | mod: Revenants.)
- A sworn companion requires defeating and sparing a valid revenant; active presence also requires an open companion slot. (`src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:816-844`; `src/systems/revenant/revenantCompanions.js:97-99` | mod: Revenants / port.)
- The source reviewed here does not show a separate Renown gate for calling a companion, assigning stars, or missions. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantCompanions.js:144-169,222-250,370-411` | mod: Revenants.)

## Where it shows in the interface
- Open Enhanced pause menu - Holdings - Companions for the sworn roster; row actions and pages are implemented in `companionRoster.js`. (`src/ui/companionRoster.js:2-24,49-52` | port / mod: Revenants.)
- The Revenants roster is separate from the Companions page; check the localized label and its UI entry before writing a menu route. (`src/ui/companionRoster.js:2-5` | mod: Revenants.)
- Select a companion's Gear, Mission or Stars action from its roster card; those open their respective sheets/windows. (`src/ui/companionRoster.js:17-24,127-190` | mod: Revenants.)

## Online vs offline
- Revenant creation is explicitly suppressed offline: `revenantCandidate` and return handling use online state and the online lane's Unleveled Loot/Loot Rarity policy. (`src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:253-259,508-535`; `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:397-415` | mod: Revenants / online lane.)
- Player-owned offline save state does not establish online encounter schedules; online hunt timing follows the shared character clock and online encounter path. (`src/systems/revenant/revenant.js:915-932,1088-1110` | mod: Revenants.)
- A sworn companion uses online companion replication/host actions when present with the player; its roster actions are not proof that local/offline behavior is identical. (`src/systems/revenant/revenantCompanions.js:68-103`; `src/scenes/crewAshore.js:1-80` | mod: Revenants / port.)
