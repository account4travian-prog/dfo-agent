## Player-facing summary
- An Elite is a promoted foe with better survivability, damage and loot; elite dungeon tier changes those multipliers. (`src/systems/eliteFoes.js:34-56` | mod: Elite Foes; live online because the room forces the feature and mod switch.)
- The world also contains spawned dungeons. The game distinguishes **Regular Dungeon**, **Elite Dungeon** and **Abyss Dungeon** online. (`src/world/spawnedDungeons.js:22-43`; `src/systems/dungeon/dungeonTier.js:19-38` | port; online tier labels are a port feature.)
- The sea serpent, Starfall and festivals use shared event clocks. Check the live phase and event window before travelling to an event site. (`src/net/serpent/serpentLaw.js:26-50`; `src/net/starfall/starfallLaw.js:31-51`; `src/net/festivalLaw.js:24-47` | port.)
- Dagon's Breach rises daily, with a 17:00 omen, 19:00 rise, 20:00 opening and 22:00 seal on the shared 24-hour game-day clock. (`src/net/gate/gateLaw.js:20-39,46-72` | port.)
- The sea serpent rises on alternating shared-world days, with the sighting, rise, seal and sound phases at set times in its day. (`src/net/serpent/serpentLaw.js:26-40,58-85` | port.)
- Wild-zone monsters and dungeon halls use a special, substantially more dangerous rule set; do not apply ordinary elite multipliers to them. (`src/systems/wild/wildZone.js:27-48`; `src/systems/wild/wildDungeons.js:181-201` | mod: Wild Zone.)

## Rules and numbers

### Elites and champions
- The online Elite Foes system sets base elite health to x5 and damage to x3; inside an Elite Dungeon the values are x7 and x4, and in an Abyss Dungeon x9 and x4.5. (`src/systems/eliteFoes.js:34-44` | mod: Elite Foes; `ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS` forces its switch.)
- A promoted elite grows to 1.25x size; overworld promotion chance is 5%, while regular dungeon promotion selects 3-4 foes and has a 20% chance. (`src/systems/eliteFoes.js:46-54,86-105,152-179` | mod: Elite Foes.)
- Elites require character level 3 and Renown 5; the online gate is Renown-based through `eliteRenownOpen`. (`src/systems/eliteFoes.js:71-82` | mod: Elite Foes.)
- Elite loot's configured magic multiplier is x2, with a 6% legendary chance and 20-60 gold per level; the exact rare chance is level-derived by `eliteRareChance`. (`src/systems/eliteFoes.js:56-68,205-218` | mod: Elite Foes.)
- Champions use a 70-per-mille promotion weight, minimum character level 3, x2 health and x1.25 damage; the named trait data is in `CHAMPION_TRAITS`. (`src/systems/champions.js:39-57,83-114` | mod: Champions.)

### Spawned dungeons and danger tiers
- Spawned dungeons use a 30% normal-spawn chance plus a separate 10% elite-spawn chance; the combined spawn chance is 40%. (`src/world/spawnedDungeons.js:22-43,68-71` | port.)
- A spawned elite dungeon uses x2 health and damage and x1.2 drop and quality multipliers in that spawn system; these are distinct from `eliteFoes.js`'s elite-foe multipliers. (`src/world/spawnedDungeons.js:37-44` | port.)
- Spawned dungeons have a 300 m clearance; cleared locations expire after 2 days, general locations after 7 days, and rebirth after 12 days. (`src/world/spawnedDungeons.js:222-224,280-303` | port.)
- Online dungeon labels are Regular Dungeon, Elite Dungeon and Abyss Dungeon; code calls the last tier `super`, and `dungeonTier()` gives that tier precedence over `elite`. (`src/systems/dungeon/dungeonTier.js:18-38` | port.)
- Wrothgarian Wild Zone halls are elite dungeons selected at 10 / 8 / 6 / 4 halls across its four rings; cemetery and graveyard templates are excluded. (`src/systems/wild/wildDungeons.js:24-30,175-179` | mod: Wild Zone.)
- Wild-zone foes use x4 health, x4 damage, x2 gold, drop and rare-loot multipliers; the ring loot multipliers are x1.25 / x1.5 / x1.75 / x2. (`src/systems/wild/wildZone.js:27-48` | mod: Wild Zone.)
- Wild halls replace low-tier monsters with ring-specific high-tier foes while retaining class enemies; cite the full foe table before naming species. (`src/systems/wild/wildDungeons.js:181-201` | mod: Wild Zone.)

### Shared events and clocks
- The seat system's phase clock divides the week into Muster and Reckoning, with a Turning every Sunday at 18:00 UTC; this is the clock used by the seat-control and siege cycle. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:227-238,355-358` | port.)
- Dagon's Breach uses the shared 1,440-minute game day and occurs daily; its omen/rise/open/seal are at minutes 1,020 / 1,140 / 1,200 / 1,320, with Wrath at minute 1,440. (`src/net/gate/gateLaw.js:20-39,46-72` | port.)
- The gate's possible portents are The Vanguard, The Sundering and The Captive; one may be drawn, or The Dire Breach combines Vanguard and Sundering. (`src/net/gate/gateMods.js:135-168,171` | port.)
- A raid window lasts 120 game minutes, begins no later than minute 1,320, and targets 15-25 kills; the shared room forces Raiding Parties on online. (`src/net/raid/raidLaw.js:38-60`; `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:336-340` | mod: Raiding Parties.)
- The sea-serpent clock uses 1,440 classic minutes per day and runs every two days; omen/rise/seal/sound are at hours 2 / 5 / 8 / 10 of that serpent day. (`src/net/serpent/serpentLaw.js:26-50,58-75` | port.)
- The serpent's active window begins at its rise and ends at its sound; `serpentAdmits` and `serpentHolds` define when it can be engaged and when its site remains. (`src/net/serpent/serpentLaw.js:108-116` | port.)
- Witches Festival Starfall night is derived from the holiday calendar, and the heart star is the final star of a Starfall night; it is tied to Witches Festival day. (`src/systems/starfall/starfallNight.js:19-26` | port.)
- Starfall has three falling stars per night, each with a 15-minute life; an omen begins 90 seconds before a fall, and the gather window is 10 seconds. (`src/net/starfall/starfallLaw.js:35-51,104-120` | port.)
- The festival client uses UTC day boundaries for daily finds; do not state a daily cap without checking the account-side law. (`src/systems/festival/festivalClient.js:124-168` | port.)
- The festival law sets a daily ceiling of 1,500 find requests and 120 soul cakes; night finds are doubled, and offerings stock at 60 with 10 added per fill and one taken per action. (`src/net/festivalLaw.js:36-40,72-84` | port.)
- Three Motherlodes are scheduled per day; each has a 2-hour open window, warning 10 minutes ahead (30 minutes for Sense), a 10-minute watch window, and yields 4-6 ore. (`src/net/motherlodeLaw.js:38-58,65-72,85-120,127` | port.)
- Motherlodes use ore from adamantium, ebony or orichalcum in mountain, mountain-woods, desert and desert-2 climates; nodes are level 6 and rank 25. (`src/net/motherlodeLaw.js:44-69` | port.)
- Exact Dagon's Breach event schedule and Motherlode event schedule were not established from the verified lines gathered here; cite the corresponding net laws and scheduler before publishing a timing or reward. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:257-284` establishes seat influence only; no layer claim for the missing schedules.)

## Online vs offline
- Dungeon tiers are explicitly online-only; offline dungeons retain DFU behavior and do not display the online tier label. (`src/systems/dungeon/dungeonTier.js:6-16,38-39` | port.)
- Elite promotion is allowed on the online page or in a room, while otherwise it depends on the Loot Rarity switch. (`src/systems/eliteFoes.js:86-98`; `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:397-415` | mod: Elite Foes / online lane.)
- Use the event-specific online clock, not classic Daggerfall calendar assumptions; the serpent law reads the shared classic-minute clock. (`src/net/serpent/serpentLaw.js:26-50` | port.)
