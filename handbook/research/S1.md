# S1: Playing with others
Commit: 0df2d87eb · Researcher: Luna · Date: 2026-10-21

## Player-facing summary
- Chat channels reach different audiences; Trade and LFG are optional channels you join. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:37-51`; layer: port.)
- Parties hold up to eight accounts; invitations expire after two minutes. (`src/net/wire.js:715-716,805-809`; layer: port.)
- Recent party attackers increase shared foe strength; Renown kill XP rises with party size rather than being divided. (`src/systems/party/partyScale.js:20-43,74-97`; `src/net/renown.js:217-233`; layer: port.)
- Sharing a quest sends a copy to party members, each of whom keeps their own quest state. (`src/systems/quest/questShare.js:91-114,220-230`; `src/net/wire.js:57,91`; layer: port.)
- Online quest instances have an owner and admit that owner's party; the instance door can offer a public room or your own quest room. (`src/systems/instancePlaces.js:130-137`; `src/net/instanceRoom.js:103-111`; layer: port.)
- Duels are accepted, bounded bouts refereed by the relay; Arena bouts and ratings use a separate match system. (`src/net/duel/duelRef.js:34-39,64-90`; `src/net/arena/arenaLaw.js:82-123`; layer: port.)
- A block hides a player's chat and requests. Use `/report` for player conduct and `/bug` for a private developer report. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:165,201-229`; `src/net/safety/safetyLaw.js:15-43`; `src/net/privateReport.js:2-20`; layer: port.)

## Rules and numbers

### Chat
- `/world` or `/g` sends to everyone online; `/region` or `/r` to your region; `/party` or `/p` to your party; `/guild` or `/gu` to your guild; `/local`, `/l`, `/say` or `/s` to nearby players. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:37-45`; layer: port.)
- `/trade` or `/tr` and `/lfg` send to opt-in channels; `/join trade|lfg` and `/leave trade|lfg` add or remove those channels. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:46-51`; `src/net/chat.js:59-68`; layer: port.)
- `/lc` sends to the language channel; `/lang [code|off]` chooses one, and `/help` or `/?` shows the command list. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:11-22,41`; layer: port.)
- `/block`, `/unblock` and `/blocked` hide a player's lines, invites and requests; the safety layer caps a personal block list at 200 accounts. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:220`; `src/net/safety/safetyLaw.js:15-21`; layer: port.)
- `/report <name>` or a chat line's Report action submits a conduct report; `/bug` opens a private report form for developers. (`src/net/chatCommands.js:165-171,217-221`; `src/net/privateReport.js:2-20`; layer: port.)

### Parties and shared play
- The party limit is eight accounts including you; invites expire after 120 seconds. (`src/net/wire.js:715-716,805-809` `PARTY_MAX`, `INVITE_TTL_MS`; layer: port.)
- The party's fight-size window counts players who struck the foe in the prior 30 seconds; each additional fighter raises shared foe HP by 50% and damage by 10%. (`src/systems/party/partyScale.js:20-43,74-93` `PARTY_FIGHT_WINDOW_MS`, `PARTY_SCALE_HP_PCT`, `PARTY_SCALE_DAMAGE_PCT`; layer: port.)
- Outdoor encounters add one foe for each two party members after the first, up to three extra foes. (`src/systems/party/partyScale.js:74-97` `PARTY_SCALE_EXTRA_EVERY`, `PARTY_SCALE_EXTRA_MAX`; layer: port.)
- Party Renown kill XP is multiplied by 1 plus 10% for every additional party member present; kill credit is not split. (`src/net/renown.js:217-233` `renownPartyXp`; layer: port.)
- A shared quest is reconstructed as a separate local quest copy; questline steps are excluded from sharing. (`src/systems/quest/questShare.js:91-114,205-230`; layer: port.)
- A quest instance is keyed to its owner and quest epoch; instance admission allows the owner and the owner's party. (`src/systems/instancePlaces.js:43-81,130-137`; `src/net/instanceRoom.js:99-111`; layer: port.)
- A public dungeon's foes and containers respawn after 20 real minutes, or 40 minutes in an Elite dungeon; each dead foe or emptied container has its own timer. (`src/systems/dungeon/dungeonRespawn.js:2-19`; layer: port.)
- Shared room containers use a shared base list for everyone, with a separate personal bonus layer; looting a shared base item can affect what remains for other players. (`src/scenes/roomLootHost.js:32-44`; layer: port.)

### Duels and Arena
- Duelists must be within 10 m to start; the ring radius is 12 m. A challenge lapses after 30 seconds, and a bout lasts at most five minutes. (`src/net/duel/duelSession.js:38-63`; layer: port.)
- The relay referee admits and judges duel starts, blows and results; a duel is not ordinary open-world player damage. (`src/net/duel/duelRef.js:34-39,64-90,101-129`; layer: port.)
- Arena seasons last eight weeks; starting rating is 1,000, K is 32, and ratings are bounded from 100 to 4,000. (`src/net/arena/arenaLaw.js:82-118`; layer: port.)
- The Arena ladder is a separate sequence of AI bouts; its tier specification is stored in the port's Arena law. (`src/net/arena/arenaLaw.js:180-204,238-239`; layer: port.)

### Safety and reports
- A block list hides the blocked account's requests and party invites; the blocked player is not told. (`src/net/social.js:123-148,180-205`; layer: port.)
- Conduct reports may include up to 16 nearby chat lines, and reported lines can be no older than 24 hours. (`src/net/safety/safetyLaw.js:23-43`; layer: port.)
- Private developer reports require a registered account; report forms ask for a 3–80 character subject and a 10–3,000 word description. (`src/net/accountClient.js:263-270`; `src/net/reportLaw.js:20-23`; layer: port.)

## Gates
- Parties, chat and social tools are DFO online systems with no character-level gate stated in the source. (`src/net/wire.js:40-57,89-92`; `src/net/social.js:7-19`; layer: port.)
- Arena seasons/rating rules apply to Arena play; the source gates a persistent ladder climb on a registered account. (`src/net/arena/arenaLaw.js:82-123`; `src/net/accountClient.js:237`; layer: port.)
- Quest sharing requires an active quest that passes `canReceiveSharedQuest`; questline steps and already-finished shared copies are refused. (`src/systems/quest/questShare.js:220-230`; layer: port.)

## Where it shows in the interface
- Chat tabs include World, Region, Local, Party, Guild, Lang, Trade and LFG; optional tabs appear when joined. (`src/net/chat.js:56-72`; `src/ui/chatPanel.js:159-169`; layer: port.)
- The Social panel offers Friends and Party views; the party HUD marks its leader and lists party members. (`src/ui/socialPanel.js:1-23`; `src/ui/partyPanel.js:1-16,30-41,74-86`; layer: port.)
- The Arena window builds its labeled pages from `ARENA_TEXT.window.tabs` and shows season ratings and bout cards. (`src/ui/arena/arenaWindow.js:57,120-137,168-178,217-240`; layer: port.)

## Online vs offline
- Chat rooms, parties, quest sharing, instances, player reports, duels and the Arena are DFO port systems; the source does not define these services for offline Daggerfall Unity. (`src/net/wire.js:40-57,89-92`; `src/systems/instancePlaces.js:55-81`; layer: port.)
- Arena and duel actions are judged by relay rules, rather than the ordinary player-versus-enemy combat path. (`src/net/duel/duelRef.js:64-90`; `src/net/arena/arenaLaw.js:8-20`; layer: port.)
