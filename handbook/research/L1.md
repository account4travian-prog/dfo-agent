## Player-facing summary
- Elite Gates are the staged route into the Elite Breach; Weir Keys are its entry currency. Their issue and use have separate gates.
- Redoubts are the Breach's named encounters and contribute to account-scoped Marks rewards; the weekly limit is per Redoubt, not one Breach run total.
- The Abyss Dungeon (the player-facing name for the system also called Super Dungeon in code) is a shared, temporary, single world dungeon.
- The Wrothgarian Mountains Open Zone is a shared PvP-risk region; distinguish its rules from ordinary towns and wilderness.
- The Eltheric Ocean and the Veil mark later sea exploration; the inspected source establishes routes and transitions, but I found no universal character-level gate.
- Arena seasons are eight weeks. The Royal Tourney is a distinct, seat-week event with pair limits.
- Project Legacy carries bounded benefits to descendants, including a capped estate and heirloom progression; it is an optional personal mod, not a port-wide rule.

### Arena seasons and Royal Tourney
- Arena seasons last eight weeks from the Monday 2026-09-28 00:00 UTC epoch; Arena weeks are seven days within that clock. (`src/net/arena/arenaLaw.js:82-97` (port))
- Arena rating starts at 1,000, uses Elo K=32, and validates ratings from 100 to 4,000. (`src/net/arena/arenaLaw.js:100-118` (port))
- Matchmaking begins with a 100-point rating band, widens by 100 every 10 seconds, and offers require a response within 20 seconds. (`src/net/arena/arenaLaw.js:123-134` (port))
- The same rated pair can have at most five counted bouts per UTC day and ten in one Arena season; later matches remain unrated. (`src/net/arena/arenaLaw.js:421-429` (port))
- Royal Tourney sign-up closes 10 minutes before battle and the same pair has at most three counted bouts per UTC day. It is proclaimed for a seat week, not an eight-week Arena season. (`src/net/townSeatLaw.js:1122-1123,1312-1313` (port); `src/net/arena/arenaLaw.js:82-97` (port))

### Legacy heirs
- Project Legacy is a player-owned mod online; its inheritance system is not globally forced. (`src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:450-482` (mod: Project Legacy))
- Blood grants at most +3 per inherited attribute and Hearth at most +3 per skill; the source validates these caps. (`src/systems/legacy/legacyLaw.js:20-22,46-58` (mod: Project Legacy))
- An heir receives one quarter of the deceased character's carried gold, capped at 9,900 gold. (`src/systems/legacy/legacyLaw.js:23-28` (mod: Project Legacy))
- A death can leave one tenth of carried gold, capped at 2,500, in remains; this is distinct from the estate inherited by a descendant. (`src/systems/legacy/legacyLaw.js:29-32` (mod: Project Legacy))
- Heirlooms gain up to five generations, adding 5% weapon damage or 2 armour per generation; worn eligible gear is selected and attuned as the lineage item. (`src/systems/legacy/heirloom.js:29-33,59-97,133-155` (mod: Project Legacy))
- A Legacy blessing adds 3 points to one skill and the blessing value is capped at 9. (`src/systems/legacy/heirloom.js:39-42,112-123` (mod: Project Legacy))
