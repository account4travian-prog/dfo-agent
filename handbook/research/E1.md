# E1: Professions
Commit: 0df2d87eb · Researcher: Codex · Date: 2026-10-21

## Player-facing summary
- Start the registered-account **The Court Crafter** introduction at character level 2; the professions are online-only and can be closed by the server's `PROFESSIONS_OPEN` switch. (`src/systems/questlines/qlLines.js:133-136` `QL_LINES`; `server-account/src/professions.js:109-123` `profOpenFor`, `asks`; layer: port.)
- There are five gathering tracks and five craft tracks. Herbalism and Mining supply some craft inputs; disciplines such as Alchemy and Cooking raise the shared Provisioning track. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:34-80` `PROFESSIONS`, `DISCIPLINES`, `trackOf`; layer: port.)
- Tracks run from rank 0 to 100; tier requirements are 0, 10, 25, 40, 55, 70 and 90. You can raise every gathering track, but only two crafts can rise above Journeyman (50). (`src/net/professionLaw.js:85-110,127-129`; layer: port.)
- Gather in the world: nodes are shared, deterministic for each map pixel and UTC day, and their types and counts depend on climate. Harvests give profession XP and materials; harvest skill use has no daily cap in this snapshot. (`src/net/nodeLaw.js:9-15,53-70,124-147`; `src/net/professionLaw.js:112-118,261-303`; layer: port.)
- Use the Stores page to work from stored materials; a Materials Bag holds 300 kg and lets you carry crafting materials between the field and a station. (`src/ui/professions/profPages.js:4-20,163-176`; `src/net/bagLaw.js:47-82`; layer: port.)
- Specialisations open at 50 and 100. A paid change costs 1,000 Marks and takes seven days; the current choice stays active until then. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:131-145,248-250`; layer: port.)

## Rules and numbers

### Tracks, ranks and progression
- The ten tracks are Mining, Logging, Herbalism, Hunting, Fishing, Smithing, Building, Outfitting, Provisioning and Enchanting; the eight craft disciplines are Smithing/Jewelcrafting, Carpentry/Masonry, Outfitting, Alchemy/Cooking and Enchanting, mapped to five craft tracks. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:34-80` `PROFESSIONS`, `DISCIPLINES`, `trackOf`; layer: port.)
- Each track ranges 0–100; rank names begin at Novice 0, Apprentice 25, Journeyman 50, Expert 75 and Master 100. XP for rank n is 10 × n² (6,250 / 25,000 / 56,250 / 100,000 XP for ranks 25 / 50 / 75 / 100). (`src/net/professionLaw.js:85-103` `PROF_RANK_MAX`, `RANK_NAMES`, `xpForRank`; layer: port.)
- The seven gathering/crafting tiers open at ranks 0, 10, 25, 40, 55, 70 and 90. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:104-110` `TIER_RANKS`, `tierOpen`; layer: port.)
- Each harvest grants 15 XP per tier, plus 50% for a clean act; a harvest more than two tiers below your highest opened tier pays one quarter XP. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:112-118` `HARVEST_XP_PER_TIER`, `harvestXp`; layer: port.)
- You may raise all five gathering tracks; only two craft tracks may exceed rank 50. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:127-129` `CRAFTS_ABOVE_JOURNEYMAN`, `JOURNEYMAN_RANK`; layer: port.)
- You choose one specialisation at each rank 50 and 100 milestone; changing a choice costs 1,000 Marks and takes seven days. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:131-145` `SPEC_RANKS`, `RESPEC`; layer: port.)

### Gathering nodes, climate and seasons
- A wilderness pixel's daily nodes are deterministic and shared; counts vary by climate. For example, the table gives Woodlands 12 trees, 8 herbs, 4 veins and 3 boulders, and Desert 0 trees, 6 herbs, 10 veins and 5 boulders. (`src/net/nodeLaw.js:9-15,53-70` `NODE_COUNTS`, `nodeCount`; layer: port.)
- Daily node counts are listed as trees/herbs/veins/boulders: Woodlands 12/8/4/3; Mountain Woods 10/6/6/4; Mountain 4/4/12/5; Haunted Woodlands 8/8/4/3; Swamp 6/10/2/0; Rainforest 12/10/2/0; Subtropical 8/8/4/3; Desert and Desert2 0/6/10/5. (`src/net/nodeLaw.js:58-70` `NODE_COUNTS`; layer: port.)
- Herb types are climate-specific; a patch's tier is at most 2 until its map pixel is confirmed by three agreeing reports. (`src/net/nodeLaw.js:17-22,77-93,202-220` `HERB_TABLES`, `herbPatch`; layer: port.)
- The online gathering calendar follows UTC days; seasonal herbs can yield 1.5× in their favored spring/fall season, while winter-bare plants are absent. (`src/net/nodeLaw.js:105-132` `SEASON_MULT`, `daySeason`, `herbSeasonMult`; layer: port.)
- Mining, Logging and Herbalism use land nodes; Fishing uses a net haul, Hunting harvests witnessed creature remains. The server-account profession service is the authority that rolls and credits the material and XP. (`src/net/nodeLaw.js:9-15,145-163`; `server-account/src/professions.js:4-32,74-80`; layer: port.)
- Dungeon mining has up to four veins per dungeon per UTC day; each dungeon's count is generated from one to four. (`src/net/nodeLaw.js:267,343` `DUNGEON_VEINS_MAX`, `dungeonVeinCount`; layer: port.)
- High-tier hides (tiers 5–6) are limited to three per day; there is no general daily harvest-count cap in the current profession law. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:261-303,579-588` `HIGH_HIDES_PER_DAY`, `HARVEST_*`; layer: port.)
- Current Seasons/Tides affect work only after a Season is counted: Harvest raises land-gathering yields by 25%; Blight lowers herb and wood yields by 25%; Storm Season raises fishing haul yield by 50%. (`src/net/tideLaw.js:2-12,51-53,57-75,96-106` `TIDE_EFFECTS`, `tideYield`; layer: port.)
- Weekly Tide weights are Calm 40%, Harvest 10%, Blight 5%, Plague 5%, Orc Raids 10%, Daedric Incursion 10%, Royal Wedding 5%, Bandit Summer 5%, Storm Season 5% and Tax Revolt 5%. A Season's Turning reveals this and the next week's Tide for each kingdom, the Marches and the Free Lands. (`src/net/tideLaw.js:18-53`; layer: port.)

### Crafting, quality and specialisations
- Crafting runs through the five tracks while retaining eight disciplines; for example a Ring's Jewelcrafting work raises Smithing. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:52-80,233-245` `DISCIPLINES`, `trackOf`, `SPECIALISATIONS`; layer: port.)
- Quality names are Crude, Standard, Fine, Superior and Masterwork; the odds depend on the crafter's rank relative to recipe rank and can be shifted by recipe and specialisation steps. (`src/net/recipeLaw.js:645-688` `QUALITY_NAMES`, `QUALITY_ROWS`, `qualityOdds`, `craftQuality`; layer: port.)
- Quality odds (Crude/Standard/Fine/Superior/Masterwork) by rank margin are: 0–9: 20/60/20/0/0%; 10–24: 0/50/40/10/0%; 25–44: 0/20/50/28/2%; 45+: 0/0/40/52/8%. (`src/net/recipeLaw.js:650-667` `QUALITY_ROWS`, `qualityOdds`; layer: port.)
- Each profession/discipline's specialisations are specified in the table at rank 50 and 100. The craft tracks combine their paired disciplines' choices; examples include Smithing's Weaponsmith/Armoursmith at 50 and Masterwright/Quartermaster at 100, and Building's Carpentry and Masonry choices together. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:143-240` `OWN_CHOICES`, `SPECIALISATIONS`; layer: port.)
- Building 50 choices include Bowyer (bows +1 quality step), Joiner (furniture uses half the planks), Quarryman (Rough Stone cuts 1:1 rather than 2:1), and Builder (fortification projects need 10% less stone). At 100, Siegewright strengthens rams and completes siege works a day sooner; Fortifier once per Season prevents a seat wall dropping on capture. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:192-205`; layer: port.)
- Smithing 50 choices improve weapon or armor quality by one step; at 100, Masterwright adds five percentage points to Masterwork chance and Quartermaster doubles ingot and repair-kit yield. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:180-185`; layer: port.)
- Specialisation choices with a `later` tag, including Hunting's Trophy Hunter and Outfitting's Couturier/Saddler, are not selectable until that dependent feature is available. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:168-190,243-247` `specOk`; layer: port.)

### Stores, stations and daily work
- The Stores hold up to 5,000 units of one material; a single withdrawal is capped at 200. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:272-294` `STORES_MAX`, `WITHDRAW_MAX`; layer: port.)
- A Materials Bag holds up to 300 kg; its base price is 250 gold before shop quality, region and haggling, and the bag is a single inventory item. (`src/net/bagLaw.js:47-82` `BAG_KG_LIMIT`, `BAG_BASE_PRICE`; layer: port.)
- Crafting stations draw inputs from the Stores and carried Materials Bag/pack; the interface offers Stores and profession work only when the registered account's profession book is open. (`src/net/profBook.js:29-38,136-156`; `src/ui/professions/profPages.js:4-20,173-193`; layer: port.)
- The service accepts up to 600 profession operations per account per hour, and enforces act request IDs and retries for persisted operations. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:294-303`; `server-account/src/professions.js:109-123`; layer: port.)

## Gates
- **The Court Crafter** is the profession introduction, offered at character level 2 to a registered account while online (`src/systems/questlines/qlLines.js:133-136` `QL_LINES`; `server-account/src/professions.js:109-123`; `handbook/STRUCTURE.md:20-23`; layer: port.)
- The profession system requires a registered account and an open `PROFESSIONS_OPEN` switch; a guest or closed service is refused. (`server-account/src/professions.js:109-123` `profOpenFor`, `asks`; layer: port.)
- Most specialisations unlock at rank 50 and 100; `later`-tagged choices additionally wait for their named feature. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:131-145,168-190,243-247`; layer: port.)

## Where it shows in the interface
- Open the pause menu's Stats rail and choose **Professions** or **Stores**. The Professions page groups Gathering and Crafting; Stores shows material counts and withdrawal/work actions. (`src/ui/professions/profPages.js:4-20,178-193`; layer: port.)
- While gathering, the profession HUD displays the act prompt, progress meter and material/XP/rank toasts. (`src/ui/professions/profHud.js:27-44,74-90,141-182`; layer: port.)
- Station names and cold-state explanations are supplied for the Forge, Workbench, Loom, mason's bench and jeweller's bench; Alchemy and Enchanting stations are also offered by the Stores page. (`src/ui/professions/profPages.js:197-218`; layer: port.)

## Online vs offline
- Daggerfall Unity has no profession tracks or online Stores; the profession system is a DFO port feature, deliberately guarded as online-only and by account/service availability. (`src/net/professionLaw.js:10-15`; `src/ui/professions/profPages.js:4-10`; `server-account/src/professions.js:10-24`; layer: port.)
- Professions are not an enabled vendored mod, so no mod formula supersedes the port's profession law; the account service performs the authoritative harvest/craft and material updates. (`src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:308-435,450-515`; `server-account/src/professions.js:4-32`; layer: port.)
