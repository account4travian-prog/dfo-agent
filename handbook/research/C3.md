## Player-facing summary

- You gain a character level when the skill sum for your career advances far enough; in the ordinary online leveling lane, spend 5 attribute points each level. [src/systems/advancement.js:76-79] (`calculatePlayerLevel`); [src/systems/levelEven.js:7-15] (`ONLINE_BONUS_POOL`)
- Online level-ups also apply a health floor based on your class, level, and current permanent Endurance; Endurance points can therefore lift the floor retroactively. [src/systems/levelEven.js:7-23] (`evenHpFloor`)
- Skill progress comes from actions the game tallies; skills are checked for increases after more than 360 classic minutes, using skill level, a skill-specific multiplier, class advancement multiplier, and character level. [src/systems/skills.js:169-202] (`tallySkill`); [src/systems/advancement.js:64-70] (`raiseSkills`, `skillUsesForAdvancement`)
- Strength affects carry capacity, fatigue, and physical damage; Intelligence determines spell points; Endurance affects health gained per level and fatigue. [src/combat/formulas.js:101-118] (`maxEncumbrance`, `entityMaxEncumbrance`); [src/systems/statMods.js:178-184] (`maxFatigue`); [src/systems/chargen.js:193-202] (`hitPointsPerLevelUp`); [src/combat/formulas.js:126-132] (`spellPointsFor`)
- Agility and Luck affect hit calculations; Willpower adds magic resistance; Personality and Mercantile affect shop prices. [src/combat/formulas.js:126-129,302-305] (`magicResist`, `toHitModifier`, `statsToHit`); [src/systems/shopStock.js:836-905] (`calculateTradePrice`)
- The source lists 35 skills, but the inspected code does not state their governing attributes. [src/systems/skills.js:18-29] (`SKILLS`)

## Rules and numbers

### Attribute formulas

- All eight live attributes read as permanent value plus active modifiers, clamped between 0 and 100, with effect maximum modifiers able to raise the upper bound. [src/systems/statMods.js:18-103] (`liveStat`)
- Strength gives base carry capacity `floor(Strength × 1.5)`; the live entity may gain an additional truncated percentage from Increased Weight Allowance. [src/combat/formulas.js:101-118] (`maxEncumbrance`, `entityMaxEncumbrance`)
- Strength increases physical damage using `damageModifier(Strength)`; the source applies it to weapon and player hand-to-hand damage. [src/combat/combatStats.js:216-223] (`damageModel`, attribute effect); [src/combat/formulas.js:429-430] (`calculateAttackDamage`)
- Fatigue maximum is `(live Strength + live Endurance) × 64`; fatigue is stored in these multiplied units. [src/systems/statMods.js:178-184] (`FATIGUE_MULTIPLIER`, `maxFatigue`)
- Intelligence gives spell points `floor(Intelligence × class spell-point multiplier)`; the career ability bitfield selects ×3.00, ×2.00, ×1.75, ×1.50, ×1.00, or ×0.50 (unknown bit patterns default to ×1.00). [src/combat/formulas.js:126] (`spellPointsFor`); [src/systems/chargen.js:180-186,244] (`SPELL_POINT_MULTIPLIERS`, `spellPointMultiplier`, `applyCharacter`)
- Willpower gives magic resistance `floor(Willpower / 10)`, which is added to a spell target’s saving chance. [src/combat/formulas.js:127] (`magicResist`); [src/systems/spellcast.js:151-153] (`savingChance`)
- Agility contributes `floor(Agility / 10) - 5` to the to-hit modifier; in the core weapon hit calculation it also contributes the attacker-minus-target agility difference divided by 10. [src/combat/formulas.js:128] (`toHitModifier`); [src/combat/formulas.js:302-305] (`statsToHit`)
- Luck contributes the attacker-minus-target Luck difference divided by 10 to hit chance; the enhanced combat core also uses Luck in its critical-hit chance calculation. [src/combat/formulas.js:302-305] (`statsToHit`); [src/combat/combatStats.js:98-102] (`criticalModel`)
- Endurance modifier is `floor(Endurance / 10) - 5`; it adds to each level’s rolled health gain, with a minimum gain of 1. [src/systems/chargen.js:193-202] (`hitPointsModifier`, `hitPointsPerLevelUp`)
- Level-one maximum health is `25 + class hitPointsPerLevel`. [src/systems/chargen.js:193-196] (`rollMaxHealthLevel1`)
- Health regeneration per rest step is `max(floor(Endurance/10)-5 + Medical × maxHealth / 1000, 1)`. [src/systems/rest/rest.js:59] (`healthPerRestStep`)
- Speed is read by movement and weapon animation timing; the exact movement functions are in `src/player/motor.js` and the weapon rig reads live Speed. [src/player/motor.js:704,816-824] (`walkSpeed`, `swimSpeedScale`); [src/combat/weapon/weaponRig.js:451] (`liveSpeed`)
- Personality enters talk reaction as `trunc(Personality / 5)` and is one input to shop trade pricing alongside Mercantile. [src/systems/talk/talkTopics.js:496] (`reaction`); [src/systems/shopStock.js:836-905] (`calculateTradePrice`)
- Shop pricing uses both live Mercantile and Personality; online each is clamped to the interval 0–100 before calculation. [src/systems/shopStock.js:836-905] (`calculateTradePrice`, `ONLINE_HAGGLE_MAX`)
- In the classic combat core, Luck and Agility each add `trunc((attacker stat - target stat) / 10)` to the weapon hit modifier; source also shows the online enhanced core’s alternative attribute effects in the combat-stat display. [src/combat/formulas.js:302-305] (`statsToHit`); [src/combat/combatStats.js:221-228] (`effect`)

### Online level-up and skill advancement

- Ordinary skill raises are evaluated only when classic game time has advanced by strictly more than 360 minutes since the prior check. [src/systems/advancement.js:64,135-138] (`SKILL_RAISE_CHECK_INTERVAL`, `raiseSkills`)
- Skill uses needed for a raise are `floor(skill value × skill multiplier × career advancement multiplier × 1.04^level × 2/5) + 1`. [src/systems/advancement.js:68-70] (`skillUsesForAdvancement`)
- The skill-specific multipliers in `SKILL_ADVANCEMENT_MULTIPLIER` are indexed in the skill order above: Medical 12; Etiquette 1; Streetwise 1; Jumping 5; each of the nine languages 15; Lockpicking 2; Mercantile 1; Pickpocket 2; Stealth 2; Swimming 1; Climbing 2; Backstabbing 1; Dodging 4; Running 50; Destruction 1; Restoration 2; Illusion 1; Alteration 1; Thaumaturgy 2; Mysticism 1; Short Blade 2; Long Blade 2; Hand-to-Hand 2; Axe 2; Blunt Weapon 2; Archery 1; Critical Strike 8. [src/systems/advancement.js:40-62] (`SKILL_ADVANCEMENT_MULTIPLIER`)
- The accumulated eligible skill sum is `sum(primary) + sum(major) - lowest major + highest minor`; every 15 points of progress moves the calculated level, with the formula `floor((currentSum - startingSum + 28) / 15)`. [src/systems/skills.js:262-275] (`levelUpSkillSum`); [src/systems/advancement.js:76-79] (`calculatePlayerLevel`)
- The level-up door marks a level as owed when calculated level exceeds current level; each visit applies one level increment. [src/systems/advancement.js:312-319,375] (`checkForLevelUp`, `applyLevelUp`)
- Online gives a fixed bonus pool of 5 points per level; offline/default Daggerfall leveling rolls 4–6. [src/systems/levelEven.js:7-23] (`ONLINE_BONUS_POOL`); [src/systems/advancement.js:64-66,350-354] (`bonusPoolFor`)
- Online max health is at least the class/level average-roll value at current permanent Endurance: `floor(25 + h + (level−1) × max(1, (trunc(h/2)+h)/2 + floor(Endurance/10)−5))`, where `h` is class hit points per level. [src/systems/levelEven.js:7-23] (`evenHpFloor`)
- When a level is applied, it adds a rolled class health amount plus the Endurance modifier, floored to at least 1; online’s floor can then raise the effective maximum to its value after allocated attribute points. [src/systems/chargen.js:197-202] (`hitPointsPerLevelUp`); [src/systems/advancement.js:375-386] (`applyLevelUp`)
- The six skill schools and masteries can advance above 100 only under Master Skills online; a normal skill’s raise loop caps at 100. [src/systems/advancement.js:158-186] (`raiseSkills`)
- `tallySkill` caps ordinary pending uses at 20,000; movement is tallied from movement events, while above 100 Master Skills only accepts weighted real use. [src/systems/skills.js:169-202] (`tallySkill`, `tallyMovementSkill`)

## Gates

- Character creation assigns attributes and the 35 skills; the handbook places attribute and skill basics in the character creation stage. [src/systems/chargen.js:58-63,87-95] (`rollStats`, `rollSkills`)
- The first level-up gate is skill-sum progress, not a fixed character level or Renown threshold. [src/systems/advancement.js:312-319] (`checkForLevelUp`)

## Where it shows in the interface

- The enhanced Growth panel has a “Level” page; it displays attributes, skills, and progress toward the next level and offers the level-up allocation screen when owed. [src/ui/growth/growthTab.js:140-155] (`levelPage`)
- The character sheet exposes the character’s level, health, magicka, fatigue, and attribute/skill pages. [src/ui/enhanced/enhancedCharSheet.js:123-150] (`sheetModel`)

## Online vs offline

- Online ordinary level-ups use a fixed 5-point attribute pool and the online health floor; classic/offline leveling uses a random 4–6-point pool and its stored per-level health rolls. [src/systems/levelEven.js:7-23] (`ONLINE_BONUS_POOL`, `evenHpFloor`); [src/systems/advancement.js:64-66,350-354] (`bonusPoolFor`)
- Online shop haggle caps Mercantile and Personality at 100; offline keeps Daggerfall’s unbounded skill read. [src/systems/shopStock.js:836-905] (`calculateTradePrice`, `ONLINE_HAGGLE_MAX`)
- The inspected `levelEven.js` describes the health floor and fixed pool as online-only; offline does not receive the floor. [src/systems/levelEven.js:7-23] (`evenHpFloor`)
