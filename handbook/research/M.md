## Read this first

- `onlineLane.js` is the authority for online mod-key scope: most mod toggles remain the player's; room keys are forced by vendor and key; whole mods read their shipped defaults except listed player-owned keys. `ONLINE_ROOM_MOD_KEYS`, `ONLINE_WHOLE_MODS`, `ONLINE_PLAYERS_OWN_MODS`, `onlineForcedModSetting`, and `onlineWholeModKey` implement those three policies. (`src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:308-435,450-525`)
- “Forced for the world” below means the online lane supplies a value regardless of the player's stored value. “Whole at shipped defaults” means its dials are not the player's online. A mod not in either category is “player's own.” These policies apply to a switch read; owner-authoritative results such as a host's enemy or loot still appear to other players. (`src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:209-242,268-302,484-550`)
- The registry is presentation and declaration, not a second implementation. Mod rows are generated from `MOD_SETTINGS`; port preference rows declare `initial` and `online`, and `declareOnlinePrefs` derives the lane table from them. (`src/systems/features.js:6-13,111-129,1756-1765`; `src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:131-138`)
- This is a source-code map, not a claim that every player personally experiences every optional feature. Mod and preference values are read at the system and scope named below. (`src/systems/online/onlineLane.js:518-550`)
