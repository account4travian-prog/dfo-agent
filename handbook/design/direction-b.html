Choosing a race

The eight races, what each starts with, and how much the choice matters later.

Gate: noneVerified 0df2d87eb

At a glance

  • Each of the eight races changes your starting attributes and sets its own specials and resistances.
  • Compare races against the playstyle you want; there is no single best build.
  • Online, racial combat bonuses come from the online combat rules, not the classic formulas.
  • Some race traits matter more later; those links are marked later.

How race works online

Your race is one of the first choices on the creation screens. It shifts your starting attributes and gives you specials and resistances that stay with your character.

Online rule

Online, racial combat bonuses are decided by PCAAO, not the classic parts of the combat formulas. The values on this page are read from the online path.

Choosing for your playstyle

A fighter values different starting attributes than a caster, and a thief values different specials again. Read the table against the playstyle you want, not against a single number.
