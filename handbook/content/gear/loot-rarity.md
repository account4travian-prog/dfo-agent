> [!tip] At a glance
> - Online Loot Rarity is active from your first eligible drop; **The Smith's Secrets** introduces the gear systems at level 4.
> - Magic, Rare and Legendary are the tiers the rarity ladder rolls.
> - Aetheric gear comes from set sources, Artifacts are Daggerfall's own, and Gilded gear is a fixed unique spoil.
> - Read a piece's card: its tier, affix lines, sockets and identification state all matter.

## The rarity ladder

The ladder contains seven tiers. The middle three are generated by the rarity system; the other tiers have distinct
sources and should not be read as another random drop rank.

| Tier | What the tier means |
|---|---|
| Common | Plain gear without the rarity ladder's added affixes. |
| Magic | One or two added affixes, or Daggerfall's own magic item. |
| Rare | Three or four affixes, a generated name and a catalogue enchantment. |
| Legendary | A fixed named record with its signature affixes and enchantment. |
| Aetheric | Set gear from its designated boss or the Sigil Broker; not rolled by the rarity ladder. |
| Artifact | Daggerfall's own artifacts. |
| Gilded | A fixed, unique spoil; not a random rarity roll. |

Affixes add readable effects such as damage, armour, attributes, resistance, skills or carrying capacity. The item card
shows these lines when identified. Some magic effects work before identification, but the added lines remain hidden
until you identify the item.

## Material and rarity are different

Online, Loot Rarity and Unleveled Loot both affect what you find. Loot Rarity determines the item's tier and added
lines. Unleveled Loot determines the material of random weapons and armour from factors such as Luck, shop quality and
dungeon kind; it does not use your character level to select the material. The online lane holds this mod to its
shipped defaults. A material name therefore does not tell you an item's rarity, and a rarity label does not determine
its material.

## Finding and reading a piece

![Generic item-card fields to inspect before comparing gear](theme/art/diagram-item-card.svg "Caption: Read material, rarity, condition and improvement fields as separate parts of a piece.")

Check the tier label and item card before you compare gear. Rare and Legendary pieces can arrive unidentified; use an
Identify service to reveal their rolled lines. Online, the Reforge is available through the Mages Guild's Identify
service. It handles socket work and later improvements too; see [Sockets and reforging](sockets-and-reforging.md).

The **Smith's Secrets** introduction opens at character level 4. Its steps require Loot Rarity to be on; online the
port forces that feature on. It introduces the system rather than unlocking the first rarity drop. See the
[questline ladder](../reference/questline-ladder.md) for its giver and other gates.

> [!online] Sources of the tier
> Ordinary drops, set pieces and artifacts do not all use the same roll. Aetheric, Artifact and Gilded entries on the
> ladder identify their special sources; they are not promises that each is available from every loot container.

## Common mistakes

- **Treating material as an item tier.** Material and rarity are separate rolls.
- **Expecting all seven tiers to be random drops.** Only Magic, Rare and Legendary are rolled by the ladder.
- **Selling an unidentified piece by its name alone.** Identify it to read its added lines before deciding what it is
  worth to you.
