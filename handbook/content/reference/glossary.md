## A–C

### Abyss Dungeon
The player-facing name for the shared, timed dungeon whose internal system name is Super Dungeon.

### Abyss phase
One of the shared dungeon's states as it rises, is found, falls or disappears.

### Account
The service identity that owns account-wide balances, event receipts and limits.

### Arena
The online system for rated bouts, exhibitions and private sessions.

### Arena rating
The numerical score the Arena updates from rated outcomes.

### Arena season
An eight-week rating and reward period measured from a UTC epoch.

### Arena week
A seven-day segment used by Arena-related event reward rules.

### Attribute
One of a character's core numerical traits used by advancement and combat systems.

### Bank of the Empire
The online service that exchanges Marks for gold.

### Bounty
A hunt taken from a town's Bounty Board and held by a character for one game day.

### Carried remains
Eligible bag or cart contents left behind by a player's Open Zone death.

### Census
The account count used when the Abyss Dungeon director selects an eligible region.

### Champion
A named enemy promotion with a visible champion trait.

### Character level
The skill-driven character progression level, distinct from Renown.

### Chat channel
A recipient group chosen from chat tabs or a channel command.

### Clock hour
The fixed hour bucket, reset on the hour, used for the Renown hourly limit and the services' request limits.

### Combat Marks
Marks earned from Dagon's Breach, town raids, the sea serpent, Redoubts and Starfall, under one shared account ceiling per UTC day.

### Court writ
A profession delivery that grants Marks and profession progression under an account limit.

### Craft
A profession track that can advance through crafting actions.

## D–F

### Daggerfall
The 1996 role-playing game *The Elder Scrolls II: Daggerfall*, of which DFO Online is an independent reimplementation. DFO needs your own copy of Daggerfall's original game data (ARENA2) to run; see [What DFO Online is](../begin/what-is-dfo-online.md).

### Daggerfall Unity
The open-source Daggerfall reimplementation (MIT, by Interkarma and contributors) that DFO Online began as a port of. DFO keeps it as the reference for its data and logic, not a target to match, so offline Daggerfall Unity rules can differ from online play; see [Which rules apply online](online-ruleset.md).

### Dagon's Breach
A scheduled gate event with account-scoped reward claims.

### Deep Delver
A Mining specialisation: dungeon veins yield double, and every vein in the dungeon is marked on the compass.

### Default binding
The key or button assigned to an action when no saved rebind overrides it.

### DFO Online
The browser-played, online reimplementation of Daggerfall at daggerfalljs.dev, with shared-world systems and rules on top of the Daggerfall Unity-derived port. See [What DFO Online is](../begin/what-is-dfo-online.md).

### Dice roll
A chat command that rolls a parsed dice expression.

### Elite
A promoted foe enabled for a character's own world after the elite gate is met.

### Elite Breach
The shared Elite Gates event whose halls are called Redoubts.

### Elite Dungeon
A dungeon instance with elite foes and its own respawn timing.

### Elite foe
An enemy with the elite promotion and its associated enhanced stats or rewards.

### Elite Gate
A gate in the Weir Road and Elite Breach progression.

### Emote
A chat-triggered gesture such as waving or bowing.

### Enhanced AI
The port's enemy-behaviour enhancement, forced on for everyone online. It supplies the telegraphed attacks that land when their wind-up completes; see [Fighting](../hold/fighting.md).

### Event receipt
An account-service record used to prevent duplicate claims for a reward event.

### Fighters Guild
One of DFO's four NPC guilds, offering combat training and guild services at its halls; you apply there like any NPC guild. See [The NPC guilds](../town/npc-guilds.md).

### Firmament
The online star tree with two point pools: Stars from Renown, which buy any star, and Craft points from profession ranks, which buy life-ring stars only.

## G–I

### Game day
A day of the shared event clock, two real hours long, used by scheduled events such as Dagon's Breach and town raids. It is not the sky's day you see in the world.

### Gold
The character's own carried and saved currency, separate from Marks. It has weight, is exposed to the death penalty, and the Bank of the Empire exchanges Marks for gold one way; see [Money and markets](../feet/money-and-markets.md).

### Gothway Garden
The first town out of Privateer's Hold and the setting of the handbook's first-town pages; see [Leaving the Hold](../hold/leaving-the-hold.md).

### Guild
A player organization with its own treasury and city-seat activity.

### Guild channel
Chat addressed to members of the player's guild.

### Guild Marks
Marks held in a player guild's shared treasury.

### Heir
A later member of a Project Legacy family line who can receive bounded inheritance.

### Heirloom
An eligible weapon or armour piece carried across Project Legacy generations.

### Hour That Was Not
The questline, opening at character level 12, that introduces Abyss Dungeon content.

### Iliac Bay
The region of Tamriel covered by Daggerfall's world map, and the name the handbook uses for DFO's shared game world. Its regions, settlements, wilderness and dungeons are the places you travel between; see [Danger and regions](../travel/danger-and-regions.md).

## J–L

### Knightly order
A chivalric NPC organization (one of the realm's ten knightly orders). All orders share one membership slot and offer training and services; see [The NPC guilds](../town/npc-guilds.md).

### Language channel
An optional chat channel selected with the language command.

### LFG channel
An opt-in channel for looking for a group.

### Local chat
Nearby in-character speech between players in range.

## M–O

### Mages Guild
One of DFO's four NPC guilds, offering magic services, training and rank-gated teleport at its halls. See [The NPC guilds](../town/npc-guilds.md) and [Mages Guild services](../town/mages-guild-services.md).

### Market listing
A timed item offer occupying one of an account's active listing slots.

### Market order
A timed buy request occupying one of an account's active order slots.

### Marks
The online currency used by account and guild services.

### Mentor mode
The automatic scaling that plays a higher-level party member at about the party's level when the party is well below theirs.

### Motherlode
A scheduled shared resource event with a limited number of world appearances.

### NPC guild
A town-based organization — a Fighters Guild, Mages Guild, temple or knightly order — that you apply to at its hall for rank, training and services. It is not a player guild; see Guild and [The NPC guilds](../town/npc-guilds.md).

### Open Zone
The Wrothgarian Mountains region where PvP deaths can leave shared remains.

## P–R

### Pair limit
A counted-bout limit applied to a particular opponent pair rather than all of one player's matches.

### Party
A player group with shared activity rules and leader travel tools.

### Physical Combat and Armor Overhaul (PCAAO)
The balance mod that DFO forces on and locks at its shipped defaults online. Online hit chance, damage, armour, shield blocks and racial weapon bonuses come from it; see [Which rules apply online](online-ruleset.md).

### Privateer's Hold
The starting dungeon where a new character begins. Online its public room can be entered once per character, and leaving it leads toward Gothway Garden; see [Your first minutes](../hold/first-minutes.md).

### Profession request window
The clock hour within which an account's requests to the profession service are counted.

### Project Legacy
The player-owned mod that tracks family lines, heirs and heirlooms.

### Redoubt
A named encounter inside the Elite Breach.

### Redoubt receipt
An account record that marks a weekly reward claim for one Redoubt.

### Region channel
Chat addressed to players in the current region.

### Renown
Online progression earned from play and tracked separately from character level.

### Renown track
One character's progress along the Renown ladder.

### Roleplay & Realism
The balance mod that DFO forces on at its shipped defaults online. It supplies the online encumbrance rules (weight slows you past 75% of your limit), the loan limits and the repair rules; see [Which rules apply online](online-ruleset.md).

### Royal Tourney
A duel ladder held through a proclaimed seat week in a crown city, with its own counted-bout limit.

## S–U

### Shared action key
A key assigned to more than one action, with behavior determined by the active context.

### Soul Cake
A collectible found during the Witches' Festival under account limits.

### Starfall
A Witches' Festival event in which fallen stars must be held against waves of foes; a star held pays Renown and Marks, under the combat Marks ceiling.

### Super Dungeon
The internal code name for the player-facing Abyss Dungeon.

### Temple
A religious NPC organization (one of the eight divines' temples). All temples share one membership slot and offer healing, training and penance services; see [The NPC guilds](../town/npc-guilds.md).

### Tide
A condition rolled each seat week for each kingdom, the Marches and the Free Lands; depending on the Tide it changes gathering or fishing yields, influence, festival costs, couriers or sea travel.

### Trade channel
An opt-in chat channel for trade conversations.

### Unleveled Loot
The balance mod that DFO forces on online; an item's material comes from luck, place and source rather than your character level. See [Loot rarity](../gear/loot-rarity.md).

### Unstuck
A command flow that moves a stranded character to a permitted recovery point.

### UTC day
The midnight-to-midnight UTC period used by many account daily limits.

## V–Z

### Weir Key
A stackable inventory item used by the Elite Gates progression.

### The Weir Road
The questline that leads into Elite Gate progression.

### Wild remains
The shared drop record left after a death in the Open Zone.

### Witches' Festival
A seasonal event with Soul Cakes and Starfall activity.

### World channel
Chat addressed across the online world.

## Action names

These entries are input actions, not game systems. [Keys and commands](keys-and-commands.md) lists their default bindings and how to rebind them.

### ActChoice
The action that chooses what Interact starts at a gathering spot, such as gathering herbs or foraging food at an herb patch.

### BoatSailUp
The Come Sail Away action that sets more sail at a ship's helm.

### FreeMouse
The action that releases or recaptures the pointer for interface use.

### LegacyFamily
The Project Legacy action that opens the family view.

### ModeWheel
The held action that opens the interaction-mode wheel: Steal, Grab, Info or Talk.

### Quick Loot All
The action that takes the loot currently offered by a container or source.

### Quick Loot Open
The action that opens the highlighted loot container.

### QuickDial
The action that opens the four-way dial to your skills, items, map and magic.

### SocialInteract
The action that opens the social menu on another player, or the friends and party panel when nobody is in reach.

### WeaponTechnique
The dedicated input action for using a weapon's technique line.
