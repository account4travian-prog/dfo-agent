## First session

### Is DFO Online free?
The client is free and open source, and Daggerfall itself has been free since 2009; you supply the game data. See [What DFO Online is](../begin/what-is-dfo-online.md) and [Playing in the browser](../begin/playing-in-the-browser.md).

### Do I need to own Daggerfall to play?
Yes — the game reads the original Daggerfall data files, which are not bundled, and the official site points to free sources. See [Playing in the browser](../begin/playing-in-the-browser.md).

### Which files does the game ask for?
It asks for the **ARENA2** folder of your own Daggerfall install (under `DF/DAGGER/ARENA2` in a Steam copy), or a zip of it on a phone, and it remembers the pick. See [Your first launch](../begin/your-first-launch.md).

### Does it run on Mac, Linux or a phone?
The browser client runs in a current desktop browser or on a phone, and the optional desktop app ships for Windows, macOS (Apple silicon) and Linux. See [Playing in the browser](../begin/playing-in-the-browser.md).

### Do I need a registered account?
You can play as a guest, but professions, the market, player guilds, Marks, festival rewards and several questlines need a registered account, so register before you invest. See [Your account](../begin/your-account.md).

### What happens when I die, and do I lose my items?
You respawn at a safe point with half health and lose one tenth of the gold you carry, while an ordinary death keeps your items; the Open Zone and a revenant's theft are separate exceptions. See [Dying online](../hold/dying-online.md).

### How does saving work, and is my progress safe?
Offline saves are browser-storage slots or desktop files, an online character is held by the account service and saves as you go, and loading during online play is disabled. See [Saving and returning](../begin/saving-and-returning.md).

### How do I make my first money?
Early gold comes from introduction quests (100–400 gold a step), bounties and the loot you sell at any counter, and banking the leftovers protects it. See [Making money](../feet/making-money.md).

### Where should I stand for questline letters?
A letter reaches you only outdoors in a town by day, one new line starts at most once per character hour, and a step that needs a building waits for a town that has it. See [Courier letters](../town/courier-letters.md).

### Which guild should I join, and what does it cost?
An NPC guild hall takes an application when your standing and guild skills qualify and the source names no joining fee, while a player guild costs {{fact guild.foundingGold}} gold and Renown {{fact guild.foundingRenown}} to found. See [The NPC guilds](../town/npc-guilds.md) and [Player guilds](../rivals/player-guilds.md).

### Can I respec later?
Firmament refunds are free below Renown {{fact firmament.refundFreeBelowRenown}} and cost {{fact firmament.refundCostPerStar}} Marks a star after that, a whole-sky respec is {{fact firmament.respecGold}} gold, a specialisation change is 1,000 Marks, a skill swap is 100,000 gold, and a mastery can never be undone. See [The Firmament](../feet/the-firmament.md), [Craft specialisations](../gear/craft-specialisations.md) and [Masteries and specialisations](../late/masteries-and-specialisations.md).

### Can other players attack me?
Not in ordinary play; player damage happens in duels you accept, Arena bouts, guild sieges and the Wrothgarian Open Zone. See [Meeting other players](../town/meeting-players.md) and [The Open Zone](../late/open-zone.md).

### How do I report a bug or a player?
`/bug` opens the private issue form, `/report <name>` reports conduct, and the Discord or issue tracker carry anything the in-game forms miss. See [Keys and commands](keys-and-commands.md).

### How much time does the game ask, and what is the endgame?
The source sets no completion time; Renown runs to {{fact renown.max}}, questlines open through level 12, and the late game is Elite Gates, Abyss Dungeons, Arena seasons, the Open Zone, the Eltheric Ocean and Legacy. See [The questline ladder](questline-ladder.md) and [Catching up](../bay/catching-up.md).

### What does the Draft badge mean?
**Draft** means the page has been checked against the game's source but not yet read end to end by the maintainer for sense or contradictions. See [How this guide is checked](how-this-guide-is-checked.md).

## Modes, mods and getting unstuck

### What is the difference between offline and online?
Offline is DFO's single-player game on this device; online adds an account, the shared ruleset, other players, Marks, Renown, the market, guilds, events and the account-held character. See [Offline and online](../begin/offline-and-online.md).

### Do I need to decide a mode before creating a character?
Yes. The front door asks Online or Offline first, and a character belongs to one of those worlds; an offline DFO character can be brought online once, and an online character copied to offline. See [Offline and online](../begin/offline-and-online.md).

### Can I use Daggerfall Unity or Nexus mods?
No. DFO has no mod support and does not load DFU mods, Nexus mods or load orders; the **Features** rows are the game's own systems and settings, and online their scope is fixed for everyone. See [Mods, saves and wipes](mods-saves-and-wipes.md).

### Can I import my Daggerfall or Daggerfall Unity save?
Not yet. DFO characters are created in DFO; the only transfers are its own offline-to-online and online-to-offline doors, not saves from another game. See [Mods, saves and wipes](mods-saves-and-wipes.md).

### Will my character be wiped at launch or between seasons?
The source and the official site state no wipe schedule. What resets is seasonal **standing** in the Arena and city control, not the character; character deletion is the permanent, player-triggered act. See [Mods, saves and wipes](mods-saves-and-wipes.md).

### How do I play with a friend?
Add the player as a friend, form a party, meet or use party travel, and remember that kills, quest sharing, shared containers and mentor mode have separate rules. See [Play with a friend](../town/play-with-a-friend.md).

### Why can't I hit anything?
Check weapon skill, fatigue, weapon condition, target armour, range and the enemy's tells before blaming the character; online combat uses PCAAO's hit and damage rules. See [Combat maths](../hold/combat-math.md).

### Where do I store gold, loot and materials safely?
Gold belongs in the bank; ordinary death only takes part of carried gold. Homes, wagons, ship holds, profession Stores and guild vaults hold different goods and have different risks; the Open Zone is the exception. See [Safe storage](../gear/safe-storage.md).

### Why do guards and guilds treat me differently?
Three separate tracks are at work: People reputation, legal standing in each region, and faction standing with a guild or temple. One does not repair another. See [Standing and reputation](../town/standing-and-reputation.md).

### I am stuck in a quest, or an NPC is missing. What do I do?
Use the recovery ladder before assuming a bug: re-read the journal, check a held step, try `/unstuck`, look for the NPC in the right place and time, then restart or report. See [Stuck and recovery](../hold/stuck-and-recovery.md).

### I know Daggerfall or Daggerfall Unity. What changed?
Fifteen online deltas matter first: walked travel, PCAAO combat, telegraphed attacks, the forced ruleset, no mid-session load, death as respawn, Marks, Renown, immediate guild promotions and online quest behaviour. See [Coming from Daggerfall or Daggerfall Unity](coming-from-daggerfall.md).

## Character creation

### Does race change my attributes?
No; attributes come from your class plus a roll and then your bonus points, while race sets your look, some defences and online weapon bonuses. See [Choosing a race](../creation/races.md).

### Which class should I choose?
Choose by the skills and role you want to play; compare the options in [Classes](../creation/classes.md).

### Can I make my own class?
Yes; the custom builder lets you set skill tiers, health, attributes, advantages and disadvantages. Read [Custom classes](../creation/custom-classes.md).

### What should a warrior, mage, thief or hybrid prioritize?
Each style asks for a different balance of combat, magic and utility skills; see [Choosing a playstyle](../creation/playstyles.md).

### What should I double-check before entering the world?
Review the creation summary, your class skill tiers and any custom-class restriction, because the final summary does not show your biography answers and the source provides no general undo. See [Choices that are hard to undo](../creation/hard-to-undo.md).

### What does Layered Gearing do?
It presents worn armour on your character model; see [Appearance and Layered Gearing](../creation/appearance.md).

## Playing online

### Why does online play force some settings?
Shared-world systems override local switches so everyone in a room meets the same world and balance; see [Online rules at a glance](online-ruleset.md).

### What key swings my weapon?
The default is the right mouse button, but the Controls screen shows your current binding. See [Keys and commands](keys-and-commands.md).

### What key uses a weapon technique?
The default technique action is the mouse's back side button, and you can rebind it in Controls. See [Keys and commands](keys-and-commands.md).

### Why does Enter open chat?
Online, Enter opens chat; press Y to free the mouse cursor instead. See [Keys and commands](keys-and-commands.md).

### Where do I change key bindings?
Open **Settings › Controls › Key bindings**; see [Keys and commands](keys-and-commands.md).

### How do I find the available chat commands?
Type `/help`; other in-game lists include `/lang`, `/emotes` and `/breach help`. See [Keys and commands](keys-and-commands.md).

### Why did my command not appear in chat?
Unknown slash commands are refused rather than sent as ordinary text, and a line beginning `//` sends text that begins with a slash. See [Keys and commands](keys-and-commands.md).

## Progress and events

### What is the difference between character level and Renown?
They are separate tracks: character level follows your skills, while Renown is the online ladder to {{fact renown.max}} that adds health and spell points and opens systems. See [Renown](../feet/renown.md).

### When do I unlock the Firmament?
The **Written in the Stars** introduction opens at character level 3, and the tree itself is in the pause menu's **Growth** tab. See [The Firmament](../feet/the-firmament.md).

### Why did I stop earning Marks or Renown?
Account ceilings, service windows and event receipts use different scopes; check [Caps, timers and resets](caps-and-timers.md).

### When does a daily cap reset?
Many account caps use UTC days, while service rate limits reset each clock hour; see [Caps, timers and resets](caps-and-timers.md).

### How often can I fight the same Arena opponent for rating?
Rated pairs are limited to five counted bouts per UTC day and ten per season. See [Arena seasons and the Royal Tourney](../late/arena-seasons.md).

### Is the Royal Tourney part of the Arena season?
No; it runs through a proclaimed seat week and has its own pair limit. See [Arena seasons and the Royal Tourney](../late/arena-seasons.md).

### What unlocks ordinary elite enemies?
Elite spawns begin at level 3 and Renown 5 in your own world. See [Elite Gates and the Elite Breach](../late/elite-gates.md).

### Does level 12 unlock the Abyss Dungeon itself?
No; level 12 opens **The Hour That Was Not**, and the shared dungeon has its own spawn cycle. See [Abyss Dungeons](../late/abyss-dungeons.md).

### How do I start the Elite Breach commands?
Use `/breach`, and `/breach help` lists the options. See [Elite Gates and the Elite Breach](../late/elite-gates.md).

### What can happen to my items in the Open Zone?
Eligible carried items and cart contents can be left in shared remains after PvP death. See [The Open Zone](../late/open-zone.md).

### Is Project Legacy forced on every online character?
No; it is player-owned. See [Legacy](../late/legacy.md).

### How much gold can an heir inherit?
The estate is one quarter of carried gold, capped at 9,900. See [Legacy](../late/legacy.md).

### Is there a level requirement for the Eltheric Ocean?
The source sets no universal character-level gate for it, but past Veilhaven's peace the sea is an **online open-PvP zone** with four-times-stronger foes and death's drop. See [The Eltheric Ocean](../late/eltheric-ocean.md).

### Where can I see recent changes?
This sync's player-facing changes are on [What changed](what-changed.md).

### What does an unfamiliar handbook term mean?
Look it up in the [Glossary](glossary.md).
